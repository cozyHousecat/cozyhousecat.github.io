Someone Foolish Enough

2022-12-20cozyHousecat

Argentina’s World Cup winners got home today to millions of people on the streets of Buenos Aires, so many that the open-top bus couldn’t get through, and the players had to be taken out by helicopter. Seeing the whole country come out for Messi and his teammates was one of the happiest things I’ve seen all year. Meanwhile, Musk has finally responded to his own poll, saying he’ll resign as Twitter’s boss “as soon as I find someone foolish enough to take the job” and that he’ll keep running the software and servers. That’s a very Musk way of keeping his word while hanging on to most of the control. Still, today had a lot of joy in it, and with Christmas getting closer I’m happy to focus on that.
