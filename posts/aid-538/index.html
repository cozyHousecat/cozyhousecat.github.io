Monday Night

2022-11-07cozyHousecat

The Guardian is reporting tonight that Gavin Williamson told a senior civil servant to “slit your throat” when he was Defence Secretary, which is far worse than angry texts to a Chief Whip, and he strongly denies bullying. Sunak brought him back into the Cabinet less than two weeks ago while promising “integrity, professionalism and accountability,” and I can’t see how both of those survive the week. Across the Atlantic, the US midterms are tomorrow, and Twitter’s new owner has just told his millions of followers that independent-minded voters should back Republicans, a few days after saying the platform would be neutral. Between that, the $8 ticks and the impersonation rules, it feels like the place where a lot of this election will be argued about has been rewired in a fortnight. I’m going to bed and seeing what America looks like in the morning.
