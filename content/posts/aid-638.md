France beat Morocco 2–0 tonight to reach their second World Cup final in a row, with Theo Hernández scoring in the fifth minute and Randal Kolo Muani adding a second near the end. Morocco never stopped pushing, and even in defeat they made history as the first African and first Arab team to reach a World Cup semi-final. Their run has meant so much to so many people, in Morocco, across Africa and the Arab world, and in Moroccan communities everywhere, and their fans have been the soul of this tournament. They beat Belgium, Spain and Portugal and only lost to the reigning champions, which is an amazing achievement. Morocco have my respect, and I hope they’re welcomed home as heroes.
