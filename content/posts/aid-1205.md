Tonight Boris Johnson announced he’s quitting as an MP with immediate effect, after seeing a draft of the committee report on whether he misled Parliament over Partygate. He called the committee a “kangaroo court” and accused it of trying to force him out, while critics say he’s resigning to avoid facing the consequences of the report, which could have triggered a recall vote in his Uxbridge seat. Earlier today his resignation honours list was published, giving peerages and honours to several of his allies, so it’s been quite a day for him. Across the Atlantic, Donald Trump’s federal indictment was unsealed, with 37 charges over his handling of classified documents after he left office, which he says is a politically motivated witch hunt. Two former leaders, on two sides of the Atlantic, both insisting they’re being unfairly targeted, on the same Friday night.
