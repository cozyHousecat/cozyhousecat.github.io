Today a jury found neonatal nurse Lucy Letby guilty of murdering seven babies and attempting to murder six others at the Countess of Chester Hospital in 2015 and 2016. It’s an unimaginably painful day for the families, who have waited years for answers, and many of whom say they still don’t fully understand what happened to their children. There are also serious questions about why concerns raised by doctors at the time weren’t acted on sooner, and the government has announced an independent inquiry. Hospitals are supposed to be the safest places in the world for the tiniest, most vulnerable babies, which makes this so hard to take in. Tonight I’m just thinking of those families, and of all the nurses and doctors who care for premature babies every day.
