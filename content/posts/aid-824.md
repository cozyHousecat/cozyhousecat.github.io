Joe Biden made a surprise visit to Kyiv today, his first since the war began, after travelling into Ukraine secretly by train. He walked through the city with Zelensky while air raid sirens sounded, which made for some incredible pictures, and announced another $500 million in US support. Coming just days before the first anniversary of the full invasion, it was a clear message that America isn’t walking away. For an 80-year-old president to go into a war zone without any public warning is genuinely brave, whatever you think of his politics. It’s been an emotional day, with Kyiv standing strong at one end of the news and Turkey shaking again at the other.
