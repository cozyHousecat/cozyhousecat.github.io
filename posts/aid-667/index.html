Looking Back

2022-12-30cozyHousecat

Brazil has declared three days of mourning for Pelé, and the New Year Honours came out tonight with the Lionesses rightly all over the list after winning the Euros in the summer. That feels like a good moment to look back on a year that just would not sit still: war returned to Europe, the Queen died, we went through three Prime Ministers and a lettuce, inflation passed 11%, half the country seemed to be on strike, and a billionaire bought the place where we all argued about it. There were bright spots too: Kherson, Morocco’s run, Messi finally getting his World Cup, Hamza on Strictly, and a lot of ordinary people being brave and kind when they didn’t have to be. I started this blog in August as a little daily diary, and somewhere along the way it became my record of a very strange autumn, which I’m glad I kept. One more day of 2022, and I’m hoping 2023 is a bit kinder to all of us.
