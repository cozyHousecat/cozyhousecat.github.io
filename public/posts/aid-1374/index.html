Confirmed

2023-08-27cozyHousecat

Russian investigators have said today that DNA tests confirm Yevgeny Prigozhin was among the ten people killed in Wednesday’s crash, which ends a few days of “is he really dead?” speculation. The Kremlin has called suggestions that it was involved an “absolute lie”, but plenty of Western officials clearly have their suspicions, and I suspect we’ll never get a version of events that everyone believes. It’s hard not to think about the timing, exactly two months after a mutiny that, for one strange weekend in June, seemed to shake the whole country. I’m not going to pretend I have any sympathy for a man whose mercenaries were behind so much brutality, but the other people on that plane had families too, and I hope they get some kind of peace. It’s a reminder of how little any of us really knows about what goes on behind those walls.
