Kick-Off

2022-11-20cozyHousecat

The World Cup is finally underway, and Qatar became the first host nation ever to lose their opening match, 2–0 to Ecuador, with Enner Valencia scoring both. The BBC didn’t show the opening ceremony on BBC One and opened its coverage with Gary Lineker talking about the human rights concerns instead, which I thought was the right call. It’s been hard to enjoy, though, because I woke up to news of a shooting at Club Q, an LGBTQ+ nightclub in Colorado Springs, where five people were killed the night before Trans Day of Remembrance. Waking up to that, then watching a tournament in a country where being gay is illegal, with FIFA’s president saying “today I feel gay,” left me feeling hollow. England play Iran tomorrow, and I’ll watch, but tonight I’m thinking about the people at Club Q and everyone who just wants to be themselves and stay safe.
