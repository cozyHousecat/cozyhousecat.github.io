It’s the end of half-term for a lot of families, and the end of a week that’s felt much longer than five days. We’ve had Sturgeon resigning, inflation falling a bit, mystery objects in the sky turning out to be nothing much, and the heartbreaking news about Brianna, along with the vigils that showed how many people care. In Turkey and Syria, the number of people who died has passed 40,000, and survivors face a long, cold road to recovery. At the same time, AI chatbots are suddenly everywhere, confidently getting things wrong or acting strangely, which feels like a very 2023 thing to worry about alongside everything else. This weekend I’m planning very little: some long walks, good food and time with the people I love.
