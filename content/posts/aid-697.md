It’s Friday the 13th, which seems appropriate for a week in which Royal Mail has been hit by a cyber attack that has stopped it sending letters and parcels abroad. It’s reportedly the work of the LockBit ransomware gang, who encrypt a company’s systems and then demand money to unlock them, and it’s left small businesses that rely on overseas orders stuck with piles of packages they can’t send. It’s frightening how much damage one attack can do to an organisation that’s been around for more than 500 years, especially at a time when it’s already struggling with strikes. If there’s a lesson for the rest of us, it’s that the boring stuff really matters: keeping things updated, having offline backups, using proper passwords and multi-factor authentication, and training people to spot dodgy emails. Away from all the screens, fighting in eastern Ukraine around a town called Soledar has been some of the fiercest of the war, so I’m keeping things in perspective tonight.
