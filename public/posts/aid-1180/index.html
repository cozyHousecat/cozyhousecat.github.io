Goodbye, Spring

2023-05-31cozyHousecat

It’s the last day of meteorological spring, and it’s ending in long, warm, light evenings, the kind where it’s still bright at nine o’clock. This spring started with snow in March and ends with barbecues, and in between there were tanks for Ukraine, a coronation, Eurovision, banks collapsing, AI suddenly everywhere and far too many sad goodbyes. It feels like the world has been changing faster than I can keep up with, and writing a little every day has helped me make sense of it. Summer starts tomorrow, and I’m hoping for sunny weekends, more time outside, and slightly fewer headlines that make my stomach drop. Here’s to June.
