Zelensky arrived in Hiroshima today to meet G7 leaders in person, wearing his usual green military top as he stepped off a French government plane. On the same day, the head of Russia’s Wagner mercenary group claimed his forces had taken full control of Bakhmut, the eastern town at the centre of the longest and bloodiest battle of the war. Ukraine says the fighting isn’t over and that its troops still hold part of the city and are advancing around its edges. Months of fighting have left Bakhmut almost completely destroyed, with huge losses on both sides, all for a town that had about 70,000 people before the war. It’s a strange contrast, with world leaders meeting in a city rebuilt after unimaginable destruction while another city is being flattened.
