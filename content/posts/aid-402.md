Right, that's the heating on. Third of October. I held out for a fortnight longer than I would normally and I want that noted somewhere permanent.

It went on at half seven, after an evening of jumpers and a blanket and me saying "it's not that cold actually" in a room where it was, in fact, that cold.

Second post today, which I never do, but this felt like a date worth having on the record.

If you're currently sat in a jumper being stubborn about it: it's fine. Put it on. Being cold in your own house is not a personality and there's no prize.
