Burning Man, Drowning Mud

2023-09-02cozyHousecat

Burning Man has turned into a giant mud pit after heavy rain hit Nevada’s Black Rock Desert overnight, and organisers have closed the gates, stopped all vehicles and told the tens of thousands of people there to shelter in place and save their food and water. Some people, including a few celebrities, have apparently walked out miles through the mud to reach the road, which looks absolutely miserable. The internet has also been doing its usual thing of spreading wild rumours, including a completely made-up claim about a disease outbreak, so I’m only paying attention to the actual updates from organisers and local officials. I’ll admit there’s a tiny bit of a “festival goes wrong” spectacle to it all, but it’s a reminder that a desert can be just as dangerous when it’s wet as when it’s scorching. I really hope everyone stays safe and dry, and gets home without too much trouble. 🌧️
