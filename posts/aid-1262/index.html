Happy Birthday, NHS

2023-07-05cozyHousecat

The NHS is 75 years old today, and it started just down the road from here, when Aneurin Bevan opened Park Hospital in Davyhulme, now Trafford General, on 5 July 1948. The idea that everyone could get healthcare free when they needed it, whoever they were, was revolutionary then, and I still think it’s one of the best things this country has ever done. It’s having a hard birthday, though, with record waiting lists, exhausted staff and months of strikes over pay, and plenty of people are worried about its future. Whatever the arguments about how it should be run and funded, today feels like a day to say thank you to the nurses, doctors, porters, cleaners and everyone else who keeps it going. Elsewhere, Just Stop Oil protesters ran onto a court at Wimbledon and scattered orange confetti and jigsaw pieces, which was quickly swept up, and the tennis carried on, very British.
