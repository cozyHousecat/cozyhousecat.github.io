One Damaged File

2023-01-11cozyHousecat

This afternoon our time, every domestic flight in the US was grounded for a while after a system that sends safety notices to pilots stopped working, the first nationwide ground stop since 9/11. Thousands of flights were delayed or cancelled, and early reports suggest the cause was a single damaged database file. As someone who works around IT systems, that’s the kind of thing that makes me wince, because it shows how much depends on old, important systems that people don’t think about until they break. It’s a good reminder to test your backups, avoid single points of failure, and never assume that because something has always worked, it always will. Back home, ambulance workers were on strike again today, so it’s been a tough day for the people keeping things running in every sense.
