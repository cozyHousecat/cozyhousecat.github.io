Speaker Ousted

2023-10-03cozyHousecat

For the first time in American history, the US House of Representatives has voted to remove its own Speaker, ousting Republican Kevin McCarthy by 216 votes to 210 tonight. The vote was forced by fellow Republican Matt Gaetz, who was furious that McCarthy had worked with Democrats at the weekend to pass a short-term funding bill and avoid a government shutdown, and in the end eight Republicans joined every Democrat in voting him out. McCarthy’s supporters say he did the responsible thing by keeping the government open, while his critics on the right say he broke promises he made to win the job back in January, after that marathon fifteen rounds of voting. Either way, it leaves the House without an elected Speaker, which means it can’t really pass any legislation until a new one is chosen, and with another funding deadline coming up in November, that could get messy fast. Watching from over here, it’s a slightly surreal reminder that political chaos isn’t just a British speciality right now. 🇺🇸🏛️
