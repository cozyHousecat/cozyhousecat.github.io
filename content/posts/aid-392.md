Two weeks ago I wrote a fairly mournful post about a season of a video game ending and how you can't go back, and I stand by all of it, and I would like to report that I have not thought about Vibin' once since about Tuesday.

Paradise is great. I feel slightly disloyal saying so.

It's the Chrome that's done it. There's this silver stuff you can throw at a building, and the building turns to chrome, and then you can just walk through the wall. Somebody has spent months of their life in a proper job making it possible to walk through a wall, and the first time I did it by accident I made a noise out loud.

You can also turn yourself into a chrome blob, which makes you fast and immune to fall damage and, as far as I can tell, completely unbothered by anything. I have not worked out how to use this well. I mostly use it to get away from things at speed while shouting, which I suspect is not the intended competitive application.

The thing I actually wanted to say, though, and the reason I'm writing this at all, is about the grieving.

I was so sure that the specific thing I liked was the thing I liked. The map I knew, the routes I had, the spot I always dropped at. And it turns out what I liked was having a map I knew, and having routes, and having a spot, and two weeks in I have all of those again about a completely different place, and the fondness transferred over more or less intact while I wasn't looking.

Which is either a comforting thought or quite a bleak one depending on how much sleep you've had, and I've had a reasonable amount this week, so today I'm going with comforting.

Still miss the tree though. Not going to pretend about that.

Anyway. I'm terrible at this game and having the time of my life. New rates started today, meters read, bins are Wednesday, and I stopped work at four o'clock for the first time since August.
