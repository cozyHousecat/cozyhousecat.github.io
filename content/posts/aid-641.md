Overnight Twitter suspended several journalists from the New York Times, CNN, the Washington Post and others, all of whom had been reporting on the @ElonJet account being banned, with Musk claiming they’d shared “assassination coordinates” by linking to it. It also suspended Mastodon’s own account and started marking links to Mastodon as potentially harmful, which feels like someone trying to stop people finding the exit. The EU’s Věra Jourová has warned of “red lines” and possible sanctions under new EU rules, and the UN has criticised it too. Six weeks ago this platform was supposed to become a free speech haven, and tonight it’s banning reporters and blocking a competitor. If I needed a reason to finally move my main presence somewhere I actually have some control over, this is it.
