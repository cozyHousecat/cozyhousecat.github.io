“Not in a Vacuum”

2023-10-24cozyHousecat

The UN Secretary-General, António Guterres, has caused a big diplomatic row today after telling the Security Council that nothing could justify Hamas’s “appalling” attacks on Israel, but that they “did not happen in a vacuum”, pointing to the Palestinian people’s decades of living under occupation. Israel’s ambassador to the UN called for him to resign, and Israel’s foreign minister cancelled a planned meeting with him, saying the remarks were a justification of terrorism. Guterres’s supporters say he very clearly condemned the attacks, and that understanding the wider context isn’t the same as excusing violence, while his critics say that this wasn’t the moment to talk about context, with so many hostages still being held. He also called for an immediate humanitarian ceasefire, warning of the “clear violations” of international humanitarian law he says are happening in Gaza, where conditions are getting worse every day. I find it hard to know what the right words are at a time like this, but I really hope the arguments between diplomats don’t distract from getting the hostages home and getting real help to the civilians caught in the middle. 🕊️
