Elton John headlined the Pyramid Stage at Glastonbury tonight in what’s billed as his final ever UK show, and it was a proper celebration of a lifetime of songs. Watching tens of thousands of people singing along to “Your Song,” “Rocket Man” and “I’m Still Standing” on the telly was surprisingly emotional, and he brought out guests including Brandon Flowers to make it even more of an occasion. Over on the Other Stage at the same time, Queens of the Stone Age were headlining, five days after Matilda and I saw them in Halifax, so I kept flicking between the two. It’s been a long, hot, strange weekend of news, and ending it with Elton saying goodbye to the UK felt like the right way to close it out. What a career, and what a send-off.
