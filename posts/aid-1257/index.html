Centre Court

2023-07-03cozyHousecat

Wimbledon got going today, and of course rain stopped play on the outside courts for a good chunk of the afternoon, so Centre Court’s roof earned its keep. Novak Djokovic started his bid for an eighth Wimbledon title there, while everyone’s wondering whether this might be Andy Murray’s last Wimbledon and whether the young Spanish star Carlos Alcaraz can finally take the crown. I love how Wimbledon takes over the whole country for two weeks, even for people who never think about tennis the rest of the year. There’s something very soothing about the sound of the ball on grass and the polite applause drifting from the TV while you get on with other things. Two weeks of tennis, Pimm’s and dodging the rain, and I’m here for all of it.
