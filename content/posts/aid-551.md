It’s the first Remembrance weekend with a King rather than a Queen, and it’s another small reminder of how much has changed since September. Yesterday’s celebrations in Kherson are still all over my feed, and after this autumn it’s been genuinely moving to see that much joy in one place. Looking back at the week, we had a Cabinet minister resign over bullying claims, the economy shrinking, a crypto giant collapsing, Twitter selling and then pausing fake blue ticks, and the US midterms not going the way anyone predicted. Nevada is still counting tonight, and if it goes the Democrats’ way they keep the Senate without needing Georgia. Tomorrow I’ll take the two minutes, put the news away, and try to just have a quiet Sunday.
