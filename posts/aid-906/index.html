Twenty Minutes of Football

2023-03-11cozyHousecat

Tonight’s Match of the Day was just 20 minutes long, with no presenter, no pundits, no commentary and no theme tune, after nearly everyone refused to take part in support of Gary Lineker. The walkout spread across BBC Sport today, with other shows pulled from TV and radio, and even some players not doing post-match interviews. Some people say presenters on the BBC should keep their political views to themselves to protect its impartiality, while others say Lineker is a freelance sports presenter, not a news journalist, and should be free to speak, especially when former BBC people have strongly backed government policies. BBC Director-General Tim Davie has apologised for the disruption but says he won’t resign, and it’s hard to see how this gets fixed without someone backing down. I watched it anyway, and honestly it was strangely peaceful, just football, with no noise around it.
