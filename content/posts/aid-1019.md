Today was a double celebration: Easter Sunday and my mum’s birthday, all rolled into one long, happy, very full day. After a year of grim headlines, it was lovely to spend a whole day not thinking about the news and just enjoying being with family. Days like this are a reminder of what really matters, and that the small, ordinary moments are often the ones you remember. Happy birthday again, Mum, and I hope everyone else had a lovely Easter too.
