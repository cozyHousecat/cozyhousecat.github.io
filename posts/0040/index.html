the best sunday of the year

2022-08-28 1 min read (114 words)

Bank holiday Sunday, which is the best Sunday of the year on a technicality. All of the Sunday, none of the consequences. Tomorrow is somebody else’s problem and I am not required to be anywhere.

Everyone was out. Not doing anything especially, just out when the weather is behaving itself and there’s another day still to come. I like it out there on days like this but I don’t always.

It’s the last Sunday of August, which I feel rather more than seems reasonable. I get the distinct sense of something being quietly packed away.

Wasps were quiet today, incidentally. Either they’ve moved on or they’re regrouping.

I’ve decided not to look into it.
