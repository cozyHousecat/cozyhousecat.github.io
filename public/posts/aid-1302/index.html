Lauren James

2023-07-28cozyHousecat

The Lionesses beat Denmark 1–0 this morning, thanks to a brilliant early strike from Lauren James, who’s looking like one of the stars of the tournament. The worry is Keira Walsh, who was carried off with what looked like a nasty knee injury, and the whole team seemed shaken by it. Losing her would be a huge blow, since she’s so central to how England play. Still, two wins from two, and they’re nearly through to the knockouts. I’ve ended the week with a few rounds of Rocket League and a pizza, while hoping for good news about Walsh.
