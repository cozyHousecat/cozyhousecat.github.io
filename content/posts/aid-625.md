This afternoon four children were pulled from a frozen lake at Babbs Mill Park in Solihull after falling through the ice, and tonight they’re all in a critical condition in hospital. Emergency crews are still searching the lake in the dark and freezing cold because there are fears more children may have been on the ice. People who were there say members of the public and a police officer went into the water to try to reach them, which is incredibly brave. I keep thinking about the families, who must be living every parent’s worst fear right now, and about how quickly a fun afternoon in the snow turned into this. If you have children in your life, please remind them to keep off frozen water, however solid it looks.
