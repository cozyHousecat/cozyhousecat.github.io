Russia announced today that it’s pulling out of the Black Sea grain deal, the agreement that let Ukraine safely ship millions of tonnes of grain to the rest of the world during the war. The deal, brokered by the UN and Turkey last summer, helped bring global food prices down and get food to countries in Africa and the Middle East that rely on it. Russia says its own food and fertiliser exports were being blocked by sanctions and that it would rejoin if its demands are met, while the UN says millions of the world’s poorest people will pay the price. It’s the second time Russia has walked away from the deal, after briefly pulling out last October, but this time it sounds much more final. When we’re already struggling with food prices here, it’s frightening to think about the impact on people who were relying on that grain just to eat.
