From Partygate to Labour

2023-03-03cozyHousecat

The big Westminster story today is that Sue Gray, the senior civil servant who led the Partygate inquiry into Boris Johnson’s Downing Street, has resigned to become Keir Starmer’s chief of staff. Conservative critics say it raises questions about whether her investigation was impartial, and the Cabinet Office is looking at whether the rules on talking to an opposition party were followed. Labour says she’s a respected public servant with a long record of working for governments of all parties, and that she was appointed properly. Her supporters point out that her Partygate report was based on evidence, photos and messages, not opinion, so its findings stand either way. I suspect this will run for a while, mostly because it gives both sides something to argue about.
