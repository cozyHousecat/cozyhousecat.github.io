I’ve noticed that, on heavy news days like today, I reach for a game controller a lot more than I used to. Rocket League has become my go-to way of switching off after work, because a few chaotic five-minute matches of rocket-powered car football leave no room in my head to worry about anything else. Fortnite’s jungle season, with its raptors and vines, has been perfect for longer evenings, especially when the weather outside has been miserable all July. I used to feel a bit guilty about gaming, as if it was a waste of time, but I’ve started to see it as a genuine way of looking after myself, like reading a book or going for a walk. With so much going on in the world, I think it’s okay to have places where the stakes are just whether you score a goal.
