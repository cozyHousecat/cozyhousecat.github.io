Ukraine says it has taken back its first villages in the south-east of the country, the first concrete gains of its long-awaited counteroffensive. Soldiers posted videos raising the Ukrainian flag over a village called Blahodatne, and officials have announced a few more nearby. These are small places, and everyone warns this will be a long, hard fight against heavily dug-in Russian forces, with high losses on both sides. But after months of waiting and speculation, it feels like a significant moment, especially after the destruction of the Kakhovka dam earlier this week flooded huge areas and forced thousands from their homes. Here, the weekend’s heat broke into thunderstorms tonight, which felt strangely fitting for such a heavy, uncertain moment.
