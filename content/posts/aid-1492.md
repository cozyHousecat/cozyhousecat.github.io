South Africa are world champions again after beating New Zealand 12–11 in an incredibly tense Rugby World Cup final in Paris tonight, becoming the first team ever to win the trophy four times. It was a brutal, nervy game with no Springbok tries at all, as their points came from Handré Pollard's boot, while New Zealand had their captain Sam Cane sent off, the first red card ever shown in a World Cup final. The All Blacks still managed a try and pushed right to the end, but a missed late penalty kick and some heroic South African defence meant they couldn't quite get over the line. After England lost to the Springboks by a single point in the semi-final last weekend, and France lost to them by a single point in the quarter-final, I think it's fair to say they've mastered the art of winning ugly when it matters most. Congratulations to South Africa, and thank you to France for hosting such a brilliant tournament, because I've enjoyed it far more than I ever expected! 🏆🏉
