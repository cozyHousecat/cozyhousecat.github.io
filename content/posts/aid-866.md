There’s been a small bit of progress in the NHS pay disputes this week: the nurses’ union has paused its strikes while it holds proper talks with the government about pay. Junior doctors in England are going the other way, though, and are due to strike for three full days from 13 March after 98% of those who voted backed action. They say their pay has fallen by more than a quarter in real terms since 2008, and that many are leaving for Australia or quitting medicine altogether, while the government says their demands are unaffordable. It’s hard to see how the NHS gets through a long, tiring winter and into a better spring if so many of its staff feel this undervalued. I really hope the nurses’ talks lead somewhere, and that the same thing happens for the doctors before the 13th.
