Good Company

2023-08-05cozyHousecat

This evening we hung out with Davey and Dingle, and it was exactly what I needed after a busy few weeks. There was food, drinks, lots of catching up and the kind of laughing where your face hurts by the end of the night. Earlier in the summer my anxiety kept me from seeing people, so it means a lot to me to have an easy, happy evening with friends where I didn’t overthink a single thing. It’s funny how much a few hours with the right people can reset your whole week. I’m heading to bed tired, full and very grateful for good friends.
