Today is Holocaust Memorial Day, 78 years since Auschwitz-Birkenau was liberated, and this year’s theme is “Ordinary People.” It’s about how the Holocaust wasn’t carried out by monsters alone, but made possible by ordinary people who took part, looked away or stayed quiet, and also how ordinary people sometimes risked everything to help. Six million Jewish people were murdered, along with Roma and Sinti, disabled people, gay men and many others, and the number of survivors still able to tell their stories gets smaller every year. After seeing a huge celebrity post a swastika to millions of people only a few weeks ago, it feels more important than ever to remember, and to call out antisemitism and hatred wherever it shows up. Tonight I’m thinking of the people who were lost, and promising myself not to be one of those who look away.
