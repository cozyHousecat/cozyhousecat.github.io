Six

2023-08-01cozyHousecat

The Lionesses finally clicked today, beating China 6–1 to top their World Cup group, and Lauren James was unstoppable, scoring twice and setting up three more. After two nervy 1–0 wins, it was such a relief to see them play with freedom and confidence, and the goals were brilliant. They play Nigeria in the last 16 next Monday, and suddenly it feels like they could go all the way. In very different news tonight, Donald Trump has been indicted for a third time, this time on federal charges over his efforts to overturn the 2020 election, which he says is another politically motivated attack. A very good day for English football, and a very big one for American politics.
