Italy has become the first Western country to temporarily block ChatGPT, with its data protection regulator saying there’s no proper legal basis for collecting the huge amount of personal data used to train it. It also pointed to a bug earlier this month that showed some users other people’s chat titles and payment details, and said there are no proper checks to keep children off it. OpenAI says it follows privacy laws and has switched ChatGPT off in Italy while it sorts things out. As someone who cares a lot about privacy and who owns our data, I’m glad at least one regulator is asking these questions now rather than years later. It’s been quite a March for AI, from GPT-4 to calls for a pause, and I suspect the arguments about rules and regulation are only just beginning.
