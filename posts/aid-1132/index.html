Emilia-Romagna

2023-05-17cozyHousecat

Northern Italy’s Emilia-Romagna region has been hit by devastating floods, after months’ worth of rain fell in a couple of days and more than 20 rivers burst their banks. At least nine people have died, thousands have been forced from their homes, and whole towns are under muddy water. This weekend’s Formula 1 race at Imola has been cancelled, which tells you how serious it is. The cruel part is that the region had been suffering from drought, so the dry, hard ground couldn’t soak up the rain, which is exactly the kind of extreme swing scientists warn climate change is making more common. I’m thinking of everyone there trying to save what they can, and of the volunteers already shovelling mud out of homes and shops.
