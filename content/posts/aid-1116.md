Elon Musk has announced that Linda Yaccarino, a senior advertising executive from NBCUniversal, will be Twitter’s new chief executive, with Musk focusing on the product and the technology. It makes sense on paper, since Twitter has lost a lot of advertisers since Musk took over, and she has a lot of experience working with big brands. The big question is whether Musk will really let her run things, given how much he’s controlled every decision over the past six months, from blue ticks to banning journalists. After everything that’s happened since October, from mass layoffs to fake verified accounts, it’s hard to imagine any CEO having an easy job. I’ll be watching with interest, mostly from my own little corner of the internet.
