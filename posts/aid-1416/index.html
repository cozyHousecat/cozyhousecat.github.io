A Stand-Up Strike

2023-09-15cozyHousecat

For the first time ever, the United Auto Workers union in the US has gone on strike against all three of Detroit’s big carmakers at the same time, with around thirteen thousand workers walking out at a Ford, a GM and a Stellantis plant from midnight. Instead of a full walkout, the union’s calling it a “stand-up strike”, starting small at three factories and threatening to spread to more if talks don’t move, which I think is a really clever bit of strategy. The workers are asking for big pay rises after years of high inflation and record company profits, along with an end to the system where newer staff are paid less for the same job, and the companies say giving them everything would make it hard to compete, especially as they spend huge sums moving to electric vehicles. Hearing that people doing the same job on the same line can be on completely different pay scales just doesn’t sit right with me. It’s going to be fascinating to see how it plays out, and I hope both sides can reach a deal that’s fair to the people actually building the cars. 🚗✊
