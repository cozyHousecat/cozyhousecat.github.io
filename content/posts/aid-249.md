Monday, and the first properly warm one since he came back. Not twenty eight yet. Building up to it slowly, like he wants us to notice the effort involved.

There's an education person in the paper today explaining that girls will do better than boys in this week's GCSEs because the girls revised during lockdown and the boys played PlayStation.

I have read this three times now and it has not improved.

I would like to register, from a safe distance of some years, that it is entirely possible to do both. I did both. Simultaneously, on occasion, which I appreciate is not the same as doing either of them well.

The other possibility, which the piece does not trouble itself with, is that a cohort sitting their first real exams after two years of not having any might produce results that resist one tidy explanation. But that won't go in a headline. PlayStation goes in a headline.

Elsewhere, and considerably less funny, something like 1.7 million households are reckoning on cancelling their energy direct debits. Not switching, not haggling, not ringing up to complain. Cancelling. Which tells you fairly precisely where we've got to, four days out. Its a great time to work for an energy company, sigh.

Twenty eight on Wednesday, then. Three days of being pleased about the weather, and then Friday, and then I expect we all go a bit quiet for a while.
