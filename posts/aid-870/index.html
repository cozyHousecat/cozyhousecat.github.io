Black Balloons

2023-03-05cozyHousecat

Tens of thousands of people marched in Athens, Thessaloniki and other Greek cities today in anger and grief over the Tempi train crash, which killed 57 people, many of them students. People released black balloons into the sky, one for each person who died, and there were chants and placards asking why safety systems that workers had warned about for years still hadn’t been fixed. Most of the protests were peaceful, though there were some clashes with police in Athens. It’s the kind of anger that comes when people feel a tragedy didn’t need to happen, and when the victims were young people who had their whole lives ahead of them. I hope the families get real answers, and that Greece’s railways are made safe so this never happens again.
