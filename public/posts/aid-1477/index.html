A Heavy Day for Sport

2023-10-21cozyHousecat

Manchester woke up to sad news this morning, with the announcement that Sir Bobby Charlton has died at the age of 86. He survived the Munich air disaster, won the World Cup with England in 1966 and the European Cup with United in 1968, and was always described as a gentleman of the game, so it feels like the end of an era, not just for United fans, but for the whole city and for English football. Then tonight came the rugby, and what a gut-wrenching game it was, with England leading South Africa 15–6 for most of the World Cup semi-final in Paris, before the Springboks came roaring back late on and Handré Pollard kicked a long-range penalty with just a couple of minutes left to win it 16–15. I really thought England were going to do it, and I’m so proud of a team that nobody expected to get anywhere near this far after a rough summer. A little bit of brighter news from the Middle East too, with the first lorries of aid finally crossing into Gaza from Egypt today, though aid agencies say it’s only a tiny fraction of what’s needed. It’s been a day of a lot of emotions, so I’m going to have a cup of tea and an early night. 🕊️🏉
