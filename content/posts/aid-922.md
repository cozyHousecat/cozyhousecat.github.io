Jeremy Hunt’s Budget today included a big expansion of free childcare in England, eventually giving working parents 30 free hours a week from when their children are nine months old, which could make a huge difference to families. He also scrapped the limit on how much people can save in their pensions tax-free, which the government says will keep doctors and experienced workers from retiring early, though critics say it mostly helps the wealthy. The government’s energy bill support will stay at its current level until the summer, fuel duty is frozen again, and the forecasters now think the UK will avoid a recession this year. At the same time, hundreds of thousands of teachers, junior doctors, civil servants and Tube drivers were on strike over pay, a reminder that many people still feel left behind. It’s the most hopeful Budget for a while, but whether it feels that way depends a lot on who you are.
