The Women This Year

2023-03-08cozyHousecat

On International Women’s Day, I’ve been thinking about how many of the biggest news stories of the past year have really been about women. There are the women in Iran protesting under the slogan “Woman, Life, Freedom,” the Afghan women and girls banned from schools and universities, and the Ukrainian women holding families together through the war. Closer to home, there are the nurses, most of them women, striking for fair pay, and the women failed for years by the police in the David Carrick case. Then there’s Brianna, and every young trans girl who deserves to grow up safe. It’s a lot to carry, but it also shows just how much women everywhere are pushing for change, and today I’m grateful to every woman in my life who makes the world kinder and braver.
