Atlas Lions

2022-12-06cozyHousecat

Morocco knocked out Spain on penalties tonight after a 0–0 draw, with goalkeeper Yassine Bounou saving twice and Achraf Hakimi finishing it with a cheeky chipped penalty. That puts Morocco in the quarter-finals for the first time ever, only the fourth African team to get that far, and the celebrations from their fans, across the Arab world and in Morocco’s diaspora everywhere, have been wonderful to watch. Spain had loads of the ball and barely threatened, which feels like their whole tournament in one game. Later, Portugal beat Switzerland 6–1, with Gonçalo Ramos scoring a hat-trick in his first World Cup start, playing in Ronaldo’s place after Ronaldo was dropped to the bench. Morocco against Portugal in the quarters is now one of the games I’m most excited about.
