Nobody Saw That Coming

2022-11-22cozyHousecat

Saudi Arabia beat Argentina 2–1 today, and it’s already being called one of the biggest shocks in World Cup history. Messi scored an early penalty and it looked like it would be easy, then Saleh Al-Shehri and Salem Al-Dawsari scored within five minutes of each other after half-time, ending Argentina’s 36-game unbeaten run. The Saudi players went wild, the fans went wild, and King Salman has declared tomorrow a public holiday, which is the most extra (and honestly brilliant) reaction to a football match I can imagine. Later, France went a goal down to Australia before winning 4–1, with Olivier Giroud scoring twice to equal Thierry Henry’s record of 51 goals for France. After a weekend that was mostly about armbands and politics, it was really nice to have a day where the football itself was the story.
