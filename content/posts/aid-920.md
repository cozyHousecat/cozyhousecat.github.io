OpenAI released GPT-4 today, the new version of the AI behind ChatGPT, and the company says it can pass difficult exams like the bar exam and will be able to understand images as well as text. It’s only been three and a half months since ChatGPT first appeared, back on my 600th post, and it already feels like it’s everywhere, from homework to customer service. As someone working in IT, I’m genuinely excited about what tools like this could do for boring, repetitive tasks. I’m also wary of how much power it puts in the hands of a few big companies, and of how confidently these systems can get things wrong. Whatever happens, I don’t think anyone can call this a passing fad any more.
