Zelensky made a surprise visit to London today, his first since the full invasion, and spoke to MPs and peers in Westminster Hall. He thanked the UK for standing with Ukraine from the start, then asked for fighter jets, handing the Speaker a pilot’s helmet signed by a Ukrainian airman with the words “we have freedom, give us wings to protect it.” He also met the King, and Sunak announced the UK will start training Ukrainian fighter pilots, saying nothing is off the table. Seeing him in Westminster Hall, where so much of our own history has happened, was really moving. On a week when the news from Turkey and Syria has been so devastating, it was a reminder of how much people can achieve when they refuse to give up.
