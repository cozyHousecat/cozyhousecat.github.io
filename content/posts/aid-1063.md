The London Marathon was brilliant this morning: Kelvin Kiptum smashed the course record in the men’s race, and Sifan Hassan won the women’s race after stopping mid-run to stretch out an injury, which is absolutely incredible. Then this evening, Manchester United beat Brighton on penalties, setting up the first ever all-Manchester FA Cup final in June, so the whole city is going to be talking about nothing else. Away from the sport, British forces evacuated UK diplomats and their families from Sudan overnight, but thousands of British citizens there haven’t been evacuated yet, and some of their families are understandably worried and angry. The government says the situation is incredibly dangerous and that it’s working on options. It’s been a day of huge highs and real worry, and I hope everyone still stuck in Khartoum gets out safely soon.
