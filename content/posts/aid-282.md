**I have started building something that nobody asked for, nobody needs, and which will take roughly four times as long as the sensible version, because I have decided it ought to look like a spaceship.**

It's a status page. For the house. A screen that tells everybody living here whether the films are working, whether the photos are working, and whether the internet is having one of its afternoons.

The useful version of this is a list. White background, green ticks, red crosses, finished by lunchtime, everybody gets on with their day.

I am not building the useful version.

Every computer in this house is named after a Stargate episode and has been for years. There was never a realistic scenario in which I was going to build a screen for this house and then make it look like a normal screen. Chunky blue panels. Slightly too many lights. The general air of a room in which somebody is about to say "we're picking up an energy signature".

I spent forty minutes today choosing a typeface. Forty minutes. For a page that four people will ever look at, in a building where I could simply shout.

In my defence, and I would like a defence, it does solve something real. Nobody here ever tells me when something's broken, because they don't want to bother me, because I am perpetually doing something to the server. So the page will say when a thing is broken and also whether I already know about it, which means nobody ever has to stand there weighing up whether it's worth mentioning.

That's the justification. It's a genuinely good justification.

It is not why I'm doing it.

Anyway. New Prime Minister on Monday, apparently. September is being extremely September about everything.

I'm going to go and have an argument with a colour picker.
