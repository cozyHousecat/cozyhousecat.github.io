At Ulster University in Belfast today, Biden praised the Good Friday Agreement as one of the great achievements of modern politics and urged Northern Ireland’s leaders to get the Stormont government working again. He also promised that American businesses are ready to invest in Northern Ireland if the political situation becomes stable, which is quite an incentive. Not everyone was won over, with some unionists feeling his visit leaned more towards Ireland than the UK, and the DUP showing no sign yet of ending its boycott of Stormont. Still, it was a hopeful speech about how far Northern Ireland has come in 25 years. It’s been a busy news day full of politics, but the strike and the wind are what most people here will remember.
