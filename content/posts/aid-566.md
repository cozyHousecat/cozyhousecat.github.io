All day my timeline has been half goodbye party, half emergency evacuation, with people posting their Mastodon handles and other places to find them in case the whole thing falls over this weekend. Musk is posting memes and claiming usage just hit an all-time high, while someone in San Francisco has been projecting messages onto the Twitter building calling it his “hellscape.” Under the jokes there’s a real point: years of people’s posts, photos and conversations sit on servers that one person now controls, run by a team that’s been halved and then halved again. That’s exactly why I care about owning my own stuff, so this weekend I’m going to request my Twitter archive, just in case. Then I’ll put my phone down, watch a bit of Children in Need, and get ready for the World Cup starting on Sunday.
