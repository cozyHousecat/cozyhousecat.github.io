Final Eve

2023-08-19cozyHousecat

Tomorrow morning at 11, the Lionesses play Spain in the Women’s World Cup final in Sydney, and I’m already feeling the nerves. England have never won a World Cup in women’s football, and the men haven’t won one since 1966, so a whole country is going to be glued to the TV. Spain have some of the best players in the world, despite a difficult year behind the scenes, when many of their top players refused to play in protest over how the team was run. Sarina Wiegman has now taken teams to three major finals in a row, winning the Euros with both the Netherlands and England, which is an incredible record. I spent the evening playing Rocket League to calm my nerves, and I don’t think it worked at all.
