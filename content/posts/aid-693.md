Last night was supposed to be a historic moment for the UK, the first ever attempt to launch satellites into orbit from British soil. Virgin Orbit’s modified 747, called Cosmic Girl, took off from Spaceport Cornwall in Newquay and released its rocket over the Atlantic, but something went wrong with the rocket’s second stage and the nine satellites it was carrying were lost. The crowd who’d gone to watch in the cold had been so excited, and you could hear the disappointment in the coverage this morning. It’s a real shame for everyone who worked on it, especially as it was meant to show the UK could have its own space launch industry. Space is hard, though, and almost every big launch programme has failures early on, so I hope they get to try again.
