The G7 summit in Hiroshima ended today, and the leaders agreed to keep supporting Ukraine “for as long as it takes,” while also warning China about using its economic power to pressure other countries. Zelensky said photos of Hiroshima after the bomb reminded him of Bakhmut today, and that seeing how Hiroshima was rebuilt gave him hope for his own country. It’s a powerful image, a city that was almost wiped out now hosting world leaders talking about peace. Not everyone agrees on how the war should end, and China has strongly criticised the summit, but it was a clear show of unity behind Ukraine. It’s been quite a weekend of news, from trophies in Manchester to history in Hiroshima.
