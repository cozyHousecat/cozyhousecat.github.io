Canada Points the Finger at India

2023-09-18cozyHousecat

Big diplomatic news tonight: Canada’s Prime Minister Justin Trudeau told parliament that Canadian security agencies are investigating “credible allegations” that agents of the Indian government were linked to the killing of Sikh leader Hardeep Singh Nijjar, who was shot outside a gurdwara in British Columbia in June. Canada has already expelled a senior Indian diplomat, which is a huge step between two close democratic partners, and coming just after the G20 in Delhi it feels even more striking. India’s government has been critical of Nijjar, who campaigned for a separate Sikh homeland, and I’d expect it to strongly deny any involvement, so this could get very tense very quickly. It’s worrying to think about what it means for the large Sikh and Indian communities in Canada, who will be caught in the middle of all this. I’m trying to wait for more facts before drawing any conclusions, because accusations like this are incredibly serious, and the evidence will matter enormously. 🇨🇦🇮🇳
