It’s the start of what people are calling “Awful April,” when lots of household bills go up at once. Council tax is up by as much as 5% in many areas, water bills are up by around 7.5% on average, and many phone and broadband contracts are rising by around 14% mid-contract, while the £400 energy discount everyone got over the winter has now ended. Even with inflation supposedly starting to come down, it’s a lot to absorb for families who’ve already been stretched for months. If you can, it’s worth checking whether you can switch phone or broadband deals, or whether you qualify for a social tariff, which can be much cheaper. It’s not the most cheerful post for the first day of spring proper, but I’d rather know what’s coming than be caught out.
