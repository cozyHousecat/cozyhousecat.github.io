Just days after Morocco's earthquake, there's more devastating news from North Africa: Storm Daniel has caused catastrophic flooding in eastern Libya, and in the coastal city of Derna two dams reportedly burst overnight, sending a wall of water through the city. Officials in the east of the country are saying more than two thousand people are feared dead and thousands more are missing, with whole neighbourhoods reportedly swept away. Libya has been split between rival governments for years, which I imagine will make an already overwhelming rescue and relief effort even harder. It's the same storm that caused deadly floods in Greece, Turkey and Bulgaria last week, and it's hard not to wonder how much warmer seas are making storms like this more intense. My heart goes out to everyone in Derna and across eastern Libya tonight, and I hope help reaches them quickly. 💙
