Balmoral

2022-09-06 2 min read (257 words)

New Prime Minister officially took the job today, and it happened in Scotland, which apparently it never has before.

The handover normally goes on at Buckingham Palace. This time everybody flew up to Balmoral and did it there instead, because the Queen isn’t travelling much these days.

I find that I have no opinion whatsoever about this and I have been quietly enjoying that.

There’s a photograph of it that’s been absolutely everywhere today. She looks small, and rather cheerful about the whole business. Ninety six years old and still doing the job on a Tuesday.

Meanwhile the thing I’m actually waiting on is still somewhere over the horizon. Energy package, days away, possibly. Twenty five days until the cap.

In considerably smaller news, I have spent a week building a screen for my hallway and it has now taught me, on three separate occasions, that I design things for the chair I happen to be sitting in rather than the place they end up living.

Today’s instalment: I built a form for reporting that a fault is known about, which works beautifully at a desk, for a job that only ever happens while walking past something with your hands full.

I’m told this is called user research and that there are people who get paid for it. I have been doing it by building the wrong thing repeatedly and then walking past it.

Autumn’s properly settled in now. Jumper weather. The specific kind where you aren’t actually cold, you just fancy a jumper.

Twenty five days.
