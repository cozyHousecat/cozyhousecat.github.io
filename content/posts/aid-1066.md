Tonight the US announced a 72-hour ceasefire in Sudan between the army and the paramilitary group they’ve been fighting for more than a week. Hundreds of people have already been killed and thousands injured, with many hospitals in Khartoum forced to close and people running out of food, water and power. The UK government says it’s preparing to evacuate British nationals, and families with relatives there will be hoping the ceasefire holds long enough to get people out. Previous truces have collapsed almost straight away, so nobody is getting their hopes up too much. I’m thinking of everyone in Sudan who has nowhere to go and no way out.
