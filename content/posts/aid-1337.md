This afternoon we went to Dragfest in Manchester, and if I’m honest, I came away a bit disappointed. I’d been really looking forward to it, and I have nothing but respect for the performers, because drag is hard work and takes real guts, but the event as a whole just didn’t land for me the way I’d hoped. Maybe it’s because Cheddar Gorgeous’s Party Monster Ball back in June set the bar so high, with all its energy and creativity, that anything else was going to struggle to live up to it. It’s still nice to get out and support queer performers in Manchester, even if not every show is going to be a favourite. At least the day started brilliantly, with the Lionesses beating Colombia, so it wasn’t a total loss.
