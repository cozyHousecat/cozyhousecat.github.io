Turkey has started detaining building contractors and issuing arrest warrants after so many newer apartment blocks collapsed in the earthquakes. More than 25,000 people are now known to have died across Turkey and Syria, and the focus is slowly shifting from rescue to recovery, though people are still being found alive in a few places. There’s a lot of anger about whether earthquake building rules were properly followed, given how many blocks crumbled while others nearby stayed standing. Grieving families deserve answers, and real accountability could save lives next time, as long as it doesn’t stop with a few individuals. Tonight I’m thinking about everyone still waiting for news of the people they love.
