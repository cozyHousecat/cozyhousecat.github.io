Hamza Yassin and Jowita Przystał won the Strictly Come Dancing final tonight, and I don’t think I’ve cheered at the telly that much in ages. Hamza is a wildlife cameraman and CBeebies presenter who’d never really danced before, and over the series he went from nervous to absolutely flying, especially in that salsa. It was just pure, simple joy to watch, and after a month of strikes, fires, lockdown protests, freezing lakes and Twitter meltdowns, it was exactly what I needed. Sometimes the nicest thing you can do is curl up on the sofa with a cup of tea and watch someone you’ve been rooting for get their happy ending. Tomorrow it’s the World Cup final, so the weekend isn’t done yet.
