Drones Over the Kremlin

2023-05-03cozyHousecat

Russia says it shot down two drones over the Kremlin overnight and is accusing Ukraine of trying to assassinate Putin, threatening to retaliate. Ukraine denies having anything to do with it, and some analysts think it could have been staged, or will be used as an excuse for more attacks. Nobody outside Russia seems to know for sure what happened, and the US says it’s being very careful about believing either side without evidence. It’s also World Press Freedom Day, which feels fitting when it’s so hard to get reliable information out of Russia, especially with journalists like Evan Gershkovich still in prison there. Days like this are a reminder to read news carefully and not jump to conclusions, especially when claims are coming from governments at war.
