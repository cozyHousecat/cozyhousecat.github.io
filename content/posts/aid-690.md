The day after the attack on Brazil’s government buildings, around 1,500 people have been detained, and Lula has walked through the wrecked rooms of the presidential palace with officials. Brazil’s Supreme Court has suspended the governor of the capital’s region for 90 days, after questions about why security let the crowds get so close. Bolsonaro himself is in hospital in Florida with stomach pains, and has said on social media that the riots went beyond peaceful protest, though critics say he spent years encouraging the idea that the election would be stolen. Leaders around the world, from left and right, have condemned the attack and backed Lula, which is reassuring. After two days of watching it all, I’m grateful our own fights about strikes and pay are happening through talks and picket lines rather than broken windows.
