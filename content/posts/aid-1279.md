Markéta Vondroušová of the Czech Republic won the Wimbledon women’s title today, beating Ons Jabeur in the final, and she’s the first unseeded woman to win it in the Open era. Even better, a year ago she came to Wimbledon as a tourist with her arm in a cast after wrist surgery, so this has to be one of the most remarkable comebacks in tennis. My heart did go out to Jabeur, though, who lost her second Wimbledon final in a row and was clearly devastated, after being such a popular player all fortnight. The Princess of Wales comforting her on court was a lovely moment. It was a great reminder that sport, like life, can turn around completely in a year, for better or worse.
