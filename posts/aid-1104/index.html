Windsor Concert

2023-05-07cozyHousecat

Tonight’s coronation concert at Windsor Castle was a big, cheesy, joyful evening, with Katy Perry, Lionel Richie and Take That on stage and lots of flag-waving from the crowd. The drone show was the highlight for me, with lights in the sky forming shapes from a teapot to a lion, while landmarks around the country were lit up at the same time. Prince William gave a warm speech about his dad, saying how proud the family is of him, and the King looked genuinely happy, tapping his foot along to the music. After yesterday’s ancient ceremony, it felt more like a big family party for the country. Tomorrow is a bank holiday, with the “Big Help Out” encouraging people to volunteer, which is a nice way to end the weekend.
