Bring Your ID

2023-05-02cozyHousecat

Thursday’s local elections across much of England will be the first where voters have to show photo ID at the polling station, like a passport, driving licence or certain bus passes. The government says it’s about stopping voter fraud and protecting trust in elections, while critics point out that in-person fraud is extremely rare and worry that people without ID, often older, poorer or younger voters, might be turned away. There was a free voter ID certificate people could apply for, but the deadline has already passed, so if you’re voting, it’s worth checking you’ve got an accepted ID before Thursday. Whatever you think about the rule, local elections matter a lot for things like bins, roads and libraries, and turnout is often low. If you can vote, please do, and remember your ID.
