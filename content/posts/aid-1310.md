England won the final Ashes Test at the Oval by 49 runs today, levelling the series at 2–2, and Stuart Broad took the last two wickets in the final moments of his career, including the very last ball that won the match. You honestly couldn’t have written a better ending, with the crowd on its feet and his teammates mobbing him on the pitch. Australia keep the Ashes because they held them coming into the series, but after being 2–0 down, England fought back brilliantly, and the whole summer has been unforgettable. It’s been one of the most exciting cricket series I can remember, full of drama, arguments and comebacks, and it got me properly hooked on Test cricket. Goodbye July, and thank you, Broady.
