This morning in Nanterre, near Paris, a 17-year-old called Nahel was shot dead by a police officer during a traffic stop, and a video filmed by a bystander appears to contradict the police’s first account of what happened. Tonight there’s been anger and unrest in the area, and people across France are asking hard questions about policing, especially in poorer suburbs with large immigrant communities. Then this evening in Ukraine, a Russian missile hit a busy restaurant in the centre of Kramatorsk, where people, including children, were having dinner, and the number of people killed is still rising. Two young lives, two families’ worst nights, in two very different places. Tonight I’m thinking of everyone in Nanterre and Kramatorsk.
