**Season ends Sunday and I have got about forty levels left to do, which is entirely my own fault and which I will be correcting this week at the expense of both sleep and dignity.**

It's called Vibin', this one. Started in June, runs to the eighteenth, and the entire theme of it has been summer, chilled out, nice. There's a mountain with a rave going on inside it and a rollercoaster round the outside of it. You plant seeds in the ground and they grow into trees that drop guns, which is gardening, when you think about it, I have spent three months doing horticulture in a shooter. There was ice cream you could eat to make yourself go faster.

The secret skin is Darth Vader, which you unlock by doing Indiana Jones quests, and I would like to be clear that I typed that sentence out and read it back to myself and it does still say what it says.

Then in August they put Dragon Ball in it, so for a few weeks you could Kamehameha somebody off a roof, and there was a cloud you could stand on and fly about. I have no particular attachment to Dragon Ball whatsoever and I did it constantly anyway.

So, six days left, and then the map changes and everything I've learned about where to land and which way to run stops being worth anything at all, which I gather happens every three months and which is very obviously the entire point of the thing, although this is the first time I've been sorry about it.

The thing I keep thinking though, and I appreciate this is an unreasonable amount of weight to hang on a video game, is that it was called Vibin' and it has been a genuinely lovely summer in there. Sunny, daft, ice cream, a rave in a mountain, seeds growing into guns. Whereas out here we've had forty degrees, a drought, hosepipe bans everywhere except conveniently up here, everybody's bills going up eighty per cent in one step, and then a fortnight I don't need to describe to anybody who's been outside.

None of which got anywhere near it, because why on earth would it.

I don't think that's escapism exactly, or if it is then I've gone off using escapism as the criticism people use it as. It was somewhere the summer went well. I would have quite liked a summer that went well and I did not get one out here, and I did get about a hundred hours of one in a place where the worst thing that can happen to you is somebody turning up with a better gun.

Forty levels. Six days. Going to be a great deal of Rave Cave.

Back to it.
