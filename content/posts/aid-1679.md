What a week it's been in tech, so here's a quick round-up of the stories that caught my eye. On Wednesday the European Parliament approved the AI Act, the world's first major AI law, and on the same day the US House voted to force TikTok's owner to sell the app or see it banned, which shows how much the conversation about who controls our data and our algorithms is heating up. Earlier in the week, a startup called Cognition showed off "Devin", an AI it calls a software engineer that can write and fix code on its own, which got a lot of developers nervously laughing. The Kate photo saga also turned into a very public lesson in how a few edits can wreck trust in an image, especially now that AI makes fakes so easy. Then on Thursday, Starship flew further than ever before, which was a lovely bit of pure wonder in among all the policy and drama. It's a strange and exciting time to work in tech, and I can't wait to see what next week brings! 💻📰
