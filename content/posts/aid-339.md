Finished the battle pass this afternoon with about six hours to spare, went back round the Reality Biome one last time, and got shot in the back of the head while standing still admiring some mushrooms.

Entirely deserved. I'd have shot me as well.

And then it went, and I've been thinking about something since that hadn't occurred to me once in three months.

You can't go back and look at it.

There's no old version you can load up. Nowhere to wander round the biome again, no archive, no museum build, nothing. It ran for three months and then it was replaced, and the island I spent all summer in now exists in screenshots and other people's videos and my own increasingly unreliable memory of roughly where everything was.

Which is presumably obvious to anybody who's been playing these things for years. It genuinely had not landed on me until tonight.

I don't think it should be any different, mind. If they'd left the old island up forever it would have turned into a museum instead of a place everybody was in at the same time, and the whole reason it felt like anything was that everybody was in it at once and now nobody is.

Doesn't make it feel any less strange though.

New season's up in the small hours. I'll go and look tomorrow, or the day after.

Funeral in the morning. Everything's shut.
