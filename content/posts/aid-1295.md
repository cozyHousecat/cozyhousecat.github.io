It actually happened: Twitter’s website now shows a plain black “X” logo instead of the little blue bird, after Musk announced the rebrand last night. In San Francisco, workers started taking the Twitter sign off the company’s headquarters, only for police to turn up because nobody had got permission, which feels like a perfect summary of the last nine months. Musk says X will become an “everything app” for messaging, payments and more, but for now it’s mostly just the same site with a new logo and a lot of confused users. Losing one of the most recognisable logos in the world feels strange, and I suspect people will be calling it Twitter, and posts “tweets,” for years. Elsewhere, drones hit buildings in Moscow again this morning, a reminder that the war is reaching Russia’s capital more often.
