Tonight I took part in one of Fortnite's Performance Evaluation tournaments, which is very different from my usual relaxed heists on the sofa. The lobbies were a lot sweatier than I'm used to, with everyone building and editing at lightning speed, and my heart was pounding from the very first drop. I definitely made some silly mistakes under pressure, but I also had a few moments where everything clicked, and those little wins felt amazing. More than anything it was a great reminder of how much there is still to learn, from rotating early to knowing when to fight and when to just stay alive. I'm proud of myself for having a go rather than just watching from the sidelines, and I'm already thinking about what I'd do differently next time. 🎮🏆
