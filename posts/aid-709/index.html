Brovary

2023-01-18cozyHousecat

This morning a helicopter crashed next to a kindergarten in Brovary, just outside Kyiv, killing Ukraine’s Interior Minister Denys Monastyrskyi, other senior officials and people on the ground, including children. Nobody yet knows why it came down, and Ukraine says it’s investigating, but the thought of it falling at a nursery while children were arriving for the day is almost too much. It comes only four days after the missile strike on the apartment block in Dnipro, so it’s been a brutal week for Ukraine. Here, nurses were on strike today and are out again tomorrow, which is a real problem, but tonight it feels small compared to what people there are going through. I’m thinking of the families in Brovary.
