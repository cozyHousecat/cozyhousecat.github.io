The White House Writes AI Rules

2023-10-30cozyHousecat

President Biden has signed a big executive order on artificial intelligence today, just days before the UK hosts its AI Safety Summit at Bletchley Park. Among other things, it requires companies building the most powerful AI models to share their safety test results with the US government before releasing them, and it asks government agencies to develop standards for testing AI and for labelling AI-generated content, so people can tell what’s real. It also talks about protecting privacy, preventing AI from being used to discriminate in areas like housing and jobs, and supporting workers whose jobs might be affected. Some people think it goes too far and could slow down innovation, while others say an executive order can only do so much, and that proper laws from Congress are what’s really needed. As someone who works in tech and cares a lot about privacy, I’m glad to see governments taking this seriously, but I’ll be watching closely to make sure the rules end up protecting ordinary people, and don’t just entrench the biggest companies. 🤖⚖️
