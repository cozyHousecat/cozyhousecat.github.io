Round Two

2023-05-14cozyHousecat

Turkey voted today in one of its most important elections in decades, and it’s heading for a run-off on 28 May. President Recep Tayyip Erdoğan, who has led the country for 20 years, is ahead but looks set to fall just short of the 50% he needed to win outright, with opposition leader Kemal Kılıçdaroğlu not far behind. Turnout was huge, and the vote comes only three months after the devastating earthquakes, which raised big questions about how the government responded and how buildings were constructed. Erdoğan’s supporters see him as a strong leader who has made Turkey more powerful, while his critics say he’s become increasingly authoritarian and has mismanaged the economy, with inflation sky-high. Whatever happens, the result will matter far beyond Turkey, from NATO to the war in Ukraine.
