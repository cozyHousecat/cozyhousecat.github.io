At 8pm tonight, nurses in England began another strike, after rejecting the government’s latest pay offer, and for the first time it includes staff in A&E and intensive care, with some exemptions agreed to keep patients safe. It was meant to be longer, but a High Court ruling last week said part of it wasn’t covered by their strike ballot, so it now finishes tomorrow night. The government says the offer was fair and that it can’t afford more without fuelling inflation, while the union says nurses are still worse off than they were years ago and are leaving the profession. It’s a really difficult situation, and I can only imagine how hard it is for nurses to walk away from their patients, even for a day. I hope everyone who needs care tonight gets it, and that a fair deal is reached soon.
