So we have a new Prime Minister. I said on Friday that Monday would tell us, and Monday has duly told us.

I'm not going to do the politics.

Not because I haven't got opinions. I have a full set and they're available on request. It's because this blog is three weeks old, I'd rather it didn't turn into that, and because everybody else on the internet has today thoroughly covered at considerable length and increasing volume.

What I care about is October.

The cap lands on the first. Three thousand five hundred and forty nine pounds. That number has been sitting in my calendar since the twenty sixth of August doing nothing whatsoever except getting closer.

Word is there's a support package coming within days. Might be enormous. Might be nothing much. Everybody has a theory, and everybody had a theory about the cap too, and every single one of those theories turned out to be wrong in both directions at once, so I have formally retired from theorising.

In the meantime the status page can now tell this house whether the internet is working, which is at least one question with a definite answer in it today.

Autumn's properly here. The sun's gone before eight now, which it wasn't a fortnight ago. The wasps have gone quiet in a way I don't entirely trust.

I'll find out about October when everybody else does.
