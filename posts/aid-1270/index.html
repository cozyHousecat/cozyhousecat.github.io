Headingley

2023-07-09cozyHousecat

England won the third Ashes Test at Headingley today, chasing 251 to win by three wickets and keeping the series alive at 2–1. Harry Brook’s 75 got them most of the way there, and Chris Woakes and Mark Wood saw them home, with my heart in my mouth for most of the afternoon. After all the anger over the Bairstow stumping at Lord’s, it felt like a proper fightback, and the Headingley crowd was absolutely buzzing. I’ve gone from someone who only vaguely follows cricket to someone shouting at the TV over every single ball, which tells you how gripping this series has been. In other news, Joe Biden has arrived in London tonight, ahead of meeting Sunak and the King tomorrow and then heading to the NATO summit in Lithuania.
