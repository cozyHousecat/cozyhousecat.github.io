My birthday landed on Mother's Day this year, which made for a really lovely, full sort of day, celebrating with the people I love and making sure my mum got plenty of love too. I've always found birthdays a nice moment to look back, and this past year has been a big one, with passing my A+, starting this blog's daily rhythm, building and launching admsn, and learning a lot about myself along the way. It hasn't all been easy, and there were stretches where my anxiety made even small things feel enormous, but I'm proud of how I've kept going and kept being kind to myself. Looking ahead, I want to keep learning, work towards my next certification, give admsn the love it deserves, and make more space for the people and moments that really matter. Thank you to everyone who sent birthday wishes today, because they genuinely made my heart so full. 💛🎂
