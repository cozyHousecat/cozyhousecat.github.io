Buckle Up

2023-01-20cozyHousecat

Rishi Sunak has been fined by Lancashire Police for not wearing a seatbelt while filming a social media video in the back of a moving car, his second police fine after Partygate. He’s apologised and called it a brief error of judgement, which is fair enough, but it’s hard to believe nobody in that car thought to mention it while the camera was rolling. In tech, Google’s parent company announced it’s cutting 12,000 jobs, two days after Microsoft’s 10,000, so this is turning into a really grim month for people in the industry. At a big meeting of Ukraine’s allies in Germany, there was still no agreement on sending German-made Leopard tanks, even after the UK committed Challenger 2s last weekend, which must be frustrating for Ukraine given everything this week has shown. So it’s a Friday that ends with a reminder to buckle up, back up your CV, and hope the tanks arrive soon.
