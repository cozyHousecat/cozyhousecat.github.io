I haven't got anything to add to today and I'm not going to pretend otherwise.

Everything's cancelled. The strike that was meant to happen today was called off. The football's gone for the weekend. The telly has been rearranged and everybody on it is speaking slower than usual.

I went out at lunch because I couldn't sit still. There was a sign in a shop window, A4, in a plastic wallet, wording slightly off in the way things are when somebody has written it themselves rather than copied it from anywhere. There were a lot of those about.

Bought some milk. The woman on the till said "strange day". I said "it is, isn't it". Neither of us said anything else, and that turned out to be exactly the right amount of talking.

That's all I've got.

Back to normal service whenever normal service turns up.
