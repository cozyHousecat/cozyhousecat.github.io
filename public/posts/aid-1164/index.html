What a May

2023-05-26cozyHousecat

As we head into the last bank holiday weekend of May, I’ve been thinking about what a packed month it’s been. We had local elections, a coronation, Eurovision in Liverpool, Zelensky touring Europe and flying to Hiroshima, Manchester City winning the league, and the loss of the brilliant Tina Turner. Behind the headlines, food prices are still high, strikes are rumbling on, and the war in Ukraine shows no sign of ending, with everyone waiting to see when Ukraine’s counteroffensive will begin. It’s strange how the news can be full of celebrations and worries at the same time, and how much of life just carries on around it. This weekend I’m planning sunshine, barbecues, and as little news as I can manage.
