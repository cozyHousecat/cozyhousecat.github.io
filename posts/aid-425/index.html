Pivot Revival

2022-10-09 1 min read (130 words)

Since I passed my first major IT Certification (IT Fundamentals+) which arguably isn’t a major certification but it is the first real certification in IT that I’ve obtained and feels like a massive validation of skills that I’ve had for years, it has given be a huge boost in confidence. Sure, the exam itself is a pre-entry level certification and was piss easy (I did it in less than twenty minutes) but it is a starting point.

It has made be reevaluate by current position in a lowly customer services department for an outgoing energy brand, is it time to move on? And by moving on I mean take six months out to do nothing but dedicate time mashing through the massive A+ certification.

Is it time? Maybe it is.
