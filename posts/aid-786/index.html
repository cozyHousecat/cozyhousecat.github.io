Ninety Hours

2023-02-10cozyHousecat

Four days after the earthquakes, more than 20,000 people are known to have died, but there are still astonishing rescues, including a newborn baby and her mother pulled out alive after around 90 hours under the rubble. There’s also growing anger in Turkey about why so many newer buildings collapsed, and whether building rules were ignored, along with frustration in Syria that help took so long to arrive. Here in the UK, the DEC appeal raised more than £30 million in its first day, which shows how much people want to help even when money is tight for everyone. It’s been an awful week, but the rescuers, volunteers and ordinary donors have shown the best of people. I’m ending this Friday grateful for my warm home and thinking of everyone who doesn’t have one tonight.
