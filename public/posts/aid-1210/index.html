Going Dark

2023-06-12cozyHousecat

Thousands of Reddit communities went dark today in protest at Reddit’s plan to start charging huge fees for access to its API, which would kill off popular third-party apps like Apollo and tools that volunteer moderators rely on. Many of the biggest subreddits are now private, so large parts of the site have effectively disappeared, and it’s a reminder that so much of Reddit’s value comes from unpaid volunteers. Reddit says it needs to make money and stop AI companies scraping its content for free, while users say it’s turning its back on the community that built it. After watching the same thing happen with Twitter’s API, it’s another example of why relying on one company’s platform is risky, and why open, community-run alternatives matter.
