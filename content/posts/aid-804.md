Tonight, thousands of people gathered at vigils across the country to remember Brianna Ghey, including in Warrington, close to where she lived. Two 15-year-olds have now been charged with her murder, and the police say they’re still looking at every possible motive. The pictures of candles, flowers and trans flags held up in the dark were incredibly moving, and showed how many people, many of whom never met her, felt they had to be there. Her family asked everyone to remember her as the happy, funny, fearless girl she was, and I think that’s what tonight was really about. I hope the trans young people who were there, and everyone who’s frightened right now, saw how many people are standing with them.
