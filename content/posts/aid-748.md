January is nearly over, and I swear it’s lasted about three months. In the space of a few weeks we’ve had floods and frost, NHS strikes and tech layoffs, a minister sacked, tanks for Ukraine, record-breaking memoirs, and far too many tragedies around the world. It’s been hard to feel like the new year was a fresh start when most of last year’s problems just carried on as if nothing had changed. Still, the evenings are getting a little lighter, the birds are back in the garden, and payday isn’t far off for a lot of people. February, I’m ready for you to be a bit kinder.
