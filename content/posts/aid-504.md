Half my timeline is posting their Mastodon handles tonight “just in case,” and after today I completely get it, because it’s a strange feeling watching somewhere you’ve spent years posting get bought by one person and changed overnight. The idea behind Mastodon is something I really like: no single owner, lots of servers run by ordinary people, and your posts not depending on one billionaire’s mood. The catch is that everyone you actually talk to is still on Twitter, and that’s always been the hard part of leaving anywhere. Looking back over the last two weeks, we’ve had a budget torn up, a Home Secretary resign and come back, a PM outlasted by a lettuce, a new PM nobody voted for, and now this, so I’m very ready for the clocks going back on Sunday. I’ll take the extra hour, put the kettle on, and maybe finally pick a Mastodon server.
