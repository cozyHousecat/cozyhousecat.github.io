Ciudad Juárez

2023-03-28cozyHousecat

Dozens of migrants were killed in a fire at an immigration detention centre in Ciudad Juárez, in Mexico, right on the border with the US. Footage appears to show guards walking away while men were still locked behind bars as smoke filled the room, and the Mexican authorities have opened a criminal investigation. Most of the people who died had travelled from Central and South America, hoping to reach the United States, and ended up dying in a locked cell. After all the debate here this month about small boats, it’s another reminder that behind every argument about migration are people who just wanted a safer life. Tonight I’m thinking of their families, many of whom may not even know yet.
