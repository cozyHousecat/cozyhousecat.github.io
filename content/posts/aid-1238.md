Something very strange is happening in Russia tonight. Yevgeny Prigozhin, the head of the Wagner mercenary group that has been fighting for Russia in Ukraine, has accused the Russian army of attacking his fighters and has vowed to go after the military leadership, and Russia’s security service has opened a criminal case against him for calling for armed mutiny. Nobody seems to know exactly what’s going on or what he plans to do next, and there are reports of Wagner fighters moving towards the Russian border. It’s extraordinary to see one of Putin’s own allies openly turning on the military in the middle of a war. I’m going to bed not knowing what I’ll wake up to.
