Tonight is Lunar New Year’s Eve, and tomorrow the Year of the Rabbit begins, which is supposed to be a year of calm, gentleness and hope. In China, it’s the first new year in three years without Covid restrictions, so hundreds of millions of people are travelling home to see family, many for the first time since the pandemic began. After everything that happened there in the autumn, from lockdown protests to blank sheets of paper, I really hope these reunions are as joyful as they deserve to be. Communities here are celebrating too, with lanterns, dumplings and dragon dances getting ready across the country. After the year we’ve had, a gentle Year of the Rabbit sounds pretty perfect to me, so happy Lunar New Year to everyone celebrating.
