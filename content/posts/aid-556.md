Zelensky made a surprise visit to Kherson today, three days after it was liberated, and called it “the beginning of the end of the war.” I really hope he’s right, but there are also reports of war crimes there now that people can finally get in and see what happened. In Bali, Biden and Xi met in person as leaders for the first time, ahead of a G20 summit where Russia’s war is going to hang over everything. Sunak is there too for his first big summit, telling reporters on the flight that small boats and the economy are his top priorities. It’s been strange watching the world’s most powerful people meet on a beach while Kherson counts what it’s lost, but today does feel like things might be moving, maybe in the right direction for once.
