Serious Breach

2023-01-29cozyHousecat

Rishi Sunak sacked Nadhim Zahawi as Conservative Party chairman this morning, after the PM’s ethics adviser found a “serious breach” of the ministerial code. The investigation found Zahawi hadn’t properly declared that he was being investigated by HMRC over his tax affairs when he was appointed to senior jobs, including as Chancellor. In his response, Zahawi focused on his record in government rather than apologising, while critics said the bigger question is why it took weeks of headlines for anything to happen. To be fair to Sunak, he did act once the report came back, which is more than some previous Prime Ministers would have done. It feels like a real test of whether “integrity, professionalism and accountability” means something, and today at least it seems to have meant something.
