He Said He Won’t Go

2023-08-25cozyHousecat

I genuinely thought Luis Rubiales would resign today, but he stood up at an emergency meeting of the Spanish FA and repeated “I will not resign” over and over, insisting the kiss on Jenni Hermoso was mutual and blaming the backlash on “false feminism”, while a room full of people clapped. This evening Hermoso put out a statement saying plainly that she didn’t consent and felt vulnerable and like the victim of an assault, and she shouldn’t have had to say that publicly just to be believed. Then came the bit that gave me goosebumps: the whole World Cup-winning squad, plus dozens of other players, 81 in total, have said they won’t play for Spain while he’s in charge, and some of the men’s players have come out in support too. It’s been a week since they won the biggest prize in football, and they’re still having to fight for basic respect, which is exhausting even to watch from here. Solidarity to all of them, and I hope this ends with the right person leaving. 💛
