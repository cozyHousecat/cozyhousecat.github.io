Tuesday

2022-09-20 1 min read (166 words)

Everything opened again this morning and nobody said anything about it.

That’s the bit I keep turning over. There was no announcement, no moment where anybody declared the thing concluded. It simply became Tuesday overnight and the entire country agreed to get on with it, and the buses ran, and the bloke who does the fruit outside the Co-op was back exactly where he always is.

Eleven days of enormous slow television, and then a Tuesday.

I got a delivery that should have turned up last week. I did a shop. I answered emails that had been sat there since Thursday, with everybody being extremely understanding about the delay in a way that absolutely nobody will be next week.

Being back doesn’t feel wrong at all, it’s just that the switch was so quiet, and I’d half expected to feel something about it, and what I mostly feel is like a woman who needs to do some washing.

Bins tomorrow. Genuinely tomorrow.

I have checked twice.
