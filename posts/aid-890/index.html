Greece Stops

2023-03-08cozyHousecat

Greece came to a standstill today, with a general strike and huge protests over last week’s Tempi train crash, which killed 57 people. Trains, ferries and much of public transport stopped, and tens of thousands of people filled the streets of Athens and other cities, many of them students and young people. The crash has become a symbol of something bigger: years of underinvestment, broken promises and safety systems that railway workers say they warned about again and again. Most of the protests were peaceful and full of grief, though there were clashes with police in Athens later in the day. It’s clear that people won’t let this be forgotten until there are real answers and real changes.
