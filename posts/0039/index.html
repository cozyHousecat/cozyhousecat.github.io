Bank Holiday weekend

2022-08-27 1 min read (205 words)

Bank holiday weekend, so three whole days in which to not think about it, and I fully intended to use all of them.

Made it to about half ten.

It isn’t dread, It’s arithmetic. You catch yourself doing sums you never sat down to do, in the queue, filling the kettle, halfway up the stairs, and then you put them away again and they come back later having brought friends.

Right, enough of that.

The wasps have escalated. One got into the kitchen this afternoon and conducted itself like something with a grievance and a list. I did the thing where you open every window in the room and then stand very still in the corner, narrating your own reasonableness at it in a low voice.

It left of its own accord in the end, having made its point. I’m still not clear what the point was.

Shops were heaving. Everybody buying the same disposable barbecue and the same four bags of ice with the confidence of people who have not checked the forecast and would prefer not to be told.

Bin day’s shifted because of Monday. Posties out again Wednesday.

That’s the shape of the week ahead and, all things considered, I’ll take it.
