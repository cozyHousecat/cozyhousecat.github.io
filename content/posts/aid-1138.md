At the G7 today, Biden told allies the US will support training Ukrainian pilots to fly American-made F-16 fighter jets, which opens the door to Ukraine eventually getting them. For months, the US was reluctant, worrying it could escalate the war, so this is a big change, and it’s exactly what Zelensky has been asking for since he handed Parliament that pilot’s helmet in February. The UK and the Netherlands have been leading the effort to build a “jet coalition,” so it’s good to see the US come on board. It’ll still be months before any Ukrainian pilots are ready to fly them in combat, but it’s a clear message to Russia that support isn’t fading. With Zelensky flying to Hiroshima in person this weekend, it feels like a significant moment in the war.
