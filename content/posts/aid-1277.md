India launched its Chandrayaan-3 mission today, aiming to land near the Moon’s south pole next month, somewhere no country has ever landed before. The pictures of the rocket lifting off were incredible, and it’d be a huge achievement if they pull it off after their last attempt crashed in 2019. Meanwhile at Wimbledon, Novak Djokovic and Carlos Alcaraz both won their semi-finals, setting up the final everyone wanted on Sunday, the seven-time champion against the 20-year-old star. It’s Bastille Day in France too, so bonne fête to everyone celebrating, especially after a difficult few weeks there. I’m ending the week with some Rocket League and a takeaway, and very much looking forward to Sunday.
