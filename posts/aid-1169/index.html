Five More Years

2023-05-28cozyHousecat

Recep Tayyip Erdoğan has won Turkey’s presidential run-off with around 52% of the vote, extending his 20 years in power for another five. His supporters were celebrating in the streets tonight, seeing him as a strong leader who has made Turkey more confident on the world stage, while the opposition says the election was unfair because Erdoğan dominated the media and used state resources to his advantage. He faces huge challenges, including very high inflation, the cost of rebuilding after February’s earthquakes, and millions of refugees from Syria. His win also matters well beyond Turkey, especially for NATO, since he’s still blocking Sweden from joining, and for the war in Ukraine, where Turkey has tried to stay on good terms with both sides. On the last day of the football season, with Leicester and Leeds relegated, it felt like a day where a lot of people were watching their screens, waiting to find out their fate.
