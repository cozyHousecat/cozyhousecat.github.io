Group Stage Goodbye

2022-12-02cozyHousecat

The last day of the group stage was absolute chaos. Cameroon beat Brazil 1–0 with an injury-time header from Vincent Aboubakar, who then got sent off for taking his shirt off to celebrate, which might be the most joyful red card I’ve ever seen. South Korea scored a late winner against Portugal to go through, which knocked out Uruguay even though they beat Ghana 2–0, because South Korea had scored more goals, and the Uruguay players ended up surrounding the referee at the end. Brazil and Portugal still went through, so the big names survived, but the smaller teams made them sweat. The knockouts start tomorrow, and England play Senegal on Sunday, so at least I’ll have one good thing to look forward to this weekend.
