The Fog of War, Online

2023-10-08cozyHousecat

It’s been another awful day of news, with Israel’s government formally approving a state of war, the number of people killed in Israel rising to at least six hundred, and more than four hundred people reported killed in Gaza by Israeli strikes, according to the health ministry there. Families in Israel are still desperately searching for loved ones who are missing or were taken hostage, while people in Gaza are bracing for what comes next, with very few places to go. On top of everything, my social media feeds have been flooded with misinformation, with old videos from other conflicts and even clips from video games being shared as if they’re from this weekend, often by accounts with blue ticks and huge followings. As someone who works in IT and cares a lot about trustworthy information, it’s frustrating to watch, because in a situation this painful, fake content just adds more fear and anger to people who are already hurting. So I’m sticking to reputable news outlets, checking things before I share them, and trying to keep my heart open to every innocent person caught up in this. 🕊️
