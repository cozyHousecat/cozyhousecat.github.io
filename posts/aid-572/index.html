Kick-Off Eve

2022-11-19cozyHousecat

The World Cup starts tomorrow, and for the first time I honestly don’t know how I feel about watching. I love the shared buzz of a tournament, with everyone getting behind England, but there are migrant workers who died building these stadiums, and LGBTQ+ fans being told to be “respectful” in a country where who they are is illegal. Today showed the whole range: a Prime Minister in a snowy warzone promising air defences, a man dying after being held in a centre we’ve known was overcrowded for weeks, climate talks running late, and a billionaire deciding whether to unban a former president with a Twitter poll. I think I’ll watch, but I’ll keep paying attention to the people who don’t get a camera on them. Tonight I’m just grateful for a warm house, the people I love, and the fact that tomorrow is Sunday.
