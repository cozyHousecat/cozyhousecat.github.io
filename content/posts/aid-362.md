Spent today restoring from a backup, on purpose, as a test, and found out inside twenty minutes that I could not have done it for real.

The backups are encrypted. The passphrase is in my password manager. My password manager lives on the machines I was pretending had just burned to the ground.

Two entirely sensible decisions, made about two years apart, which combine into something that doesn't work. I have known both halves of that for ages and had genuinely never once put them next to each other.

So if you've got backups and you have never restored from them, and particularly if they're encrypted, go and check where the key lives. That's the whole post, really. It took me twenty minutes to find and I have been quietly smug about those backups since roughly February.

The rest of the day worked, once I'd sorted it. Took four hours rather than the forty five minutes I'd assumed it would, which is its own extremely useful thing to know about and which I had never bothered to measure because I'd never actually done it.

Bins Wednesday. Backups apparently fine.

Me, less so.
