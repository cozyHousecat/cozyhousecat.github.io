Bolsonaro finally spoke tonight after two days of silence, and he still didn’t actually say he’d lost, though his team says the handover can go ahead. Meanwhile Musk has decided the blue tick will cost $8 a month, calling the old system “lords and peasants” after Stephen King complained about the rumoured $20. I get the argument, but to me verification was never a status symbol, it was a way to know an account was actually who it claimed to be. Once anyone can buy one, I don’t see how that holds up, especially with the US midterms next week and people impersonating politicians and companies for a laugh. So day one of November: a former Health Secretary off to eat bugs on TV, a president who won’t quite say the word “lost,” and Twitter putting a price on trust, which is not the boring I asked for.
