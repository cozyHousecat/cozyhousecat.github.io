He Should Never Have Been an Officer

2023-01-16cozyHousecat

Content note: this post mentions sexual violence.

Today a serving Metropolitan Police officer, David Carrick, admitted 49 offences against 12 women over nearly two decades, including 24 counts of rape, using his job to make them afraid that nobody would believe them. The Met has admitted he came to the attention of police nine times before he was finally arrested in 2021, including allegations of rape and domestic abuse, and nothing was done, and it has publicly apologised. He served in the same armed unit as Sarah Everard’s murderer, which makes it even harder to take. The women who came forward did something incredibly brave, and it’s heartbreaking that the people meant to protect them failed so badly for so long. Real change has to start with listening to women the first time, and if this case has brought up anything difficult for you, organisations like Rape Crisis are there to help.
