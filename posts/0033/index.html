Strike day

2022-08-20 2 min read (327 words)

Strike day, so no trains, so I have spent Saturday entirely within walking distance of my own front door.

I want to be clear that I support them completely and also that I had plans.

It’s the signallers that do it. Network Rail are out alongside most of the operators, and once the signallers go it stops mattering who else fancied coming in, the whole thing simply stops working. Thursday they managed about a fifth of the network. Today felt like rather less than that, though I’m going off the app and a general atmosphere rather than anything rigorous.

The one nice part, and I wouldn’t say this to anybody actually striking, is that it’s quiet out there in a way a Saturday never usually is. No day trippers. Everyone who did make it out was on foot and had visibly decided to be cheerful about it, which I found unexpectedly nice to be around.

Ofgem announce the new cap on Friday. Everybody has a number they’re passing about, all of them different, all of them somehow worse than the last one you heard. Nobody actually knows yet, which has not slowed anyone down in the slightest. I’ve decided not to look at any of the predictions, on the grounds that I’ll find out on Friday either way and there is nothing useful to be done with six days of advance dread except sit and have it.

I have of course looked at all of them.

No sign of Helios either, incidentally. I said last time he was due back this week and he has simply not turned up, which after all that fuss I am choosing to take personally.

So it’s been an indoors day, doing the category of job you only ever get to when you can’t leave the house. The sort where you finish, and the room looks precisely the same as it did before, and you’re somehow tired.

Anyway. Trains on Monday, allegedly.
