In the early hours of this morning, three people were killed in a series of attacks in Nottingham: two university students walking home after a night out, and a school caretaker, while three other people were injured when a van was driven at them. A man has been arrested, and police are still trying to piece together exactly what happened and why. The students had just finished their first year, and the caretaker was nearing retirement, which makes it all the more heartbreaking. Tonight the city held vigils, with thousands of students and residents gathering to remember them. There’s not much more to say tonight, other than that my thoughts are with their families, their friends and everyone in Nottingham.
