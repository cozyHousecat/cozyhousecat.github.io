Bring On France

2022-12-04cozyHousecat

England beat Senegal 3–0 tonight with goals from Henderson, Kane and Saka, and Kane’s was his first of the tournament, taking him to 52 for England, just one behind Rooney’s record. The day started on a worrying note, though, with news that Raheem Sterling had flown home to be with his family after an incident at his house. However much we all love the World Cup, family comes first, and I hope they’re all okay. Earlier, France beat Poland 3–1, with Mbappé scoring twice and Olivier Giroud becoming France’s all-time record goalscorer, overtaking Thierry Henry. That sets up England against France in the quarter-final on Saturday, which is huge, and I’m already nervous about it.
