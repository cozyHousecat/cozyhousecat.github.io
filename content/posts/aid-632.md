Argentina beat Croatia 3–0 tonight to reach the World Cup final, and Messi was magical. He scored a penalty that made him Argentina’s all-time top World Cup scorer, then set up Julián Álvarez’s second goal by spinning one of the best defenders in the tournament around and around before squaring it. Croatia had been so tough to beat all tournament, but tonight they couldn’t get close. It’s very likely Messi’s last World Cup, and after losing the 2014 final, he gets one more chance on Sunday at the only trophy he hasn’t won. I’m not Argentinian, but I’ll be cheering for him.
