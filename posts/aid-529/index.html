Failed at Every Stage

2022-11-04cozyHousecat

The Manchester Arena inquiry’s report on the emergency response came out yesterday, and it’s been on my mind all day. It found serious failings in how the emergency services responded that night, with firefighters not arriving for around two hours and the response not working together the way it should have. Reading the families describe how they were let down is hard, especially when they’ve already waited over five years for answers. Twitter layoffs and interest rates feel very small next to that. For a city that pulled together so strongly afterwards, I hope this leads to real changes so no other families go through it again.
