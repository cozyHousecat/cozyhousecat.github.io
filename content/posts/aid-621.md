England are out, losing 2–1 to France in the quarter-final, and it really hurts because we were good tonight. Kane scored a penalty to equalise and match Rooney’s record of 53 England goals, but after Giroud put France back ahead, Kane got a second penalty and blasted it over the bar with minutes to go. I can’t imagine how he feels, and I hope people go easy on him, because he’s carried this team for years. The young players like Bellingham, Saka and Foden gave me a lot of hope for the next tournament, so it doesn’t feel like the end of something, more like it’s nearly there. For now I’m putting the kettle on and pretending I’m not gutted.
