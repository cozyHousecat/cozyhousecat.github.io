Seventy-One Nil

2023-09-23cozyHousecat

England beat Chile 71–0 in Lille this afternoon, and young winger Henry Arundell scored five tries all by himself, which I think might be more tries than I’ve ever scored in anything. It was a pretty one-sided game, but Chile are playing in their very first Rugby World Cup and they never stopped trying, which I have a lot of respect for. It’s been lovely watching England go from a team nobody expected much from in August to three wins out of three, with the knockout stages now very much in sight. It’s been ages since sport made me feel this hopeful, even if I still need my partner to explain what a ruck is every single match. I spent the rest of the day on the sofa with a cuppa and some Rocket League, which is about as perfect a Saturday as you can get. 🏉🌹
