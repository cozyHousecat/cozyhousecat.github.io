After yesterday’s anniversary, today has felt quieter, but this week has been a lot: Putin and Biden giving opposite speeches, China proposing its own peace plan, a big UN vote, salad shortages and a government minister telling us to eat turnips. It’s funny how the news can jump from nuclear treaties to empty vegetable aisles in the same breath, and how both can feel worrying in their own way. I spent the afternoon cooking a big pot of soup with whatever vegetables I could find (yes, including a turnip), and it was surprisingly good. There’s talk of a big Northern Ireland deal being announced on Monday, so next week might be busy too. For now, I’m enjoying a slow, warm Saturday night.
