Spent most of today with the news open in one tab and the Daily Star's lettuce livestream in the other, which feels like a completely normal way to follow British politics in 2022. The new Chancellor has basically binned the entire mini-budget three weeks after it was announced, and the energy price guarantee that was meant to last two years now ends in April, just as it's starting to get properly cold. Then Penny Mordaunt had to stand up in the Commons and reassure everyone that the Prime Minister wasn't hiding under a desk, which is not a sentence I ever expected to type. Honestly, I'm not sure who I'd bet on at this point, the PM or the lettuce. Anyway, I'm putting the heating on for an hour and pretending none of this is happening.
