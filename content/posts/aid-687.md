Tonight, thousands of Bolsonaro supporters stormed Brazil’s Congress, Supreme Court and presidential palace in Brasília, smashing windows, wrecking offices and refusing to accept that Lula won the election. Two days after the second anniversary of the attack on the US Capitol, the pictures look horribly familiar. Police have taken back the buildings and arrested hundreds of people, and Lula, who was away from the capital, has put the federal government in charge of security there. Watching it, I keep thinking about how fragile democracy is when people are told over and over that an election they lost was stolen. I hope the people responsible are held to account and that Brazil can calm down from here.
