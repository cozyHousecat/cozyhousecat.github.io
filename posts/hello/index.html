Hello

2022-08-16cozyHousecat

After walking nearly three miles into Chorlton during the hottest heatwave of the year, losing ninety-percent of my body’s mass through sweat, I got most of it back by getting drenched in a monsoon-like deluge on the way back.

And with that intro out of the way, now I’m launching my dailyblog. Yes, a dailyblog. How very early 2000s of me.

Since I’m cheap, I’m hosting it on GitHub.io, but its also because I’m not brave enough to publish it on any of the pile of the domains I own, call me an extroverted-introvert if you want.

So, grab your alpaca and flake your almonds, welcome to the world of cozyHousecat.

Time for an afternoon nap.
