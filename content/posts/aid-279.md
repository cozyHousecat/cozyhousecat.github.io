September, then, and the verdict is in. Joint warmest summer on record for England. Seventeen point one degrees, level with 2018.

Which is an odd thing to read about a season you spent complaining your way through. All that moaning, and it turns out I was present at something.

So he wasn't messing about after all. I owe Helios an apology, or at minimum an acknowledgement, which I'm perfectly willing to offer now that he's gone.

New Prime Minister on Monday. Whoever it turns out to be inherits October, which is four weeks out now and has a number attached to it.

The Treasury are apparently already drawing something up regardless, a mini budget within the fortnight. So the number might not end up being the number. I'm not going to get excited about that, having recently learned a certain amount about numbers, but I notice that I would quite like to.

It's properly autumn out there today. Not cold. Just that light.

Anyway. Monday.
