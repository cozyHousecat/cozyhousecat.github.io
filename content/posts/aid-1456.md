I've spent most of today with the news on, feeling sick, after Hamas launched a surprise attack on southern Israel from Gaza early this morning, firing thousands of rockets while gunmen crossed the border into towns, villages and a music festival. By this evening, Israeli officials were saying at least 250 people had been killed, many of them civilians, and that people, including women, children and older people, had been taken hostage into Gaza, which is just horrifying to think about. Prime Minister Netanyahu has said Israel is "at war", and Israeli air strikes on Gaza have already killed more than two hundred people there, according to Gaza's health ministry, with fears of much worse to come for the two million people living in that tiny, crowded strip. I don't feel qualified to say much about the politics tonight, and I know it's a subject people feel incredibly strongly about, but I keep coming back to the fact that ordinary people on both sides of that border are terrified and grieving tonight. I'm holding everyone affected in my heart, and hoping above all for the hostages to come home safely, and for innocent lives to be protected. 🕊️
