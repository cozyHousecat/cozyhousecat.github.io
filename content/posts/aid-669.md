Even on New Year’s Eve, Russia fired missiles at Kyiv, so Ukrainians are seeing in 2023 with air raid sirens instead of fireworks, and they’re the first people I’m thinking about tonight. Here, London’s fireworks are about to start, including a tribute to the Queen, and it’s going to feel very strange seeing in a new year without her. I’ve written something here almost every day since August, and looking back, it’s turned into a diary of one of the wildest stretches of news I can remember, from lettuces and blue ticks to blank paper and a World Cup final. Thank you to anyone who’s read along, whether you’ve been here since “Hello” or just found me tonight. Happy New Year, I hope 2023 is gentler, kinder and a lot more boring, in the best possible way.
