Game Over?

2023-04-26cozyHousecat

The UK’s competition regulator has blocked Microsoft’s $69 billion takeover of Activision Blizzard, the company behind Call of Duty, saying it could give Microsoft too much power over the fast-growing cloud gaming market. Microsoft and Activision are furious and say they’ll appeal, arguing it sends a message that the UK is a bad place to do business, while others point out that the regulator is doing exactly what it’s supposed to do. It’s interesting that the UK has acted before the EU and the US, which are still making their own decisions. As someone who cares about the internet not being dominated by a handful of tech giants, I’m glad regulators are taking a hard look at deals this big. As a gamer, I mostly just want to be able to play the games I like, on whatever device I own, without being locked into one company’s ecosystem.
