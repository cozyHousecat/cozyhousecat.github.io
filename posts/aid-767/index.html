Saturday, Summed Up

2023-02-04cozyHousecat

What a strange Saturday for the news. It started with a train full of chemicals on fire in a small Ohio town and ended with a fighter jet shooting a Chinese balloon out of the sky, with a thrilling Calcutta Cup in between. The balloon story has been oddly gripping, because it’s so visible: people across the US were filming it from their gardens, and now the two biggest powers in the world are arguing over something that looked a bit like a lost party decoration. It’s also World Cancer Day, which put things in perspective, because so many of us have someone in our lives who has been through it, is going through it now, or didn’t make it. It’s a good reminder to check in on people, go to the screenings when the letters come through, and support the researchers and nurses who make such a difference. So tonight I’m putting the phone down, sending a few messages to people I care about, and calling it a day.
