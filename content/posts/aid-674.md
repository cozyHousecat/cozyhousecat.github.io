Over in Washington, the new US House of Representatives tried to elect a Speaker today and failed three times in a row, the first time in a hundred years that’s taken more than one vote. Republicans won the House in November, but about twenty of their own members refused to back their leader, Kevin McCarthy, so no one reached the 218 votes needed. Until someone is chosen, the House can’t swear in its members or do any business at all, so they’ll keep voting tomorrow. After the autumn we had here, it’s oddly comforting to see that other countries’ politics can get stuck too. Hopefully they sort it out without needing a lettuce.
