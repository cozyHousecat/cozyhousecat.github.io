Tonight the BBC says one of its journalists, Ed Lawrence, was arrested, handcuffed, and beaten and kicked by police while covering the protests in Shanghai, before being released after several hours. There’s footage of him being dragged away and shouting for someone to “call the consulate now,” and the BBC has rejected China’s explanation as not credible. If they’ll do that to an accredited foreign journalist on camera, I don’t want to imagine what happens to the ordinary people holding up those blank sheets of paper. It makes me grateful for the boring freedoms I take for granted, like writing this blog, criticising the government, and posting what I think without being afraid of a knock at the door. Today has been football, celebrations and upsets, but the image I’ll remember is a room full of people holding up empty paper because there’s nothing they’re allowed to say.
