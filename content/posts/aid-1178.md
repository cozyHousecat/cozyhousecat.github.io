Hundreds of AI researchers and tech leaders, including the bosses of OpenAI and Google DeepMind and pioneers like Geoffrey Hinton, signed a one-sentence statement today saying that reducing the risk of extinction from AI should be a global priority, alongside pandemics and nuclear war. It’s a startling thing to read, especially when it comes from the very people building these systems. Some experts think it’s a genuine warning we need to take seriously, while others say focusing on far-off doomsday scenarios distracts from the harms AI is already causing, like bias, misinformation and job losses. As someone who works in IT, I think both conversations matter, the immediate problems and the long-term ones. It’s been an extraordinary few months for AI, and today felt like the moment the people at the top admitted they’re worried too.
