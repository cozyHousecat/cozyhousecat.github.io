Heat broke

2022-08-19 2 min read (317 words)

The heat broke on Sunday and I’ve been in a funny mood about it ever since.

I spent most of last week complaining loudly to anybody who would stand still for it that it was too hot to function. Then Helios packed it in, wandered off to bother somebody else, and now it’s nineteen degrees and grey and I find that I miss him. Not enough to want him back but still enough to be annoyed about it.

Monday through Wednesday it hammered it down, which the ground was in no fit state to deal with. That rain that just sits on top of the soil looking at you whereas down south they got proper flooding out of it, sewage on the beaches, warnings, the lot. We got puddles and a smell.

Meanwhile eight parts of England are now officially in drought and we, up here, are not. No hosepipe ban either. I have never in my life been so proud of something I had absolutely no involvement in.

Although.

The Pennine reservoirs are sitting at about half full when they’d usually be three quarters, and Dovestone currently looks like somebody’s pulled the plug out of it. So I think what’s actually happened is that we are in a drought and simply haven’t been informed on the grounds that half the region drinks Lake District rain and the Lake District has never had a dry week in its entire life.

I went up to have a look at our reservoir on Wednesday. In the rain. Like a normal person.

Still working out what this blog is for, incidentally. I had assumed I’d have a theme by now. Instead I have the weather, which is arguably a theme, just not a good one.

Forecast says he’s back next week. Low twenties, nothing dramatic. Which is the correct amount of sun, and therefore nobody will mention it at all.
