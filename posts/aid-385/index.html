Go and read your meter

2022-09-30 2 min read (226 words)

Quick one, because it’s time sensitive and because I’ve already sent it to about four people today.

Rates change tomorrow. If you’re on a standard tariff and you haven’t got a smart meter, go and take a reading tonight and send it to your supplier, so that everything you’ve already used gets billed at today’s number instead of being estimated across the changeover by somebody who is not especially motivated to guess in your favour.

Takes two minutes. There’s a form on your supplier’s site or app, and doing it there is much less painful than the phone, which will be busy tonight for obvious reasons.

And whether or not you manage to submit it, take a photograph of the meter with your phone. Doesn’t matter that the photo doesn’t go anywhere. In four months, if a bill turns up looking peculiar, that photo is the only thing in the world with both a number and a date on it, and a photo wins an argument that a memory absolutely will not.

Smart meter people, you’re fine, it does this by itself. Take a photo anyway if it makes you feel better. It makes me feel better.

I have done ours, in a cupboard, with a torch, looking like an absolute weirdo, and I’d do it again.

That’s the whole post. Go on. Before you forget.
