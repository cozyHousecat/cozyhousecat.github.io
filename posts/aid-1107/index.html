The Big Help Out

2023-05-08cozyHousecat

To end the coronation weekend, thousands of people took part in the Big Help Out, volunteering with local charities, cleaning up parks, helping at food banks and trying out other ways to give back. Members of the royal family joined in too, with William and Catherine helping renovate a Scout hut in Slough, and the hope is that more people will keep volunteering after today. I love that a weekend of pageantry ended with something so down to earth and community-focused. After a bank holiday weekend of crowns, concerts, cake and a lot of rain, it feels like the country has had a proper pause. Back to normal tomorrow, but with Eurovision in Liverpool this week, normal isn’t going to last long.
