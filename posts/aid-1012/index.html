Holy Week

2023-04-08cozyHousecat

It’s been a rare week where Easter, Passover and Ramadan all overlap, with millions of people around the world marking some of the most important days in their faiths at the same time. I love the idea of so many different communities gathering, fasting, feasting and reflecting on hope and renewal at once, even if they do it in very different ways. It’s also been a tense week in parts of the world, from clashes in Jerusalem to military drills around Taiwan, which makes the messages of peace at the heart of these festivals feel even more important. Whatever you believe, or don’t, I think there’s something in this season about new beginnings that most of us can connect with. Tomorrow is Easter Sunday, and I’m looking forward to a quiet day, a big meal and probably too much chocolate.
