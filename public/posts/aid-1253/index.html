The Spirit of Cricket

2023-07-02cozyHousecat

The second Ashes Test at Lord’s ended today in one of the biggest cricket rows in years, after Jonny Bairstow wandered out of his crease thinking the ball was dead and Australia’s wicketkeeper stumped him. It was completely within the rules, but the crowd was furious, the usually polite members in the Long Room booed and heckled the Australian players, and there’s now a huge argument about whether it was clever play or against the spirit of the game. Ben Stokes then smashed a brilliant 155 and almost won the match on his own, but Australia held on to win by 43 runs and lead the series 2–0. Even people who never watch cricket seem to have an opinion on it tonight, including both prime ministers. I spent most of the afternoon half-watching with Fortnite on in the other window, and I have to admit the cricket was more dramatic.
