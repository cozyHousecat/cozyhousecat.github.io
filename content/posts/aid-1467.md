Before anything else, my thoughts are with the people of northern Gaza tonight, after Israel told around a million people to move south within twenty-four hours, which the UN has warned is impossible without devastating humanitarian consequences. In very different news, after nearly two years of regulatory drama, Microsoft has finally completed its $69 billion takeover of Activision Blizzard today, just hours after the UK's competition regulator approved the restructured deal. The CMA had originally blocked it over concerns about cloud gaming, so Microsoft agreed to sell the cloud streaming rights for Activision games outside the EU to Ubisoft for fifteen years, which seems to have been enough to win approval. It means Call of Duty, World of Warcraft, Candy Crush and so many other huge games are now owned by the company behind Xbox, which is a massive shift in the gaming industry. I'm a bit torn, because Microsoft has promised to keep Call of Duty on PlayStation and Nintendo, but I always worry when a few giant companies end up owning so much of the culture we love. 🎮🏢
