Protests over the police shooting of 17-year-old Nahel in Nanterre spread across France last night, with cars and buildings set alight in several cities, and there are fears of more unrest tonight. Macron has called the shooting “inexplicable and inexcusable,” while also appealing for calm, and the police officer involved is being investigated. Closer to home, Thames Water, which supplies a quarter of England’s population, is in serious trouble, with its boss resigning yesterday and reports that the government is preparing for the possibility of taking it over temporarily because of its huge debts. For a company that’s been criticised for pumping sewage into rivers while paying out large dividends in the past, a lot of people are angry that customers could end up footing the bill. It’s been a day of difficult questions about who our institutions really serve.
