Kindness, Please

2024-03-22cozyHousecat

This evening, the Princess of Wales released a video revealing that she has been diagnosed with cancer and has started a course of preventative chemotherapy. It comes after weeks of wild online speculation and conspiracy theories about where she was, and listening to her calmly explain that she and her husband had needed time to tell their young children was a really sobering moment. Whatever anyone thinks of the royal family, I think it’s a powerful reminder that behind every headline and every trending hashtag there are real people, often dealing with things we know nothing about. The internet can be such a wonderful place, but it can also be incredibly cruel when curiosity tips into entitlement. I hope her treatment goes well, and I hope we all take a moment to think before joining the next pile-on, whoever it’s about. 💙
