Humza Yousaf has won the race to become the new leader of the SNP, beating Kate Forbes in a close final round, and he’s set to become Scotland’s next First Minister this week. He’ll be the first Muslim, and the first person from an ethnic minority background, to lead the Scottish government, and in his speech he talked about his grandparents coming to Scotland from Punjab with almost nothing. Whatever people think of his politics, that’s a meaningful moment for a lot of families who rarely see themselves at the top of government. He’s taking over a divided party after a bruising contest, with big questions about independence, the gender recognition law and how to fix struggling public services. It’s a huge job, and it starts right away.
