We went to see Indiana Jones and the Dial of Destiny today, Harrison Ford’s final outing as Indy, more than 40 years after Raiders of the Lost Ark. It’s an old-fashioned adventure in the best way, with chases, puzzles, Nazis to punch and that unmistakable John Williams theme, and Phoebe Waller-Bridge is great fun as his goddaughter. The opening sequence with a digitally de-aged Ford is a bit strange to watch at first, but it’s impressive how far that technology has come. It isn’t perfect, but it has a lot of heart, and seeing Ford put on the hat one last time at 80 was genuinely moving. I came out with the theme tune stuck in my head and an urge to rewatch the whole series.
