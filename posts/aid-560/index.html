Przewodów

2022-11-15cozyHousecat

This afternoon a missile landed in Przewodów, a small Polish village a few miles from the Ukrainian border, and killed two men at a grain dryer. Poland’s foreign ministry now says it was Russian-made, but nobody yet seems sure who fired it or why, and there’s a lot of speculation that could easily get ahead of the facts. Poland is a NATO country, so the whole evening has been emergency meetings and words like “Article 4” that I only half understood until tonight. What stays with me most is that two ordinary people died doing farm work, on a day when the rest of the news was about summits and population milestones. I’m going to bed hoping the morning brings calm heads and clear answers rather than a rush to escalate.
