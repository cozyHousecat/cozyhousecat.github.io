The Cummings Testimony

2023-10-31cozyHousecat

Dominic Cummings, Boris Johnson’s former chief adviser, gave evidence to the Covid inquiry today, and it was as dramatic as everyone expected, with a long list of WhatsApp messages from 2020 being read out, many of them full of very strong language about ministers and officials. He apologised for some of what he’d written, especially his comments about a senior female civil servant, but he stood by his main argument that the government’s decision-making at the start of the pandemic was chaotic and that the system wasn’t up to the job. Johnson’s allies have long said that Cummings has a grudge and isn’t a reliable witness, so his evidence needs to be weighed against what others say, and Johnson himself is due to give his own account later. What sticks with me is that every one of those messages was written while people were dying and families were cut off from each other, which makes the infighting really hard to read. I hope the inquiry focuses on the lessons that will genuinely help the country cope better next time, because that’s what the bereaved families deserve. 🕯️
