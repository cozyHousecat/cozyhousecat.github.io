Number Four

2023-08-15cozyHousecat

Donald Trump was indicted overnight for a fourth time, this time in Georgia, along with 18 others, accused of a criminal scheme to overturn his 2020 election defeat in the state. The case includes his famous phone call asking Georgia’s top election official to “find” enough votes to win, and it uses laws normally aimed at organised crime. Trump says it’s another politically motivated attack aimed at stopping him running again, while prosecutors say it’s about protecting elections. It’s extraordinary to think that a former president, and the frontrunner to be his party’s candidate next year, now faces four separate criminal cases. Tomorrow it’s the Lionesses’ semi-final, and, on a more personal note, it’s exactly a year since I started this blog.
