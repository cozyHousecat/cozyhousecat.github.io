The news from Israel and Gaza is still weighing heavily on me tonight, especially with Israel announcing a complete siege of Gaza, including cutting off food, fuel and electricity, and the families of hostages pleading for their loved ones, so I'm keeping everyone affected in my thoughts. Elsewhere, there were two stories today that I wanted to note. The first is that Harvard economist Claudia Goldin has won the Nobel Prize in Economics for her research into women's work and pay over the last two hundred years, which helped show why the gender pay gap persists even as women become more educated, and she's the first woman to win the economics prize on her own. Given that Birmingham's council went bust last month partly because of equal pay claims, her work feels incredibly relevant right now. The second is that Unity's CEO, John Riccitiello, is stepping down with immediate effect after the Runtime Fee disaster, which feels like the end of a chapter that showed how much power developer communities really have when they speak up together. 📊🎮
