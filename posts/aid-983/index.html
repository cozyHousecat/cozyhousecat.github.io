Finland Joins NATO

2023-04-03cozyHousecat

Tomorrow Finland will officially become NATO’s 31st member, less than a year after applying, one of the fastest joinings in the alliance’s history. For a country that stayed neutral for decades, shares a 1,300km border with Russia and fought the Soviet Union in the past, it’s a huge change, and it was largely driven by Russia’s invasion of Ukraine. It’ll roughly double the length of NATO’s land border with Russia, which is the opposite of what Putin said he wanted. Sweden applied at the same time but is still waiting, because Turkey and Hungary haven’t approved its membership yet. It’s another reminder of how dramatically the war has reshaped Europe in just over a year.
