Argentina against the Netherlands was the wildest game of the tournament so far. Argentina were 2–0 up and cruising, then Wout Weghorst came off the bench and scored twice, the second from a clever free-kick routine with almost the last kick of stoppage time, to send it to extra time. It was bad-tempered from start to finish, with a record number of yellow cards and players squaring up even after the shootout, before Lautaro Martínez scored the winning penalty to send Argentina into the semis against Croatia. Then came news that took all the fun out of it: US football journalist Grant Wahl collapsed in the press box during the game and has died. He’d spent years covering the workers who built these stadiums, and he wore a rainbow shirt to a match earlier in the tournament, so tonight I’m thinking of his family more than the football.
