Last night, police in Memphis released footage of officers beating Tyre Nichols, a 29-year-old Black man, after stopping him in his car earlier this month. He died in hospital three days later. The five officers involved have been sacked and charged with second-degree murder, and people who’ve watched the footage describe it as horrifying. Tyre’s family asked people to protest peacefully, and despite the anger, the protests across the US have largely stayed that way, which says a lot about the strength of his mother and stepfather. I haven’t watched the footage and don’t think I will, but I’m thinking of his family, who should never have had to see their son’s final moments become national news.
