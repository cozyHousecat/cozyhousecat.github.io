Sam Altman, the head of OpenAI, the company behind ChatGPT, told the US Senate today that AI needs proper regulation, and even suggested a licence system for the most powerful AI models. He admitted things could go very wrong and that his biggest fear is the technology causing significant harm to the world, which isn’t what you usually hear from a tech boss. Senators seemed surprised to have a tech company asking for rules rather than fighting them, especially after years of trying to rein in social media after the damage was done. Some critics, though, worry that big companies like OpenAI might help write rules that make it harder for smaller competitors and open-source projects to keep up. As someone who loves open source, I’ll be watching that part closely, but I’m glad the conversation is happening now rather than in ten years.
