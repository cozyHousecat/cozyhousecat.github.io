Tonight in central Greece, a passenger train carrying hundreds of people collided head-on with a freight train near the city of Larissa, and several of the carriages caught fire. Many of the passengers were young people heading home after a carnival weekend, and dozens are feared dead. The pictures of twisted, burnt-out carriages are horrific, and rescuers are still working through the wreckage in the dark. People are already asking how two trains ended up on the same line heading towards each other. I’m thinking of the families who’ll be waiting all night for news.
