Sweden’s Turn

2023-07-10cozyHousecat

On the eve of NATO’s summit in Lithuania, Turkey has finally agreed to back Sweden’s bid to join the alliance, after more than a year of blocking it. Sweden applied alongside Finland after Russia’s invasion of Ukraine, but while Finland joined in April, Sweden was left waiting while Turkey demanded it do more against groups it considers terrorists. There are still a few steps to go, including a vote in Turkey’s parliament, but it looks like NATO is about to grow again. The summit itself, which starts tomorrow, is expected to be dominated by Ukraine and whether it gets any clearer path towards joining NATO one day. It’s another reminder of how much the war has reshaped Europe in less than a year and a half.
