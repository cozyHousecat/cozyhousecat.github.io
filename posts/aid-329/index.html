Four days’ notice

2022-09-16 2 min read (243 words)

Bank holiday on Monday, announced last Saturday, which is four days’ notice for a thing that normally comes with about a year of it.

The entire country has spent this week quietly rearranging itself. Appointments moved, deliveries that now aren’t coming, and the bins have shifted again, which is the second time this month my bins have moved and I have completely lost track of what day they think it currently is. Somebody I know had a thing booked eighteen months ago which has now been shunted all the way into October.

Nobody’s complaining about it exactly, or at least not loudly and not where anybody might hear, which is its own very British phenomenon. You can hear it though. It’s sat in the way people say “well, you can’t really, can you” and then change the subject to something else entirely.

I don’t have anywhere I need to be on Monday and I have still somehow managed to be inconvenienced by all this, which takes a certain amount of doing.

Anyway, the queue is still going, the telly is still the telly, and the whole thing resumes on Tuesday like a system coming back up after an outage that everybody had a fortnight’s warning about and nobody could do a single thing to prepare for.

Season ends Sunday. Eleven levels left. Cutting it extremely fine and I have nobody to blame for that but myself.

Bins are Wednesday.

I think. I’ll check.
