Tonight, huge crowds poured onto the streets across Israel after Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu sacked his defence minister for calling for a pause to the government’s plans to overhaul the courts. The plans would give politicians much more power over the Supreme Court and judges, which supporters say is needed because unelected judges have too much power, and opponents say would weaken democracy and the independence of the courts. People have been protesting every week for months, but tonight was on another level, with motorways blocked and fires lit in Tel Aviv, and there’s talk of a general strike tomorrow. It’s a reminder that democracy depends on people believing the rules are fair for everyone. Whatever happens next, it feels like a major moment for the country.
