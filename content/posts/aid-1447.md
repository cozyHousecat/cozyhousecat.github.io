The Conservative Party conference has opened at Manchester Central today, so the city centre is wrapped in a ring of steel, with road closures, security checkpoints and protesters making their voices heard outside. The big story hanging over everything is HS2, because there have been reports all week that the government is about to scrap the leg from Birmingham to Manchester, and Rishi Sunak refused to confirm or deny it in interviews this morning. It feels particularly awkward to be talking about possibly cancelling Manchester's high-speed rail link while holding your conference in Manchester, and local leaders like Andy Burnham have been very vocal about how much they think the North needs it. The government argues that the costs have spiralled and the money could be better spent on other transport projects, which isn't an unreasonable point given how expensive HS2 has become. But for those of us who live here, it feels like yet another case of big promises for the North being quietly scaled back, so I'm really hoping we get clarity soon, whichever way it goes. 🚄🐝
