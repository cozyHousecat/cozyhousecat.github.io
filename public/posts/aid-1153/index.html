Copilot Everywhere

2023-05-23cozyHousecat

At its Build conference today, Microsoft announced Windows Copilot, an AI assistant built directly into Windows 11 that can summarise documents, change settings and help with tasks right from the taskbar. It’s the next step after putting AI into Bing and Office, and it means hundreds of millions of people will soon have a chatbot sitting in their operating system whether they asked for it or not. From an IT point of view, I can see how useful it could be for everyday tasks, but I’ve got a lot of questions about what data it can see, where that data goes, and how organisations will control it. It’s another reminder that the line between our devices and the cloud companies that run them is getting blurrier. As always, I’m grateful there are open-source alternatives out there for anyone who wants a bit more control over their own computer.
