Penalties and Fires

2023-07-22cozyHousecat

The Lionesses started their World Cup this morning with a nervy 1–0 win over Haiti, with Georgia Stanway scoring a penalty she had to take twice after VAR. It wasn’t pretty, and Haiti gave them a real fight, but three points is three points, and it was lovely to watch over a late breakfast. The other big story today was on the Greek island of Rhodes, where thousands of tourists and locals, including lots of British families, had to be evacuated from hotels and villages as wildfires spread across the island. Pictures of people walking along beaches with their suitcases while smoke filled the sky were genuinely frightening, and it’s being called the biggest evacuation in Greece’s history. With record heat across southern Europe this month, it’s hard not to see it as another sign of how much the climate is changing.
