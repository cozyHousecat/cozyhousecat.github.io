A small submersible taking tourists down to see the wreck of the Titanic has gone missing in the North Atlantic, with five people on board, including a British businessman. It lost contact with its support ship about an hour and three-quarters into the dive yesterday, and a huge search is underway, with reports that it only carries a few days’ worth of oxygen. It’s terrifying to think of them down there in the dark, and I really hope they’re found in time. Back in Westminster, MPs voted overwhelmingly tonight to approve the report finding that Boris Johnson deliberately misled Parliament, though many Conservative MPs, including Sunak, didn’t take part in the vote. Supporters of the report say it shows Parliament holding a former PM to account, while Johnson’s allies still insist it was a stitch-up.
