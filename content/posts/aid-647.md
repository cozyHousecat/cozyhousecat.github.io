I think I just watched the greatest World Cup final ever. Argentina were 2–0 up and cruising through Messi and Di María, then Mbappé scored twice in two minutes to level it, Messi put Argentina ahead again in extra time, and Mbappé completed the first hat-trick in a men’s World Cup final since 1966. Emiliano Martínez made a ridiculous save in the very last minute, then saved again in the shootout before Gonzalo Montiel scored the winning penalty. Watching Messi finally lift the trophy, after so many years and so much heartbreak, was genuinely emotional, and I don’t even support Argentina. Mbappé deserved so much more than to lose, but tonight belongs to Messi, and after a World Cup with so many difficult questions off the pitch, the football gave us one perfect afternoon.
