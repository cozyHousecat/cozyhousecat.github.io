One Year

2023-08-16cozyHousecat

A year ago today, I published a post called “Hello” and started this little daily blog without any real idea of what it would turn into. Since then, there have been three Prime Ministers and a lettuce, the death of the Queen and a coronation, a war in Europe that’s still going, ChatGPT arriving, Twitter turning into X, two World Cups, and today, the Lionesses reaching a World Cup final. Alongside all that there’s been my own life too: birthdays, gigs, passing my A+ exam, anxious days and brilliant nights out, all written down as they happened. More than 1,300 posts later, this blog has become my way of making sense of a very noisy world, and a corner of the internet that’s completely mine. Thank you for reading, and here’s to year two.
