When the Network Goes Dark

2023-10-27cozyHousecat

Tonight, Gaza has been almost completely cut off from the outside world, with its internet and mobile phone networks going down as Israel expands its ground operations and intensifies air strikes. Aid agencies and journalists say they’ve lost contact with their own staff, and the Palestinian Red Crescent says people can’t even call for ambulances, which is just terrifying to think about during heavy bombardment. As someone who spends her working life keeping networks running, the idea of more than two million people losing every means of communication at once really hits me, because connectivity isn’t just about scrolling and streaming, it’s how people find their families, call for help and tell the world what’s happening to them. Earlier today the UN General Assembly voted by a large majority for an immediate humanitarian truce, though Israel and the US voted against it, saying it didn’t condemn Hamas, and the UK abstained. I really hope the lines come back on soon, and that the people of Gaza, and the hostages being held there, are kept as safe as possible through what sounds like a horrific night. 🕊️📡
