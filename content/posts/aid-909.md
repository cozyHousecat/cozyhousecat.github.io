It’s been a nervous weekend for a lot of people in tech, especially the British startups that banked with Silicon Valley Bank’s UK arm and didn’t know whether they’d be able to pay staff this week. Jeremy Hunt has said the government is working urgently on a solution, and there’s talk of a buyer being found overnight. Late tonight, the US government announced that everyone with money at SVB will get all of it back, and that it’s also stepping in at Signature Bank, another bank that’s just been closed. It brings back uncomfortable memories of 2008, even if this is very different, because so much of everyday life depends on simply trusting that your money is where you left it. I’m hoping tomorrow brings calmer news, and that nobody misses a payday because of this.
