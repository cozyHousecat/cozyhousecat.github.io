Party Monsters

2023-06-17cozyHousecat

Tonight Matilda and I went to Cheddar Gorgeous’s Party Monster Ball at Cruz 101 in Manchester, a night of drag, cabaret and wild costumes from the Family Gorgeous, with a Party Monster dress code that everyone took very seriously. Cheddar was the runner-up on the last series of Drag Race UK and is a proper Manchester icon, so it was brilliant to see them on home turf. The room was full of sequins, colour and people who looked completely, joyfully themselves, which is exactly what a night out like this should be. After missing Thursday’s drinks, it meant a lot to make it out the door this time, and to share it with Matilda. I’m sitting here with glitter in places it will never leave, feeling very happy indeed.
