ntegrity, Professionalism and Accountability

2022-11-08cozyHousecat

Gavin Williamson resigned tonight, less than a day after the “slit your throat” story, and still denies bullying anyone. That makes him the third Cabinet minister to quit in barely three weeks, and the first under the PM who stood outside No 10 promising “integrity, professionalism and accountability.” To be fair, Sunak accepted the resignation fast, but people are already asking why he appointed Williamson in the first place, knowing what the Chief Whip had already raised. The first US results are just starting to come through, and everyone is expecting a big Republican night, so I’ll find out in the morning whether that happened. I’ve spent most of the last month watching politics unravel in real time, and right now I’d swap all of it for one quiet week where nobody resigns.
