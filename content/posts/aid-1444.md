Today's Premier League football has been absolutely bonkers, and the big talking point is a VAR blunder in Tottenham against Liverpool, where Luis Díaz had a perfectly good goal ruled out for offside. The referees' body PGMOL has already admitted it was a "significant human error", because the VAR team apparently thought the on-field decision was "onside" and so told the referee to carry on, when the decision had actually been "offside". As an IT person, that is the most painful kind of mistake to watch, because the technology worked fine and it was the communication process around it that failed, which is exactly the sort of thing a good checklist or confirmation step is meant to catch. Spurs went on to win 2–1 with a stoppage-time own goal, so I can only imagine how furious Liverpool fans are tonight. Meanwhile, Manchester City lost 2–1 at Wolves, which is their first league defeat of the season, so it really was a day for upsets all round. ⚽🖥️
