There Has to Be a Line

2022-12-02cozyHousecat

Kanye West (Ye) has been suspended from Twitter after posting an image of a swastika merged with the Star of David, hours after praising Hitler in a live interview. Musk said he’d “tried his best” but Ye broke the rule against incitement to violence, so the account is gone. There isn’t any room for ambiguity here: antisemitism is hateful and dangerous, and posting a swastika to millions of followers isn’t edgy or free speech, it’s a threat to real people. What strikes me is that a month after promising that Twitter would be a free speech haven and that moderation would go to a council, the call ended up being made personally by one man at midnight. That’s exactly why it matters who runs our platforms, and why I’d rather moderation came from clear, published rules than one person’s mood.
