Tattoo

2023-05-13cozyHousecat

Sweden’s Loreen has won Eurovision with “Tattoo,” becoming the first woman to win it twice, eleven years after “Euphoria.” Finland’s Käärijä won the public vote and came a very close second, and honestly the crowd’s “Cha Cha Cha” chant was the best part of the whole night. The UK’s Mae Muller came second-to-last, which was a bit of a shame because she gave it her all, but the bigger story was how beautifully Liverpool hosted on behalf of Ukraine, with Ukrainian music and stories woven through the whole show. Ukraine’s own entry, Tvorchi, also finished in the top ten, and the cheers whenever Ukraine was mentioned were incredible. It’s been a joyful, slightly chaotic week, and exactly the kind of escapism we all needed.
