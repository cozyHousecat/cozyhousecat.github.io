Police Scotland arrested Peter Murrell today, the former chief executive of the SNP and husband of Nicola Sturgeon, as part of their long-running investigation into the party’s finances, and searched their home and the party’s headquarters. The investigation is about how money raised by the party, including around £600,000 donated for an independence campaign, was used. Tonight he was released without charge pending further investigation, and it’s important to remember that being arrested isn’t the same as being guilty. The pictures of a police tent on the lawn of the former First Minister’s house were still astonishing, just over a week after Humza Yousaf took over as leader. It’s a huge shock for the SNP, and I suspect this story still has a long way to run.
