Last night, as Lunar New Year celebrations were finishing in Monterey Park, a largely Asian American city near Los Angeles, a gunman opened fire at a dance studio and killed ten people. The man suspected of the attack was later found dead in a van, and people are still trying to understand why it happened. On a day that should have been about family, fireworks and new beginnings, an entire community is grieving instead. It’s horribly familiar to see another mass shooting in the US, and I don’t think anyone should ever get used to it, however often it happens. Tonight I’m thinking of the families in Monterey Park, and I hope the Year of the Rabbit brings them some peace.
