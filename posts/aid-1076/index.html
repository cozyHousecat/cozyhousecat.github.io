Uman

2023-04-28cozyHousecat

In the early hours of this morning, Russia launched its first big wave of missiles at Ukraine in almost two months, and one hit an apartment building in the city of Uman, killing more than 20 people, including children. Rescuers spent the whole day digging through the rubble, and in Dnipro a mother and her young child were also killed. These were families asleep in their beds, which is what makes it so hard to read. It’s a reminder that, even as people talk about peace calls and spring offensives, ordinary Ukrainians are still the ones paying the price every night. I’m thinking of everyone in Uman and Dnipro tonight.
