A day off

2022-10-02 1 min read (202 words)

Took a day off today, from the thing I do at weekends that isn’t my job but has somehow acquired all the features of one, and I would like to report that I was extremely bad at it.

Had the laptop open before nine without having decided to. Caught myself, closed it, felt weirdly cross about closing it, then had to go and do something else in another room like a toddler being separated from a toy.

By about eleven I was fine. By two I’d stopped thinking about it. By four I was having a nice time.

The part that got me is that I’d have told anyone else to do this without a moment’s hesitation. I’d have been kind about it and completely certain. Somehow the same advice applied to me had to be constructed from scratch, argued out on paper, and written down as a rule with reasons attached, because apparently I’ll only listen to myself if I show my working.

Anyway. Whatever the thing is that’s eaten your weekends, and I think most people have one at the minute, it will still be there next Saturday. I checked. Mine was.

Bins Wednesday. Back to it in the morning.
