Simply the Best

2023-05-24cozyHousecat

Tina Turner has died at her home in Switzerland, aged 83, after a long illness. She was the Queen of Rock ’n’ Roll, with that unforgettable voice and energy, from “Proud Mary” and “River Deep – Mountain High” to “What’s Love Got to Do with It” and “The Best.” Her story was just as remarkable as her music: she escaped an abusive marriage in the 1970s and then rebuilt her career almost from scratch, becoming even bigger in her forties than she’d been before. She showed that it’s never too late to start again, and that you can come back from the worst things life throws at you. Tonight I’ll be playing her records loud, and probably singing along badly to “The Best.”
