Boris Johnson spent more than three hours today being questioned by the committee of MPs investigating whether he deliberately misled Parliament over lockdown parties in Downing Street. He swore “hand on heart” that he didn’t lie, saying he’d genuinely believed the rules were being followed and had relied on advice, while committee members pushed him on why he didn’t check more carefully. Supporters say it’s become a political witch hunt, while critics argue that anyone who saw those gatherings should have known they broke the rules everyone else was living under. On the same day, MPs voted by a huge majority to back the “Stormont brake” part of Sunak’s new Northern Ireland deal, with Johnson and Liz Truss among the small number voting against. And tonight, Ramadan begins, so Ramadan Mubarak to everyone observing.
