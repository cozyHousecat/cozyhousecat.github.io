The morning after Germany and the US promised tanks to Ukraine, Russia launched another big wave of missiles and drones across the country, killing several people and hitting power supplies again. Ukraine’s air defences shot down most of them, which shows why the air defence systems allies have sent matter so much, but each strike that gets through still costs lives. It feels like every bit of help from Ukraine’s allies is met with more attacks on ordinary people and the things they need to get through winter. Tomorrow is Holocaust Memorial Day, a reminder of what happens when the world looks away from cruelty. Tonight I’m thinking of everyone in Ukraine who is cold and in the dark, hoping the help arrives soon.
