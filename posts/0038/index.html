Price Cap

2022-08-26 2 min read (221 words)

Didn’t write Wednesday or Thursday. Helios turned up in the end, roughly on schedule, and I notice I’ve stopped keeping track of him.

£3,549.

There it is, then. Up from £1,971. Eighty per cent in one step, from the first of October.

I said last weekend that I wasn’t going to look at any of the predictions on the grounds that I’d find out on Friday either way and I’d like to report back that knowing is not better. I had been carrying it about as a shape, and now it’s a figure, and the figure is worse than the shape was.

Then underneath it, almost in passing, the forecasts for next year are sitting somewhere around six thousand eight hundred. This is the bad news arriving early to make room for the worse news behind it.

The library thing makes considerably more sense to me now than it did on Sunday.

Posties out today as well, a hundred and fifteen thousand of them, first of four days on strike. Everyone is asking for more money because everything costs more money, and I’m not sure what anybody imagined would happen instead.

It’s still warm out but its not unbearably warm, which makes it a shame that of this landed on a perfectly nice afternoon.

Anyway. Nothing left to count down to.
