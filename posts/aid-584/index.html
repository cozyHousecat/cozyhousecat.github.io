Urumqi Road

2022-11-26cozyHousecat

Tonight videos have been coming out of China showing people protesting against the zero-Covid lockdowns, which is something I never expected to see. It started after a flat fire in Urumqi on Thursday killed ten people, with many asking whether lockdown measures stopped them getting out, and the vigils for the victims turned into protests. In Shanghai, on a street named after Urumqi, crowds have been chanting for the Communist Party and Xi Jinping to step down, a huge risk to take in China. After watching Iran’s players stand silent during their anthem on Monday, it feels like there’s a lot of quiet bravery around at the moment from people who have so much more to lose than I ever will. It’s also a reminder of why open platforms matter, because without people sharing these videos, most of us would never know any of this happened.
