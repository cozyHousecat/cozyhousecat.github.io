Everything, Everywhere

2023-03-13cozyHousecat

I woke up to the best news: Everything Everywhere All at Once won seven Oscars, including Best Picture. Michelle Yeoh became the first Asian woman to win Best Actress, telling women never to let anyone say they’re past their prime, and Ke Huy Quan, who was a child star in the 1980s and then barely worked as an actor for decades, won Best Supporting Actor and cried his way through the most heartfelt speech of the night. Jamie Lee Curtis won too, after more than 40 years in films, and Brendan Fraser won Best Actor for The Whale, another huge comeback story. A weird, joyful, deeply kind film about family, immigration, and googly eyes beating all the big serious ones feels like a win for anyone who’s ever been overlooked. On a Monday with doctors on strike and banks wobbling, that was exactly the kind of story I needed.
