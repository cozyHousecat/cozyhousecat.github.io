God Save the King

2023-05-06cozyHousecat

Today King Charles III and Queen Camilla were crowned at Westminster Abbey, in the first coronation since 1953, mixing traditions that go back almost a thousand years with some new touches. The most sacred moment, the anointing, happened behind a screen, while other moments were made for the cameras, like Penny Mordaunt holding the heavy Sword of State for ages, a gospel choir singing, and parts of the service in Welsh, Scottish Gaelic and Irish. Whatever you think about the monarchy, and plenty of people have mixed feelings, it was an extraordinary piece of history to watch, and probably the only coronation many of us will ever see. There were real arguments today too, from the cost to the arrests of protesters, which are important conversations to keep having. On my 1,100th post, I’m glad to have written down what this day felt like, wet pavements, bunting and all.
