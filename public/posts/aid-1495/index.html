Could It BE Any Sadder?

2023-10-29cozyHousecat

I woke up this morning to the really sad news that Matthew Perry, who played Chandler Bing in Friends, has died at the age of 54. Friends was such a huge part of growing up for so many of us millennials, and Chandler was always the one with the perfect sarcastic line, the awkward joke at the wrong moment, and that wonderful friendship with Joey. Beyond the show, he was really honest in recent years about his long struggles with addiction, especially in his memoir last year, and he said he hoped to be remembered for helping other people who were going through the same thing. That honesty took a lot of courage, and I think it will mean a lot to people for a long time. Tonight I’ll probably put on a few of my favourite episodes and remember all the times he made me laugh. Thank you, Matthew. 💛☕
