Taking a Breath

2023-10-12cozyHousecat

It’s been a really heavy week, and today was no different, with US Secretary of State Antony Blinken arriving in Israel, families still waiting for news of hostages taken on Saturday, and the people of Gaza facing a siege with power, water and fuel cut off. I’ve noticed I’ve been doom-scrolling far more than is good for me, refreshing the news late into the night, and while I think it’s important to stay informed and to care, I also know I’m no use to anyone if I’m running on empty. So tonight I made a conscious decision to put my phone in another room after tea, make a big mug of hot chocolate and curl up under a blanket while the rain tapped on the window. I ended up playing a few rounds of Fortnitemares, which was a silly, spooky bit of escapism I needed, and I felt a lot calmer by the end of it. If the news is weighing on you too this week, please give yourself permission to step back for a bit, because looking after yourself isn’t the same as not caring. 💛🎃
