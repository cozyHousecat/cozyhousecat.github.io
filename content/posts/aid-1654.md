The European Commission has fined Apple €1.8 billion today, saying it abused its position by stopping music streaming apps like Spotify from telling iPhone users about cheaper ways to subscribe outside the App Store. Apple says it will appeal and has hit back at Spotify, arguing that the app has done very well on the iPhone, so this is going to be a long legal fight. The timing is really interesting too, because on Thursday the EU's Digital Markets Act starts applying to the biggest tech "gatekeepers", forcing changes like allowing alternative app stores on iPhones in Europe and making it easier to switch away from default apps. As someone who cares a lot about open platforms and owning your data, I think rules that make big tech compete fairly and give people real choices are a good thing, even if the details will be messy. It's frustrating that these changes only apply in the EU for now, so I'll be watching closely to see whether any of it finds its way to us here in the UK. 🇪🇺📱
