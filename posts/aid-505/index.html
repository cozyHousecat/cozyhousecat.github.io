A Heavier Saturday

2022-10-29cozyHousecat

I spent a lot of the last fortnight laughing at Westminster, but tonight the news is just sad: more than a hundred people, mostly in their twenties, have been killed in a crowd crush at Halloween celebrations in Itaewon in Seoul, and the number keeps going up. They were out for a night that should have been fun, and I can’t stop thinking about their families finding out. Russia has also pulled out of the grain deal that lets Ukraine ship food through the Black Sea, blaming a drone attack on its fleet and claiming British involvement, which London has flatly denied. That deal has been holding down food prices for millions of people who can afford it far less than we can, so it’s a lot more than a headline. The clocks go back tonight, and I think I’ll use the extra hour to put the phone down, hug the people I love, and be grateful for an ordinary Saturday.
