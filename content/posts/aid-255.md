The wasps have started, the proper late August asshole wasps.

I finally looked up why they turn like this, because it happens every single year and I've never known. Turns out the colony is basically finished by now. The queen has stopped laying, so there are no grubs left to feed, and it was the grubs that produced the sugary stuff the workers lived on. So the workers have no job and no income and they go off hunting sugar wherever they can find it, which is your pint, and the bin, and the windfall apples fermenting quietly in the grass.

They are, in short, unemployed and drunk and furious about it.

I have never felt closer to them.

Warm today, properly warm. Tomorrow is meant to be the twenty eight. I'll believe that when it happens, but I have carefully made no plans that would stop me sitting in it.

It's dark before nine now, which it wasn't a fortnight ago. That one always gets me. You don't notice it going and then one evening you look up and the day has simply ended without checking with you first.

The shops have moved on regardless. Pencil cases where the barbecue things were.

Three days.
