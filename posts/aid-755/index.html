Half a Million

2023-02-01cozyHousecat

Today up to half a million people went on strike, including teachers, civil servants, train drivers and university staff, the biggest single day of strike action in more than a decade. Thousands of schools in England and Wales were closed or partly closed, and there were marches in cities across the country, including big crowds in London and Manchester. The unions say pay has fallen far behind rising prices over many years and that it’s driving people out of these jobs, while the government says big pay rises would fuel inflation and that it can’t afford what’s being asked for. Both sides say they want talks, but nobody seems to be moving very much. Whatever you think about it, half a million people giving up a day’s pay to make a point tells you how stretched many of them are feeling.
