The Writers’ Strike Nears Its End

2023-09-25cozyHousecat

Hollywood’s writers have reached a tentative deal with the studios after almost five months on strike, which is huge news for the TV and film industry. From what’s been shared so far, the agreement includes better pay, a bigger share of streaming income linked to how well shows perform, and some protections around AI, so studios can’t use it to write or rewrite scripts in place of a writer, or force writers to use it. That AI part feels like a really important moment beyond Hollywood too, because it’s one of the first big examples of workers successfully setting limits on how new technology can be used in their jobs. The deal still needs to be approved by the union’s members, and the actors are still on strike, so it’ll be a while yet before our favourite shows are fully back in production. Still, it’s a reminder of what people can achieve when they stick together, and I’m really glad the writers who make the stories we love have won some security. ✍️🎬
