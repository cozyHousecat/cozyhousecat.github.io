Late last night a magnitude 6.8 earthquake struck Morocco's High Atlas mountains, south-west of Marrakech, and by this evening the authorities say more than two thousand people have died. It's the strongest quake to hit the area in over a century, and many of the worst-affected places are remote mountain villages where homes are built from mud brick and stone, and where roads blocked by rockfalls are making it really hard for rescuers to reach people. There's also damage in Marrakech itself, including parts of the old medina, and the images of families sleeping outside because they're too frightened to go back indoors are heartbreaking. I always feel a bit useless watching something like this from far away, so I'm going to look into reputable charities and appeals that are on the ground rather than just scrolling past. Sending so much love to everyone in Morocco tonight, and to anyone here with family or friends there. 💚❤️
