The Ryder Cup started in Rome today, and Europe had an absolutely dream first day, winning all four of the morning foursomes for the first time ever and finishing the day 6½–1½ ahead of the USA. I'm not usually a golf person, but there's something about the Ryder Cup atmosphere, with the noise and the drama and the Europeans actually behaving like a team, that pulls me in every time. It's also the full release of EA Sports FC 24 today, the first football game in the series without the FIFA name, and my timeline is full of people complaining about their Ultimate Team packs already. Closer to home, the Conservative Party conference arrives in Manchester this weekend, so the city centre is about to be full of ring-of-steel security, road closures and protests, which is always an interesting few days to live here. I'll be keeping my head down, staying out of town as much as possible, and probably watching the golf from the sofa instead. ⛳🇪🇺
