Early this morning, a wooden boat carrying migrants broke apart in rough seas just off the coast of Calabria in southern Italy, and dozens of people, including children, have died. Many of them were reportedly fleeing countries like Afghanistan, Pakistan and Iran, and bodies have been washing up on the beach all day while rescuers search for more. Here, we argue a lot about “small boats” as if it’s only a political problem, but scenes like this show the human beings behind those words. Whatever people think about immigration, nobody should have to risk their children’s lives on a broken boat in a winter storm. Tonight my thoughts are with the families of everyone lost, and the survivors who watched it happen.
