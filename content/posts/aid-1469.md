What a brutal day of rugby, with two home nations knocked out of the World Cup in the quarter-finals within a few hours of each other. Wales were beaten 29–17 by Argentina in Marseille this afternoon, despite leading for a lot of the game, and then this evening Ireland, the world's number-one ranked team, lost an absolute thriller 28–24 to New Zealand in Paris. Ireland had won seventeen matches in a row coming into tonight, and they fought right to the end, keeping the ball for something like thirty-seven phases at the death, which made the final whistle even harder to watch. It's now eight World Cup quarter-final defeats for Ireland, and you could see what it meant to the players and fans, who've had such an incredible couple of years. Fingers crossed England can keep the home nations flag flying against Fiji tomorrow, because I'm not sure my heart can take much more! 🏉💔
