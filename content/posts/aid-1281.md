Carlos Alcaraz beat Novak Djokovic in five sets to win Wimbledon today, in a final that lasted nearly five hours and had me on the edge of the sofa the whole time. Djokovic hadn’t lost on Centre Court in ten years, and he was going for his eighth Wimbledon title, but the 20-year-old Spaniard just kept coming back at him. At one point in the final set Djokovic smashed his racket against the net post in frustration, which tells you how much pressure he was under. It really did feel like a passing of the torch from one generation to the next, and Alcaraz’s joy at the end was lovely to see. What a way to end a brilliant fortnight of tennis.
