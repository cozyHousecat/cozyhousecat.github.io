Maui

2023-08-13cozyHousecat

The number of people killed in the wildfires on Maui has now passed 90, making it the deadliest wildfire in the US in more than a century, and officials expect it to rise further as they search the burned-out town of Lahaina. Survivors have described having just minutes to escape, with some people jumping into the sea to get away from the flames, and many are asking why the island’s warning sirens never sounded. Whole streets of homes and historic buildings are gone, along with a huge part of the island’s culture and history. After a summer of fires across southern Europe, Canada and now Hawaii, it’s hard not to feel that this is what our hotter future looks like. Tonight I’m thinking of everyone on Maui who’s lost family, friends and homes.
