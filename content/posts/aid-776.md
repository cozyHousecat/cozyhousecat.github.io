A day after the earthquakes, more than 7,000 people are now known to have died across Turkey and Syria, and the number is still rising as rescuers search through thousands of collapsed buildings. Turkey has declared a three-month state of emergency in the worst-hit provinces, and rescue teams from all over the world, including firefighters from the UK, are heading out to help. Survivors are sleeping in cars, tents and streets in freezing weather, too afraid to go back indoors in case of aftershocks. Every so often a video appears of someone, sometimes a tiny child, being pulled alive from the rubble after more than a day, and those moments are what keep everyone going. If you can, please donate to the aid charities working there, because what’s needed now is warmth, shelter, food and time.
