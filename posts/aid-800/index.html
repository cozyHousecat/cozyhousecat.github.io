Brianna

2023-02-13cozyHousecat

On Saturday afternoon, 16-year-old Brianna Ghey was found fatally stabbed in a park in Culcheth, near Warrington, not far from here, and two local 15-year-olds have been arrested. Brianna was a Year 11 pupil and a trans girl with lots of followers on TikTok, and today her family described her as “larger than life” and “fearless,” and asked for privacy to grieve. Police say they currently have no evidence that her death was hate-related, but many in the trans community are frightened and grieving, and vigils are being organised. Whatever the investigation finds, a teenager has lost her life in a park on a Saturday afternoon, and a family’s world has been torn apart. Tonight I’m thinking of her family and friends, and of every young person who just wants to be themselves and feel safe.
