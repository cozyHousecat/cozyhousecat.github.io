The Lionesses landed back at Heathrow this morning, but they left through a private exit, so the fans who’d waited overnight in arrivals didn’t get to see them, which felt like a bit of a sad full stop to such a brilliant tournament. I totally get why an exhausted squad might not want a big fuss two days after losing a final, but I hope they get a proper chance to celebrate with everyone soon, because they’ve more than earned it. My evening was Gamescom Opening Night Live, curled up on the sofa from seven o’clock, and the highlights for me were Little Nightmares III (with co-op!), a fresh look at Cyberpunk’s Phantom Liberty along with a big free 2.0 update for everyone, and Assassin’s Creed Mirage moving its release forward to 5 October. My favourite surprise was Thank Goodness You’re Here!, a daft little comedy game set in a Northern town, which looks like it was made by people who’ve actually been to one. My wallet is already nervous about this autumn, and Starfield isn’t even out yet. 🎮
