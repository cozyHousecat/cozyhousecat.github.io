Tomorrow the government is expected to unveil a new law on people crossing the Channel in small boats, with reports that anyone who arrives that way will be detained, removed, and banned from ever claiming asylum or coming back. Supporters say it’s the only way to stop dangerous crossings and break the smugglers’ business, after a record number of people made the journey last year. Refugee charities and lawyers say it would mean turning away people fleeing war and persecution, and could break international law, especially as there are so few safe and legal routes to the UK. After the shipwreck in Italy last week, it’s hard not to think about the people in those boats while politicians argue over them. Whatever is announced, I hope it’s judged by whether it actually saves lives, as well as whether it lowers the numbers.
