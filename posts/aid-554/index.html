We Will Remember Them

2022-11-13cozyHousecat

Today King Charles laid his first wreath at the Cenotaph as monarch, the first Remembrance Sunday in seventy years without the Queen at the front of it all. Watching it felt both familiar and new at once, like a lot of this autumn. Sunak used the day to praise Ukraine’s defenders, and with Kherson free again only two days ago, the silence felt much less like history than it sometimes does. Big Ben is back after years of repairs, which is a nice small bit of normal returning to Westminster. I went into this weekend wanting something quiet, and I’ve got something closer to reflective, and honestly, I’ll take that.
