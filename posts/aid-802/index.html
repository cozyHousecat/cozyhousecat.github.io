Love, Anyway

2023-02-14cozyHousecat

Happy Valentine’s Day, though it’s hard to feel very romantic with the news the way it is. Police investigating Brianna Ghey’s death now say they’re looking at whether it was a hate crime, and I woke up to news of another shooting in the US, this time at Michigan State University, where three students were killed. In Turkey and Syria, more than a week after the earthquakes, the number of people who’ve died has passed 37,000, and families are still waiting for news. On a day that’s supposed to be all hearts and roses, it seems more important than ever to just tell the people you care about that you love them, whether that’s a partner, a friend, family or the cat. My Valentine’s was a homemade card, a takeaway and an early night, and honestly, that’s all I want.
