Day Five of the Lettuce

2022-10-18cozyHousecat

Today started with an apology and ended with Downing Street refusing to promise the pensions triple lock, which she said she was committed to barely a fortnight ago. In between, cabinet “backed” her, the Bank of England pushed its gilt sales back to November, and the general mood was everyone watching to see who blinks first. Some Tory MPs are already saying publicly they won’t vote to scrap the triple lock, so even that’s a fight now. It feels less like a government and more like a group chat where everyone has muted everyone else. Five days in, and the lettuce looks steadier than the PM.
