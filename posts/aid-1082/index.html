The Godfather’s Warning

2023-05-01cozyHousecat

Geoffrey Hinton, one of the pioneers whose research made today’s AI chatbots possible, has left Google so he can speak freely about the risks of the technology, saying part of him now regrets his life’s work. He’s worried about AI being used to spread fake images and misinformation so convincing that people won’t be able to tell what’s true, and about it eventually becoming smarter than us. When someone who helped build the thing starts sounding the alarm, it’s worth listening, even if not everyone agrees with how big the risks are. Meanwhile, in the US, First Republic Bank collapsed and was taken over by JPMorgan this morning, the third American bank to fail since March. It’s been a reminder that big, complicated systems, whether banks or AI, can go wrong very quickly if nobody’s paying close enough attention.
