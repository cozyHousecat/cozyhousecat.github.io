Next Friday will mark a year since Russia launched its full-scale invasion of Ukraine, and I can still remember waking up to those first pictures of tanks and explosions. Back then, a lot of people expected Kyiv to fall in days, and instead Ukraine has held on, taken back huge areas, and become a symbol of courage for millions. But the cost has been huge: tens of thousands killed, millions forced from their homes, cities destroyed, and a whole country spending its winter in the cold and dark. Listening to today’s speeches in Munich, it sounds like the war is going to go on for a long time yet, and that the world will have to keep supporting Ukraine for as long as it takes. This week I want to make sure I don’t get so used to the war that I stop noticing it.
