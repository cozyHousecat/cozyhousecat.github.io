After yesterday’s David Carrick case, the Metropolitan Police has said it’s reviewing 1,633 past allegations of sexual offences or domestic abuse involving 1,071 of its own officers and staff, cases that didn’t lead to prosecution or dismissal. Commissioner Sir Mark Rowley says the force “failed,” that hundreds of officers are likely to lose their jobs, and that every one of the Met’s 45,000 officers and staff will be checked again against police records. Carrick himself was formally sacked today, which feels like the absolute least that should have happened years ago. It’s right that they’re finally looking, but it’s frightening that it took a case this horrific to make it happen, and that so many of those people are still in their jobs while the review goes on. For anyone who’s been let down by the police, and especially women who weren’t believed, I hope this is the start of real change rather than another apology.
