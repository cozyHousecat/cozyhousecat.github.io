Two Red Wakes

2023-10-20cozyHousecat

The results came in overnight, and Labour have won both of yesterday’s by-elections, taking Mid Bedfordshire and Tamworth from the Conservatives on huge swings. Mid Bedfordshire had been Conservative for nearly a century, and Labour overturned a majority of more than twenty thousand there, which is apparently the biggest majority they’ve ever overturned in a by-election. The Conservatives point out that turnout was low and by-elections often go against the government in the middle of a parliament, and that’s fair, but these results are definitely going to make people at Number 10 nervous with a general election expected next year. I always find it fascinating to see how local issues and national mood mix together in these contests, and I’ll be curious to see whether this is a sign of a lasting shift or just a protest vote. On a much lighter note, it’s a massive day for games, with both Super Mario Bros. Wonder and Marvel’s Spider-Man 2 coming out today, so my weekend plans are looking very colourful indeed! 🗳️🎮
