Starfield Lifts Off (For Some)

2023-09-01cozyHousecat

Starfield’s early access opened overnight for anyone with the Premium Edition, so my timeline today has been nothing but spaceship builds and screenshots of moody alien planets. The reviews landed yesterday and are mostly very positive, with lots of praise for the scale and the classic Bethesda “go anywhere, poke everything” freedom, though a fair few critics mention long loading screens and a slow start. On Metacritic it’s sitting below Skyrim but above Fallout 4, which feels about right for Bethesda’s first brand-new universe in twenty-five years. I’m trying very hard not to read too much in case I spoil the story for myself before the full launch on Wednesday. Between this, Fortnite’s heist season and Rocket League’s new season coming next week, I may need to book some annual leave. 🚀
