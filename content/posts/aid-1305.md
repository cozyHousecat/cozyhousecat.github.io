A giant, glowing X logo has appeared on the roof of the company formerly known as Twitter’s headquarters in San Francisco, with videos showing it pulsing with bright lights. Neighbours are complaining that it’s shining into their windows at night, and the city says it’s investigating because nobody seems to have got permission. After a week of trying to take down the old Twitter sign without a permit, it’s a very on-brand next chapter. I keep thinking how quickly something that felt like an internet institution has become a chaotic experiment run by one person. It’s another reminder, if I needed it, of why owning your own little website still matters.
