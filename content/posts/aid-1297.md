Wildfires are still burning across the Mediterranean, and today a Greek firefighting plane crashed while fighting a fire on the island of Evia, killing both pilots. On Rhodes and Corfu, thousands of people have spent days away from their homes and hotels, and in Algeria, wildfires in the north of the country have killed dozens of people, including soldiers trying to fight them. The heat this month has been relentless in southern Europe and North Africa, with temperatures well above 40°C in many places, and scientists say climate change is making heatwaves like this hotter and more likely. It’s been strange to watch from a cool, rainy Britain, where it barely feels like summer at all. Tonight I’m thinking about the two pilots who died trying to save other people’s homes, and everyone who’s lost theirs.
