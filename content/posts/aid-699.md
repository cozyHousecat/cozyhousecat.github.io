Today the UK confirmed it will send Challenger 2 tanks to Ukraine, the first Western country to promise modern battle tanks, after Sunak spoke to Zelensky. Hours later, a Russian missile hit a nine-storey apartment block in Dnipro, collapsing part of it and killing many people, with rescuers still searching through the rubble for survivors tonight. The pictures of a building torn open, with people’s kitchens and bedrooms just hanging there, are going to stay with me for a long time. These were families at home on a Saturday afternoon, not soldiers, and it’s exactly why Ukraine keeps asking for better air defences and support. Tonight my heart is with Dnipro.
