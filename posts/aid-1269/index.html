Summer Saturday

2023-07-08cozyHousecat

Today has been a properly slow summer Saturday, with Wimbledon on in the background, a bowl of strawberries and the windows open. I squeezed in a few rounds of Rocket League in the afternoon, and lost more than I won, as usual, but it’s still the most fun way I know to switch my brain off. After a week of heavy news, from cluster munitions to 500 days of war, it felt important to just have a day that was about nothing in particular. The evenings are still light until gone ten, which might be my favourite thing about July. Tomorrow is the final day of the Ashes Test at Headingley, so I’ll probably spend it shouting at the cricket.
