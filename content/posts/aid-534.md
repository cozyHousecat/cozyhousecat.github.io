COP27 opened in Egypt today, and for the first time “loss and damage,” meaning money for the countries hit hardest by climate change, is officially on the agenda, which campaigners have been pushing for years. I’m a Celebrity also started tonight, with Matt Hancock due to arrive later as a late entry, so climate summit and jungle telly ended up on the same evening’s news. Twitter, meanwhile, spent the day proving exactly what the $8 tick warnings were about: lots of verified comedians and celebrities changed their names and photos to Elon Musk to make a point, and some have already been suspended for impersonation. I get the joke, and it was funny, but it also showed how easy it is to make an account look official, two days before the US midterms. I suspect November is going to be about learning to double-check who’s actually talking before I believe anything.
