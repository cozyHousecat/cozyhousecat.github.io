Semi-Final Week

2023-08-14cozyHousecat

It’s World Cup semi-final week, with Spain against Sweden tomorrow and the Lionesses against co-hosts Australia on Wednesday morning, in front of a packed stadium in Sydney. The Matildas have captured the hearts of their whole country, with record TV audiences and fans everywhere, so England are going to be playing against a whole nation as well as a team. A year after winning the Euros, the Lionesses have had to deal with injuries to key players and Lauren James’s suspension, but they keep finding ways to win, which is the sign of a really good team. I’ve already planned my Wednesday morning around the 11am kick-off, and I suspect a lot of offices across the country will be very quiet. Whatever happens, they’ve already made this summer brilliant.
