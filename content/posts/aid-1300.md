Sinéad O’Connor has died at just 56. Most people know her for her heartbreaking version of “Nothing Compares 2 U,” but she was so much more than one song: a fearless, outspoken artist who never stopped standing up for what she believed in. In 1992, she tore up a photo of the Pope on American TV to protest child abuse in the Catholic Church, and was widely mocked and condemned for it, years before the full scale of that abuse came out and proved her right. She also spoke openly about her mental health at a time when very few famous people did, which must have helped a lot of people feel less alone. On what happens to be my 1,300th post, tonight I’m listening to her music and thinking about how often the world fails to listen to brave women the first time.
