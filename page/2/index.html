Shapps

2022-08-21

Trains today, in the loose sense that some of them happened.

Shapps is telling the RMT to put the eight per cent to a vote, and the RMT are telling him where he can put it instead, so I imagine we’ll all be doing this again shortly.

Read something at breakfast that I’ve not been able to put down since. Libraries and museums are working out whether they can operate as warm places this winter, for people who won’t be putting the heating on. Not as a campaign, or a stunt, or a point anybody is making. As a plan. They’re asking for money to do it properly.

Strike day

2022-08-20

Strike day, so no trains, so I have spent Saturday entirely within walking distance of my own front door.

I want to be clear that I support them completely and also that I had plans.

It’s the signallers that do it. Network Rail are out alongside most of the operators, and once the signallers go it stops mattering who else fancied coming in, the whole thing simply stops working. Thursday they managed about a fifth of the network. Today felt like rather less than that, though I’m going off the app and a general atmosphere rather than anything rigorous.
