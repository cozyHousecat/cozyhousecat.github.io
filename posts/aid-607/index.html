One Thousand

2022-12-03cozyHousecat

Tonight Lionel Messi played the 1,000th professional match of his career and scored in it, his first ever goal in a World Cup knockout match, as Argentina beat Australia 2–1. It’s wild that someone as good as Messi had never scored in a World Cup knockout game before, and you could see how much it meant to him and to the fans. Australia made it tense at the end, though: a deflected shot made it 2–1, and Emiliano Martínez had to make a huge save in the last minute to stop it going to extra time. Two weeks ago Argentina lost to Saudi Arabia and looked finished, and now they’re in the quarter-finals, which is what makes tournament football so special. England play Senegal tomorrow night, so I’m hoping for something nearly as good.
