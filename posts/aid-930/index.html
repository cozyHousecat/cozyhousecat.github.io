Rescue Sunday

2023-03-19cozyHousecat

This evening, Swiss bank UBS agreed to buy its troubled rival Credit Suisse in an emergency deal arranged by the Swiss government, to stop it collapsing before the markets open tomorrow. Credit Suisse is 167 years old and one of the most important banks in the world, so its sudden end is a really big deal. After a week of panic that started with Silicon Valley Bank in California, governments and central banks have been working all weekend to calm everyone’s nerves. It’s very different from 2008, but it’s still unsettling to watch banks that seemed solid disappear in a matter of days. Fingers crossed that tomorrow’s markets are calmer, and that this is the end of the scares rather than the start.
