I woke up to the news that military officers in Gabon have taken power, appearing on national TV just after the electoral commission declared President Ali Bongo the winner of Saturday's election, saying they had cancelled the results and dissolved the country's institutions. Bongo is reportedly under house arrest, and a strange video of him asking people to "make noise" has been doing the rounds, while General Brice Oligui Nguema has been named the head of a transitional government. The Bongo family has been in power in Gabon for more than half a century, and there have been plenty of questions about how fair the election was, so I can understand why some people are reportedly celebrating in the streets. At the same time, it's yet another coup after Niger just last month, and soldiers taking over rarely leads to the free, fair democracy people actually deserve. I hope whatever comes next gives the people of Gabon a real say in their future, rather than just swapping one unelected group for another.
