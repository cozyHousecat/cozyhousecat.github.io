Happy St Patrick’s Day! It’s also Red Nose Day, so today has been full of green pints, red noses and people doing silly things for charity, which is the best kind of Friday. The biggest news, though, came from the International Criminal Court, which has issued an arrest warrant for Vladimir Putin over the alleged illegal deportation of Ukrainian children to Russia, along with Russia’s children’s rights commissioner. Russia doesn’t recognise the court, so nobody expects him to be arrested any time soon, but it means he could face arrest if he travels to any of the countries that do. It’s hard to think of anything more cruel than taking children from their families and their country, and I hope one day every one of them gets home. Tomorrow Ireland go for the Grand Slam against England in Dublin, on St Patrick’s weekend, which feels like it was written in the stars.
