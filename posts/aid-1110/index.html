Cha Cha Cha

2023-05-09cozyHousecat

The first Eurovision semi-final in Liverpool was everything I wanted it to be: big, camp, emotional and slightly bonkers. Finland’s Käärijä, in his neon green bolero, got the whole arena chanting “Cha Cha Cha,” Sweden’s Loreen looked as strong as everyone predicted, and both sailed through to Saturday’s final. The show was hosted by Alesha Dixon, Hannah Waddingham and Ukrainian singer Julia Sanina, and there were lots of lovely touches celebrating Ukraine, which really should have been hosting this year. It’s such a joyful thing to watch after a heavy few weeks of news, and I love that Liverpool has thrown itself into it so completely. Second semi-final on Thursday, then the big one on Saturday.
