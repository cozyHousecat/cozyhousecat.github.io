The pen

2022-09-13 2 min read (227 words)

The King got beaten by a pen today and I have now watched it about nine times.

Northern Ireland, signing a visitors’ book, and the pen goes and leaks all over his hand, and he does the absolute full thing. Hands it off, gets up, says he can’t bear this bloody thing, every stinking time, and walks out with the face of a man who has had roughly enough of an entire fortnight and has decided that the pen is going to carry the blame for all of it.

Which it is. Obviously it is. Nobody in the history of pens has ever been that annoyed about a pen.

It’s been a fortnight of extremely slow television and extremely careful sentences, and then a bloke gets ink on his fingers and the whole thing cracks open for about four seconds, and everybody I know has now sent it to everybody else they know.

I don’t think that’s disrespectful either, before anybody starts, I think it’s about the warmest thing that’s happened all fortnight, because it’s the first completely ordinary moment anybody’s been allowed to see since the eighth.

Meanwhile I have spent my own evening reading about plug sockets, which I will spare you entirely.

Season ends Saturday. Thirty three levels to go. It is going to be tight and I have nobody to blame but myself.
