Ethics Adviser

2023-01-23cozyHousecat

Sunak has asked his independent ethics adviser to investigate whether Conservative Party chairman Nadhim Zahawi broke the ministerial code over his tax affairs. Zahawi has confirmed he settled with HMRC, reportedly for millions of pounds including a penalty, while he was Chancellor last summer, and he says he’ll cooperate fully. His supporters say he has paid what he owed and should be allowed to carry on, while critics say someone in charge of the country’s finances shouldn’t have been in a dispute with the tax authorities at all. Sunak came in promising “integrity, professionalism and accountability,” so how this turns out will be a real test of that. For now, Zahawi stays in his job while the investigation takes place.
