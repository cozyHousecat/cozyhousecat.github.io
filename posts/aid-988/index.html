Thirty-Four Counts

2023-04-04cozyHousecat

Donald Trump appeared in a Manhattan courtroom today and pleaded not guilty to 34 felony charges of falsifying business records, linked to hush money paid before the 2016 election. He’s the first former US president ever to be charged with a crime, and the pictures of him sitting at the defendant’s table were remarkable, though he wasn’t handcuffed or photographed for a mugshot. Outside, supporters and protesters crowded the streets, and tonight he’s due to speak to supporters back in Florida, where he’s expected to call the case politically motivated. Prosecutors say it’s about the basic principle that the law applies to everyone, however powerful. Whatever happens next, this case is going to be part of the 2024 election campaign from now on.
