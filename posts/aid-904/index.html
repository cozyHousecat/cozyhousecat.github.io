A Birthday to Remember

2023-03-10cozyHousecat

I don’t think my birthday has ever clashed with this much news: a collapsing American bank, a Chinese-brokered peace deal, Xi’s third term, a summit in Paris, and Match of the Day falling apart over a single tweet. Somewhere in the middle of all that, I had a genuinely lovely day: snow outside, warm drinks, kind messages and the people I love around me. I also quietly passed 900 posts on this blog today, which feels like a nice birthday present to myself, since I started with “Hello” in August. When the world feels chaotic, it’s the small, steady things that matter: time with people, a good meal, writing things down so I remember them. Thank you for the birthday wishes, and here’s to the year ahead being a little calmer, though I’m not holding my breath.
