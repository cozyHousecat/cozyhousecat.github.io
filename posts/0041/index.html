the monday for sunday

2022-08-29 1 min read (171 words)

Bank holiday Monday, and that is the last one until Christmas.

I look this up every year, always on the day itself in the hope that I’ve misremembered it.

This year it’s sitting a bit differently, because what’s actually between here and Christmas is the winter, and the winter has been getting a great deal of advance press lately.

Anyways that’s a problem for October.

Today I had a bank holiday and I used it correctly, which is to say almost entirely horizontally. Somebody a few doors down has been barbecuing since roughly noon with genuine commitment. You can chart the whole arc of it from here without getting up. Optimistic start, long quiet middle, and then at about six a smell strongly suggesting things had got away from them.

It’s got that last-night-of-the-holidays feeling to it, which I do resent at my old age of 30. Bins tomorrow, a day late. Posties out again Wednesday. September on Thursday.

I’ll cope. I just wish somebody would space them out a bit.
