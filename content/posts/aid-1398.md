Birmingham City Council, the biggest local authority in Europe, has issued a Section 114 notice today, which effectively means it's declared itself unable to balance its books and has to stop all non-essential spending. The main reason given is a huge bill for equal pay claims, potentially up to £760 million, mostly owed to women who were paid less than men doing comparable jobs. The detail that made my IT ears prick up, though, is that the council has also been struggling with a troubled Oracle finance and HR system, whose costs have reportedly ballooned from around £20 million to about £100 million. Politicians are already arguing over whether this is down to local mismanagement or years of tight government funding for councils, and I suspect there's some truth on both sides. Either way, it's the residents who'll pay through cuts to services, so I hope the people of Brum get some honesty about how it happened, and a lot more care with any IT project that comes next. 💷💻
