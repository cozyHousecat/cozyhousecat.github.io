Tomorrow there are three by-elections in England, in Uxbridge and South Ruislip, Boris Johnson’s old seat, Selby and Ainsty in North Yorkshire, and Somerton and Frome in Somerset. All three were won by the Conservatives at the last election, so they’re a big test for Sunak, with Labour hoping to win the first two and the Lib Dems targeting Somerton and Frome. In Uxbridge, a lot of the campaign has been about London Mayor Sadiq Khan’s plans to expand the ULEZ clean air charge, which has become surprisingly central to the whole contest. Supporters of the government hope local issues will help them hold on, while opposition parties say the results will show the public wants change. Results come in overnight, so I’ll find out in the morning.
