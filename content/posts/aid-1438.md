Joe Biden became the first sitting US president in modern history to join a picket line today, standing with striking United Auto Workers members outside a GM parts centre near Detroit and telling them through a megaphone that they deserve a significant raise. It's a pretty extraordinary image, and it's clearly a statement about which side he's on in this dispute, although critics say a president shouldn't be openly taking sides in private contract negotiations. It's also very political timing, with Donald Trump due in Michigan tomorrow to speak to auto workers too, and both of them clearly see those workers' votes as crucial for next year's election. Whatever you think of either of them, it's interesting seeing how central workers' rights have become to American politics this year, from the writers in Hollywood to the factory floors in the Midwest. I'll be keeping an eye on whether this actually helps move the negotiations forward, because in the end it's the workers and their families who need a fair deal. 🇺🇸✊
