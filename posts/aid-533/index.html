Remember, Remember

2022-11-05cozyHousecat

The fireworks have been going off outside for hours, and it’s a relief to have something lighting up the sky that isn’t bad news for once. I keep thinking about the $8 blue tick, because one campaign group has already pointed out that anyone could pay, change their name to a news outlet or an election official, and look completely genuine, three days before the US midterms. Verification used to be the shortcut that told you who was real, and if anyone can buy it, that shortcut is gone, and I don’t know what replaces it. Tomorrow COP27 opens and Matt Hancock’s first episode of I’m a Celebrity airs, which shows the range of the news at the moment. For tonight, I’m going to watch the sparkly bits out the window and leave my phone in the other room.
