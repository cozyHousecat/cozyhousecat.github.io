A passenger plane carrying 72 people crashed this morning as it came in to land at Pokhara in Nepal, and it’s feared nobody survived, making it Nepal’s deadliest air crash in decades. Video shared online shows it banking sharply just before it went down, and it’s unbearable to think about the families waiting for news. In Dnipro, rescuers have spent a second day digging through the ruins of the apartment block hit by a Russian missile yesterday, and the number of people killed keeps rising, though they’ve also pulled some survivors from the rubble. It’s a hard day to be reading the news, and I almost didn’t want to write about it at all. But I think remembering the people who’ve been lost matters, so tonight I’m thinking of Pokhara and Dnipro.
