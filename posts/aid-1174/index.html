In Broad Daylight

2023-05-29cozyHousecat

Russia attacked Kyiv again today, but this time in broad daylight, with ballistic missiles arriving in the morning as people were heading to work. It was the third attack on the city in 24 hours, after a huge overnight drone strike timed for Kyiv Day, the city’s annual celebration of its founding. Videos showed people running for shelter as explosions boomed above the city, and Ukraine says its air defences shot down most of the missiles, though some people were injured by falling debris. It’s frightening to imagine having to rush to a shelter on a sunny Monday morning, while here we’ve spent the day enjoying a bank holiday. Days like this make me grateful for how ordinary my life is, and remind me not to take it for granted.
