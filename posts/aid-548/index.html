Kherson

2022-11-11cozyHousecat

Ukrainian troops entered Kherson today after Russia withdrew, and the videos of people in the streets hugging soldiers, waving flags and crying have been on my screen all evening. Kherson was the only regional capital Russia captured after the full invasion began in February, so getting it back is a big deal, and some people are hoping it could be a turning point. It feels fitting that it happened on Armistice Day, the day we remember what war costs and why freedom matters. There’s still a long winter ahead, and Russia has spent the last month bombing power and water, so I don’t want to pretend it’s over. Tonight, though, after a week of shrinking economies, collapsing crypto and Twitter chaos, I’m just going to be glad for the people of Kherson.
