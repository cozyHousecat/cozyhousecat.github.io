Kelly Does It Again

2023-08-07cozyHousecat

The Lionesses are through to the World Cup quarter-finals, but only just, beating Nigeria on penalties after 120 tense, goalless minutes. Lauren James, who’s been brilliant all tournament, was sent off for stepping on a Nigerian player, so England had to hang on with ten players for the whole of extra time. Nigeria were fantastic and could easily have won, which made the shootout almost unbearable to watch over my morning coffee. Then Chloe Kelly stepped up to take the final penalty, just like she scored the winner at the Euros final last summer, and smashed it into the top corner. They play Colombia in the quarter-finals on Saturday, and my nerves are already shot.
