Zelensky made a surprise visit to the UK today, meeting Sunak at Chequers, the Prime Minister’s country house. The UK has promised hundreds more air defence missiles and long-range attack drones to help Ukraine protect itself and push back against Russia. Zelensky is still pushing hard for fighter jets, and Sunak says the UK will help build an international coalition to provide Ukraine with F-16s and train its pilots. Ukraine’s spring offensive is widely expected soon, so this European tour seems to be about getting everything in place before it starts. It’s been five days of Zelensky popping up in different capitals, and every visit seems to bring more support with it.
