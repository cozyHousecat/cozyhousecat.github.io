Today was all about the government’s new Illegal Migration Bill, and it’s left the country very divided. Supporters say it’s a tough but fair plan to stop dangerous Channel crossings and smuggling gangs, and that most voters want the boats stopped. Critics, including refugee charities, lawyers and the UN refugee agency, say it would deny people fleeing war and persecution any chance of asylum, and the Home Secretary herself couldn’t say it complies with human rights law. Then Gary Lineker’s tweet turned it into a row about free speech and BBC impartiality too, with some saying presenters should keep their politics to themselves and others saying he’s a sports presenter who’s allowed an opinion. Under all the noise are real people risking their lives on small boats, and I hope that doesn’t get lost while everyone argues.
