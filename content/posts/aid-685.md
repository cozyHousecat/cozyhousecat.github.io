From midnight in China, which was this afternoon our time, people arriving from abroad no longer have to quarantine, ending almost three years of some of the strictest border rules in the world. For millions of families who haven’t been able to see each other since the start of the pandemic, that must be an enormous relief, especially with the Lunar New Year coming up in a few weeks. It comes while cases in China are reportedly surging, though, and several countries, including the UK, have brought in testing for travellers arriving from there. It’s hard to believe that only six weeks ago people were holding up blank sheets of paper in protest at all this, and now it’s being dropped almost overnight. I hope the reunions are joyful and the hospitals cope.
