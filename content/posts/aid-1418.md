Matilda and I escaped the city today for a day trip to Silverdale, the little village tucked away on the edge of Morecambe Bay in the Arnside and Silverdale National Landscape. It's one of those places that feels miles from anywhere, with limestone, woodland and huge open views across the bay, where the tide seems to stretch out forever. We spent the day wandering, chatting and just breathing in the fresh air, which was exactly what my brain needed after a busy few weeks of work and an endless news cycle. There's something about being near the sea that makes everything feel a bit quieter and more manageable, and it was really lovely to have some proper time together, just the two of us. We came home tired, a little windswept and very happy, and I'm already wondering when we can sneak back. 🌊🌿
