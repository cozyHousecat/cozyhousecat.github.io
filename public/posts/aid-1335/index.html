Evacuated

2023-08-11cozyHousecat

All the asylum seekers who’d been moved onto the Bibby Stockholm barge in Dorset this week have been taken off again, after Legionella bacteria, which can cause a serious lung infection, were found in its water system. The government says it acted quickly to protect people’s health, while critics say it shows the whole plan was rushed and that basic checks should have been done before anyone moved on. It’s a reminder that behind every policy are real people, many of whom have already been through a lot to get here. Meanwhile, in the World Cup quarter-finals, Spain beat the Netherlands in extra time and Sweden knocked out Japan, so they’ll meet in the semi-final. The Lionesses play Colombia tomorrow, and the nerves are back.
