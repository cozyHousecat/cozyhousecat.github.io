The Golden Era Is Over

2022-11-28cozyHousecat

Sunak gave his first big foreign policy speech tonight at the Lord Mayor’s Banquet and said the “golden era” of UK–China relations is over, along with the naive idea that trade would lead to social and political reform. Coming after a weekend of people in Shanghai holding up blank paper and a BBC journalist being beaten, the timing could hardly be more pointed. He described China as a “systemic challenge” rather than a threat, and some in his own party are already saying that’s too soft. What gets me is how tangled up we all are in it: the phones we post protest videos from were most likely built in Chinese factories, Apple’s main iPhone plant has had its own unrest this month, and Musk is fighting with Apple over who controls what we can see. It’s one of the reasons I like running my own stuff where I can, because it’s a small way of not handing everything to a few companies or governments.
