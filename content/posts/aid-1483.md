There was a small moment of relief tonight, as Hamas released two elderly Israeli women, aged 85 and 79, who had been held hostage in Gaza since the attack on the 7th of October. They were handed over to the Red Cross and taken back to Israel, after mediation by Egypt and Qatar, but both of their husbands are still believed to be held in Gaza, so their families' relief must be mixed with so much worry. Around two hundred other hostages are still being held, and every single release feels like a reminder of how many families are still waiting by the phone. Meanwhile, the health ministry in Gaza says more than five thousand people have now been killed there since the war began, as Israeli strikes continue and aid is only trickling in through the Rafah crossing. I keep hoping that these small steps can open the door to more hostages coming home, and to proper help reaching the civilians in Gaza who so desperately need it. 🕊️
