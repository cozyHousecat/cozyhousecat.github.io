Since I passed my first major IT Certification ([IT Fundamentals+](https://www.credly.com/badges/da72f0c2-36b7-4d1e-aa69-4943b50fb9a2)) which arguably isn't a _major_ certification but it is the first real certification in IT that I've obtained and feels like a massive validation of skills that I've had for years, it has given be a huge boost in confidence. Sure, the exam itself is a pre-entry level certification and was piss easy (I did it in less than twenty minutes) but it is a starting point.

Is it time? Maybe it is.
