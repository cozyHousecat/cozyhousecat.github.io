Nurses in England, Wales and Northern Ireland walk out again tomorrow, and ambulance workers strike on Wednesday, so this is a really hard week for the NHS just before Christmas. Nobody becomes a nurse or a paramedic because they want to stand on a picket line in the freezing cold, and that so many of them feel they have no other option says a lot. The government says it can’t afford what they’re asking for, and the unions say low pay is driving people out and making care worse for everyone, so it feels completely stuck. Whatever side people are on, I hope everyone who needs care this week gets it, and that there’s a deal soon. If I need anything, I’ll be very nice to whoever looks after me.
