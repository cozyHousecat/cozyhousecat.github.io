The first UK evacuation flights have left Sudan, taking British nationals from an airfield near Khartoum to Cyprus, during the shaky 72-hour ceasefire. People were told to make their own way to the airfield, which for many meant a frightening journey through a city at war, so it’s been an incredibly tense day for families. The government says it’s prioritising families with children, the elderly and people with medical needs, and will keep running flights as long as it’s safe. Some people have criticised the time it took, but tonight there will be some very emotional reunions, with people finally safe. I’m still thinking of the Sudanese people who can’t simply get on a plane and leave.
