Paradise

2022-10-07 3 min read (465 words)

Nearly a month in to the twenty-second season of Fortnite, aka Paradise and I must say I miss Vibin or maybe I just want the summer to come back.

PARADISE. Bold choice of name, that, for a season whose entire plot is a sentient silver liquid steadily eating the island one landmark at a time. If this is paradise then I’d like a word with whoever signed off the brochure, because the brochure said nothing whatsoever about the creeping chrome.

I would like to note, for the record, that I am enjoying myself enormously. I am aware that I announced exactly that in public a week ago, in writing, in a post where I claimed not to have thought about Vibin since the Tuesday. I would like that post struck from the record.

Things I have formed opinions about since:

The season villain is called the Herald, which is a very confident name for somebody who unlocks three quarters of the way through the battle pass. Heralds traditionally arrive before the thing, its literally in the job description.

The new ‘chrome’ guns get better the more damage you do with them, climbing all the way up from Uncommon to Mythic, which is a beautiful system designed with great care for players who hit what they are aiming at. Mine has been Uncommon since the twentieth of September.

There is a block of flats in the sky called Cloudy Condos. Fastest planning approval in recorded history and nobody has said a word about it.

And there’s an item called the Port-A-Bunker, where you throw a bunker on the floor and climb inside it, which is comfortably the most relatable thing they have ever shipped, and I use it constantly, and not always because anybody is shooting at me.

The chrome blob business where you go very fast and stop being hurt by falling off things remains my only real tactic. It is still just running away, only now with sparkles.

If I’m honest with myself, the thing I’m actually missing is August. Vibin was long light evenings and the window open and that daft tree, and Paradise is dark by seven with the heating on and me reading about kilowatt hours for recreation. None of which is the game’s fault, the game is fine, but its just where I happen to notice.

And yes, in a couple of months this one will end as well and I will write a mournful little post about how much I miss the chrome, and I have stopped trying to fight that. Apparently I’m a person who gets sentimental about things that were advertised as temporary from the outset, which is going to serve me beautifully for the rest of my life.

Still terrible at it, but I’m still playing.
