It’s been a quiet Sunday here, but tomorrow nurses and ambulance workers in England are striking on the same day for the first time, which is being described as the biggest strike in NHS history. The unions say staff are exhausted and leaving because pay hasn’t kept up with prices, while the government says it can’t afford what’s being asked without making inflation worse. Either way, it’s going to be a hard day for hospitals that are already stretched, and a hard decision for staff who clearly don’t want to leave patients. I keep thinking about how much we all relied on those same people during the pandemic, clapping on our doorsteps and calling them heroes. I really hope this ends with a deal soon, because nobody wins while it goes on.
