Putin announced today that Russia will station tactical nuclear weapons in Belarus, its close ally on the borders of Ukraine, Poland and Lithuania. It’s the first time Russia has put nuclear weapons outside its own territory since the 1990s, and NATO has called it dangerous and irresponsible rhetoric. Experts say it doesn’t really change the military balance, since Russia could already reach the same places, and that it’s mainly meant to scare the West out of sending more support to Ukraine. But any talk of nuclear weapons is frightening, especially with the Doomsday Clock already closer to midnight than ever. Sitting in the dark for Earth Hour tonight, it was hard not to think about how much the world needs calmer heads.
