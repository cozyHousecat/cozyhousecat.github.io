The first families from Nagorno-Karabakh have started crossing into Armenia today, just days after Azerbaijan's lightning military offensive forced the region's ethnic Armenian leadership to agree to disarm. The region has been internationally recognised as part of Azerbaijan, but it has been run by ethnic Armenians for decades, and many of the roughly 120,000 people who live there say they don't feel safe staying under Azerbaijani rule, despite Baku promising to protect their rights. Armenia's prime minister has warned that he expects most of the population to leave, and it's heartbreaking to see images of people loading their whole lives into cars after months of shortages caused by the blockade of the only road to Armenia. It's one of those conflicts that rarely makes the headlines here, but for the people living through it, this could be the end of a community that has been there for generations. I really hope that whatever happens next, people are allowed to leave safely, or to stay safely if they choose, and that aid gets to everyone who needs it. 🕊️
