What a Super Saturday of rugby to finish the Six Nations! England pushed France all the way in Lyon in a wild, end-to-end game, only for Thomas Ramos to kick a late penalty to win it 33–31 for France, which was absolutely heartbreaking after last week's win over Ireland. Ireland beat Scotland 17–13 in Dublin to win the championship for the second year in a row, so even though England spoiled their Grand Slam, they're still worthy champions. Earlier on, Italy finished their best-ever Six Nations by beating Wales, while England can take real heart from the way they played in these last two games. I've really enjoyed this tournament, and I'm even starting to understand the rules, which is progress! Now I've just got to wait a whole year to do it all over again. 🏉🏆
