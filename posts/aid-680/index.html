Two Years On

2023-01-06cozyHousecat

It’s two years today since the attack on the US Capitol, and Biden marked it by giving medals to police officers and election workers who stood up for democracy that day. On the same day, the House of Representatives is still trying to choose a Speaker, now well over a dozen votes in, with Kevin McCarthy winning over most of the Republicans who’d blocked him after agreeing to a lot of their demands. It looks like he might finally get there tonight, though at the cost of giving his critics a lot of power over him. There’s something a bit poetic about the building where that riot happened spending the anniversary proving how messy, slow and frustrating normal democracy is, but that’s still far better than the alternative. I’ll check in the morning to see if they’ve managed it.
