Anger in Greece

2023-03-01cozyHousecat

The number of people killed in last night’s train crash in Greece has passed 40, many of them students, and the country has declared three days of mourning. Greece’s transport minister has resigned, and the stationmaster at Larissa has been arrested, accused of sending the passenger train onto the same track as the oncoming freight train. Railway workers say they’ve been warning for years about old safety systems that never worked properly, and there’s growing anger that this was a disaster waiting to happen. Protests have already started in Athens, and I don’t think anyone is going to accept that it was just one person’s mistake. Today should have been a hopeful start to spring, but my heart is in Greece tonight.
