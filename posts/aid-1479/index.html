A City Remembers

2023-10-22cozyHousecat

All weekend, tributes to Sir Bobby Charlton have been pouring in, and people have been leaving flowers, scarves and messages outside Old Trafford, by the statue of the club’s famous “United Trinity”. You don’t have to be a United fan, or even into football, to understand what he meant to this city, because his story is so tied up with Manchester’s own history, from surviving the Munich air disaster in 1958 to helping rebuild the team that went on to win the European Cup ten years later. Lots of the tributes have talked about how humble and kind he was, and how he always took time for fans, which says a lot about the kind of person he was beyond the trophies. It’s also been lovely seeing rival fans, including plenty of City supporters, paying their respects, which is a reminder that some people are bigger than the rivalries. Rest well, Sir Bobby, and thank you for everything you gave to football and to Manchester. 🌹⚽
