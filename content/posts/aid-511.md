Lula has narrowly won Brazil’s presidential runoff with about 50.9% of the vote, beating Bolsonaro in one of the tightest results in the country’s history. It’s an extraordinary comeback for someone who was in prison not that long ago, and Bolsonaro hasn’t conceded yet, so it may not be fully settled. This weekend has had far too much grief in it, with Itaewon yesterday and Morbi today, and I keep thinking about how little separates an ordinary night out from a disaster. Two weeks ago I was laughing at a lettuce on a livestream, and since then it’s felt like the whole world has been shaken up, not just Westminster. It’s Halloween tomorrow, so I’ll try to finish the month on something kinder than this.
