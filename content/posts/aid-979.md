Finland’s Prime Minister Sanna Marin has lost the country’s general election, with the centre-right National Coalition party coming first and the populist Finns Party second. Marin became internationally famous as one of the world’s youngest leaders, guiding Finland through the pandemic and its historic decision to join NATO, but at home voters were more worried about government spending and debt. It’s a bittersweet moment for her, since Finland is set to formally join NATO in just a couple of days, a huge shift for a country that stayed neutral for decades. The winning party will now try to form a coalition, which could take a while. However people feel about her politics, she leaves having steered her country through some of its biggest decisions in a generation.
