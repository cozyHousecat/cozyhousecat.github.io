The USA, who’d won the last two Women’s World Cups, are out after losing to Sweden on penalties in the last 16, their earliest ever exit from the tournament. The winning penalty was saved, then trickled over the line by what looked like a millimetre, and it took goal-line technology to confirm it, which is about as dramatic as football gets. It was also Megan Rapinoe’s last World Cup, and she missed her penalty, which was a sad way for one of the sport’s biggest stars to finish. With Germany, Brazil and now the USA all gone, this really is the most open World Cup in years. The Lionesses play Nigeria tomorrow morning, and I’m already nervous.
