Tonight Matilda and I saw Queens of the Stone Age at the Piece Hall in Halifax, their first UK show in five years, in a sold-out Georgian courtyard that has to be one of the most beautiful gig venues in the country. Coach Party opened, and then Josh Homme and the band played a mix of songs from their new album, In Times New Roman…, out just last week, alongside the classics, with the whole courtyard bouncing to “Go With the Flow.” Watching a band that loud and that tight in a historic stone square, with the sky slowly darkening above us, felt a bit unreal. It’s been a long time since I’ve felt that lost in music, and sharing it with Matilda made it even better. My ears are ringing, my feet hurt, and I wouldn’t change a thing.
