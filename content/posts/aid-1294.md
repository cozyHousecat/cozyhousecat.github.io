Tonight Elon Musk announced that Twitter is going to say goodbye to its name and the famous blue bird logo, and rebrand as “X,” with a new logo potentially going live as soon as tomorrow. It’s one of the most recognisable brands on the planet, with words like “tweet” in the dictionary, so throwing that away seems like an enormous gamble. Musk has talked for years about building an “everything app” called X, with payments, messaging and more, and this seems to be the next step. For a lot of us who spent years on Twitter, it feels like the end of an era, even if the site has already changed beyond recognition since he bought it. I’m more glad than ever that my little corner of the internet is mine, and won’t get renamed overnight.
