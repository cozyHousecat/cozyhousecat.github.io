A Whole-Life Sentence

2023-08-21cozyHousecat

Lucy Letby was given a whole-life order at Manchester Crown Court today, which means she will never be released. She refused to come into court for her sentencing, so the families read out their statements to an empty dock, and I can’t imagine how hard that must have been after everything they’ve already been through. Hearing that the court was so close to home makes it sit even heavier somehow. There are already louder calls to change the law so people can be made to face their sentencing, and serious questions about how concerns raised at the hospital were handled, and I hope both get answered properly rather than fading once the headlines move on. Tonight I’m mostly thinking about the parents, and I’m holding my own little one a bit closer. 💛
