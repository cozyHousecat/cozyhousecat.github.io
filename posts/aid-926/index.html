Grand Slam

2023-03-18cozyHousecat

Ireland beat England 29–16 in Dublin to win the Grand Slam, on St Patrick’s weekend, in front of a home crowd, so I don’t think it could have been scripted any better. Johnny Sexton, playing in his final Six Nations, also became the tournament’s all-time top points scorer, and the atmosphere in the Aviva looked incredible. England played with real heart after last week’s thrashing by France, but Ireland have been the best team all tournament and deserved every bit of this. Elsewhere, France beat Wales and Scotland beat Italy to round off a really entertaining championship. And to top it off, Gary Lineker was back on Match of the Day tonight, so it’s a calm and cheerful Saturday night all round.
