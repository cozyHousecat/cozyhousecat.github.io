Sunak and EU Commission President Ursula von der Leyen announced a new deal at Windsor today, called the Windsor Framework, to fix the post-Brexit trade rules for Northern Ireland that have caused years of rows. The idea is to make it easier for goods to move from Great Britain to Northern Ireland, with “green lanes” for things staying there, and to give the Stormont Assembly a say over new EU rules. Supporters say it’s a genuine breakthrough after years of deadlock, while some unionists and Brexit-supporting MPs want time to go through the details before deciding whether to back it. Von der Leyen also met the King afterwards, which some people felt dragged him into politics, though the palace said it was the government’s advice. After so many years of arguing about this, it would be really nice if it finally helps Northern Ireland’s government get back up and running.
