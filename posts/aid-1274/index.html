When Conditions Are Met

2023-07-11cozyHousecat

At the NATO summit in Vilnius today, the alliance said Ukraine’s future is in NATO and that it will be invited to join “when allies agree and conditions are met,” but without giving any timeline. Zelensky was openly frustrated, calling the lack of a timetable “absurd” before he even arrived, since Ukraine feels it’s already fighting for Europe’s security. Many NATO members, especially the US and Germany, worry that promising membership during a war could drag the alliance into direct conflict with Russia, which is a fair concern too. It’s a really difficult balance between supporting Ukraine and avoiding a much bigger war, and I don’t envy anyone trying to strike it. Tomorrow Zelensky meets NATO leaders directly, and there’s talk of new long-term security promises from G7 countries, so this story isn’t finished yet.
