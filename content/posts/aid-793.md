Almost a week since the earthquakes, more than 30,000 people are now known to have died in Turkey and Syria, which makes it one of the deadliest disasters in either country in modern times. Incredibly, rescuers are still pulling a few people out alive after more than 150 hours, which seems impossible and gives everyone a reason to keep digging. But for most families, the waiting is turning into grief, and the survivors face weeks of cold, homelessness and rebuilding their lives from nothing. North-west Syria is still struggling to get enough aid in, and it’s hard to watch help arrive so slowly when it’s so badly needed. Whatever else is going on in the world this week, I don’t want to forget about the people there.
