Is nothing scary anymore?

2022-10-11 1 min read (96 words)

I was having this discussion with my partner earlier, why are horror movies not scary anymore? I can’t remember the last time I was truly scared watching a horror movie but if I had to pick a film it would be Dark Skies which on first glance is your run of the mill alien abduction film but what I think makes it scary is the fact we don’t see the monsters at all, its more of a psychological thriller.

I’m sure parents find this movie scarier than the rest of us but seriously, go watch it.
