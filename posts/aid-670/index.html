Hello, 2023

2023-01-01cozyHousecat

Happy New Year! The first day of 2023 has been quiet and cosy here, but the world has kept moving: Croatia switched to the euro overnight and joined the Schengen area, so people there woke up with new money in their pockets. In Brazil, Lula was sworn in as president for a third time, and Bolsonaro didn’t even stay to hand over the sash, having flown to Florida instead. After a year that felt like one long shock after another, I’m trying to start this one gently, with fewer late-night news binges and more time with the people I love. My one real resolution is to keep writing here and to keep more of my own stuff on platforms I control, because last year showed how quickly something you rely on can change under you. Here’s to 2023, and to a few more boring days, hopefully.
