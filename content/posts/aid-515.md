It’s Halloween, and honestly nothing anyone has dressed up as tonight is scarier than the actual news this month. In October we had a Chancellor sacked, a budget torn up, two Home Secretaries (one of them twice), a Prime Minister outlasted by a lettuce, another sworn in without a vote, and a billionaire buy one of the main places we all talk to each other. On top of that came Itaewon, Morbi, Kyiv without water, and Brazil still waiting for Bolsonaro to accept the result. I started this blog in August thinking it would be a little daily diary, and some days this month it’s felt more like keeping a record of the world going a bit wobbly. Tomorrow is November, and all I want from it is boring, so we’ll see.
