Turkey and Syria

2023-02-06cozyHousecat

In the early hours of this morning, while most people were asleep, a magnitude 7.8 earthquake hit southern Turkey and northern Syria, followed hours later by a second huge quake. Thousands of people have been killed, and the number keeps rising, with whole apartment blocks collapsed and rescuers digging through the rubble with their hands in freezing winter weather. In north-west Syria, where many people had already lost everything to years of war, help is much harder to get in. The videos of people pulling children out alive are both heartbreaking and full of hope, because every life saved matters. Tonight, everything else I wrote about today feels very small, and I’ll be finding a way to donate tomorrow.
