Everything is still cancelled and the telly is still the telly.

I spent this afternoon doing something small and technical that nobody asked for, and which will save this house roughly as much money per month as one decent takeaway.

I worked at it for four hours. I have not concentrated on anything that hard since Wednesday.

Which is why, obviously. I wanted something with a right answer in it.

The last three days have not contained a single one of those anywhere, and it turns out I find that considerably harder going than I would have guessed. There's a thing people say about grief and busy hands that I have always privately thought was a bit glib, and I would like to withdraw that.

Not because anybody I know has died, to be clear. Nothing like that. Just that the week has been very large and very slow and very much not up to me, and four hours of arithmetic about something entirely within my control was the first time my head has been properly quiet since Thursday.

Anyway. Football's back next week, apparently. Bins are normal. Milk still needs getting.

Small things. But they're in the right order.
