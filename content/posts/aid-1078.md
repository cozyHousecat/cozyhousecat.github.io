It’s a week until the King’s coronation, the first in 70 years, and you can feel the build-up everywhere, from bunting in shop windows to rehearsals of the procession in central London. Most people alive today have never seen one, so even people who aren’t big royalists are curious about what it’ll be like. There’s also a debate going on about it, with some asking whether a huge ceremony makes sense during a cost-of-living crisis, and a small but growing campaign arguing the country doesn’t need a monarchy at all. Supporters say it’s a moment of history and tradition that brings people together, and that tourism should help pay for it. Either way, we’re getting a long bank holiday weekend with street parties, a concert and a day of volunteering, and I think a lot of people will just enjoy the time off.
