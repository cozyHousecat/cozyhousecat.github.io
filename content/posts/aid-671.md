It’s the last bank holiday of the festive break, so I’ve spent it doing absolutely nothing and finishing the last of the chocolate. The news is already reminding me that 2023 isn’t starting gently, though: A&E departments are under so much pressure that emergency doctors are warning long delays could be linked to hundreds of extra deaths a week. On top of that, the rail strikes start again tomorrow, just as everyone heads back to work, so a lot of people’s first commute of the year will be a mess. None of last year’s problems disappeared at midnight on Saturday, and the cost of living, the strikes and the NHS are all still here, waiting for us. Still, I’m trying to hold onto a bit of the calm from the last few days, and tomorrow is a new week.
