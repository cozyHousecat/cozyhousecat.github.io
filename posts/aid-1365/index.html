A Plane Down Near Tver

2023-08-23cozyHousecat

Big news tonight from Russia: a private jet has crashed in the Tver region north of Moscow with no survivors, and Russian aviation authorities say Wagner boss Yevgeny Prigozhin was on the passenger list. It’s exactly two months since his short-lived mutiny and march towards Moscow, so you can imagine how quickly the speculation has started, though nothing has been officially confirmed about who was actually on board or what brought the plane down. I’m trying not to get swept up in the hot takes until there’s more solid information, because early reports in stories like this often turn out to be wrong. Whatever you think of him, ten people died this evening, and there will be families waiting for answers. This one is going to be on the news for a while, I think.
