A Nobel Behind Bars

2023-10-06cozyHousecat

This year’s Nobel Peace Prize has been awarded to Narges Mohammadi, an Iranian women’s rights and human rights activist who is currently in prison in Tehran’s notorious Evin Prison. The Nobel Committee said it was honouring her fight against the oppression of women in Iran and her campaign for human rights and freedom for everyone, and it pointed out that she has been arrested thirteen times and sentenced to a total of thirty-one years in prison over the years. It feels especially powerful coming just a few weeks after the first anniversary of Mahsa Amini’s death, and the “Woman, Life, Freedom” movement that followed, which Mohammadi has supported from inside her cell. Iran’s government has, unsurprisingly, condemned the award as political, but I think it sends a really clear message that the world is paying attention to the women risking everything for basic freedoms. I hope it brings her, and everyone else imprisoned for speaking out, a little bit closer to being free. 🕊️✊
