Cluster Munitions

2023-07-07cozyHousecat

The US has confirmed it will send cluster munitions to Ukraine, weapons that scatter lots of small bomblets over a wide area and are banned by more than 100 countries, including the UK. The worry is that some of the bomblets fail to explode and can kill and injure civilians, often children, for years after a war ends. The US says it was a difficult decision, but that Ukraine is running short of ammunition, will use them carefully and only on its own territory, and that Russia is already using them. Human rights groups and some US allies have criticised the move, saying it undermines the effort to ban these weapons altogether. It’s a hard one, because I want Ukraine to be able to defend itself, but I also can’t stop thinking about the people who’ll be living on that land long after the fighting stops.
