Police confirmed today that three of the boys pulled from the lake in Solihull yesterday have died, aged eight, ten and eleven, and a fourth boy, who is six, is still critically ill in hospital. From what’s been reported, they were friends playing together after school, and some may have gone onto the ice to help each other when it cracked. People have been laying flowers at the park all day, and the police have praised the officer who tried to punch through the ice to reach them and the members of the public who went into the freezing water. There aren’t really words for something like this, and I’ve been holding my own people a little tighter tonight. Please keep talking to children about how dangerous frozen lakes and ponds are, because no family should ever have to go through this.
