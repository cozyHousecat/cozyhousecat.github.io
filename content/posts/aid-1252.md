Today Elon Musk suddenly limited how many tweets people can read each day, at first 600 for most accounts and 6,000 for paying ones, saying it’s to stop companies scraping Twitter’s data to train AI. Within hours, timelines were full of “rate limit exceeded” errors, people couldn’t load replies, and loads of users were stuck reading nothing at all. It feels like the ultimate example of how fragile it is to rely on a single platform you don’t control, because overnight the rules can change and your access disappears. I’ve seen lots of people sharing Mastodon handles again, and I’m more grateful than ever to have this little blog that nobody can rate-limit but me. In more serious news, Nahel, the teenager shot by police in Nanterre, was buried today, while France faced another night of unrest, and I hope his family find some peace.
