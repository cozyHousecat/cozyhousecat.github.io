Not Amazon

2023-07-12cozyHousecat

The second day of the NATO summit in Vilnius ended on a warmer note for Ukraine, with G7 countries announcing long-term security commitments to help it defend itself for years to come, and Zelensky sitting with NATO leaders as an equal for the first meeting of a new NATO–Ukraine Council. He said he was satisfied with the outcome, even without a membership timetable, which is a big change in tone from yesterday’s frustration. There was an awkward moment, though, when UK Defence Secretary Ben Wallace suggested Ukraine should show more gratitude for Western help, saying allies aren’t “Amazon” taking orders, and Zelensky replied that Ukraine has always been grateful. Biden closed the summit with a big speech promising the US won’t waver in its support. It’s been a fascinating couple of days of diplomacy, even if it’s clear Ukraine’s road to NATO is going to be a long one.
