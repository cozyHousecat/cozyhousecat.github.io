I've finally got a quiet moment to write about yesterday, when we headed to the Doki Doki festival at the Sugden Sports Centre, Manchester's big celebration of Japanese culture. It was lovely just wandering around soaking it all up, with the stalls, the food, and so many people in incredible cosplay who'd clearly put weeks of love into their outfits. The heatwave meant a sports hall full of people got very toasty very quickly, so I have a new and profound respect for anyone who spent the day in a full wig and costume. Events like this always remind me how welcoming fandoms can be, with total strangers chatting about their favourite series like old friends. My feet are still recovering and my wallet is slightly lighter, but I'd go back next year in a heartbeat. 🎌✨
