The UN’s climate scientists published a huge report today saying there’s still a chance to limit warming to 1.5°C, but the window is closing fast, and the UN Secretary-General called it a “survival guide for humanity.” The good news buried in it is that the solutions already exist, from cheap solar and wind to protecting forests; it’s mostly about whether governments actually move fast enough. Meanwhile, Xi Jinping arrived in Moscow for a state visit with Putin, three days after the arrest warrant, which shows just how close the two leaders are. Today is also 20 years since the invasion of Iraq began, something I remember watching unfold on TV as a kid, and its effects are still being felt across the region. On the first day of spring, it’s been a day that makes you think about the kind of world we’re building for the next twenty years.
