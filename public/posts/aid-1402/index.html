Allez Les Bleus

2023-09-08cozyHousecat

The Rugby World Cup kicked off in Paris tonight, and what an opener: hosts France beat New Zealand 27–13 at the Stade de France, the first time the All Blacks have ever lost a World Cup pool match. I’m not the world’s biggest rugby expert, but the atmosphere was electric even through the telly, and the opening ceremony was very, very French in the best possible way. England play Argentina tomorrow night in Marseille, and after a pretty rough run of warm-up results, I think most fans are going in with fairly low expectations and a lot of hope. It’s nice to have another big tournament to dip into so soon after the Lionesses’ summer, even if I’ll be learning the rules again as I go. Plus, with the heatwave still hanging about, the excuse to stay in, watch sport and play some Rocket League in the evenings is very much welcome. 🏉🇫🇷
