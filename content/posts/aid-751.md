The International Monetary Fund now expects the UK to be the only major economy to shrink this year, doing worse than every other G7 country and even Russia. It blames high energy prices, rising interest rates and higher taxes, while Jeremy Hunt points out that previous forecasts were too gloomy and says the UK will do better in the longer term. Either way, it isn’t the headline anyone wanted on the eve of the biggest strike day in more than a decade, with teachers, train drivers and civil servants walking out tomorrow. For a lot of people, these numbers just confirm what they already feel when they look at their bank balance and their bills. Goodbye January, and please let February bring some better news.
