Trains today, in the loose sense that some of them happened.

Shapps is telling the RMT to put the eight per cent to a vote, and the RMT are telling him where he can put it instead, so I imagine we'll all be doing this again shortly.

Read something at breakfast that I've not been able to put down since. Libraries and museums are working out whether they can operate as warm places this winter, for people who won't be putting the heating on. Not as a campaign, or a stunt, or a point anybody is making. As a plan. They're asking for money to do it properly.

I keep picking it up and turning it over and not getting anywhere useful with it. It's such a practical, sensible, kind thing for a library to decide to be. The bit I can't get past is that it needs deciding.

Further down the same list of the day's news, it's going to be twenty eight degrees on Wednesday. Warmer than Mexico City, apparently, which is the comparison papers reach for once they've exhausted the other ways of saying hot.

So he's back after all. I withdraw what I said about him yesterday, he was evidently just running late.

It's a strange pair of things to be holding on the same Sunday, though. Twenty eight on Wednesday, and a library somewhere working out how many chairs it can put out in January.

Fun fact, none of this actually affects me because my job is Work-From-Home and has been since Covid. I don't miss the commute but I do miss the routine of the office, can't have one without the other.

Anyway. Cap on Friday.
