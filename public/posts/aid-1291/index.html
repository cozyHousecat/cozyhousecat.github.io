Two Out of Three

2023-07-21cozyHousecat

The by-election results came in overnight, and the government lost two of its three seats. In Selby and Ainsty, Labour’s 25-year-old Keir Mather overturned a Conservative majority of more than 20,000, the biggest Labour has ever overturned at a by-election, and in Somerton and Frome the Lib Dems took the seat with another huge swing. The Conservatives did narrowly hold Boris Johnson’s old seat in Uxbridge, by fewer than 500 votes, largely thanks to anger about the expansion of London’s ULEZ charge, which Sunak said shows the next general election isn’t a done deal. Labour said Selby proved it can win anywhere, though some in the party are now worrying about how green policies like ULEZ will play with voters. On a much lighter note, it’s also “Barbenheimer” day, with Barbie and Oppenheimer both out in cinemas, and the internet has completely lost its mind over the double bill.
