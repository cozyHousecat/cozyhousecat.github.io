The crumbling concrete row has got bigger today: the government has confirmed 104 schools in England are affected, and many children are starting the new term in temporary classrooms, other buildings or at home. Education Secretary Gillian Keegan was caught on a microphone after an interview complaining that nobody thanks her for doing a good job while others had "sat on their" backsides, and she's since apologised for the language. Meanwhile, the department's former top civil servant, Jonathan Slater, has said the Treasury under Rishi Sunak funded far fewer school rebuilds than officials had asked for, which the Prime Minister has called "completely and utterly wrong". Honestly, I think most parents just want to know their children's classrooms are safe, rather than who said what on camera, so I hope the full list of affected schools is published quickly and properly. It's also a big reminder that infrastructure, whether that's buildings or IT, is only ever as good as the maintenance budget behind it. 🏫
