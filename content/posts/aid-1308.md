The Ashes is going right down to the wire at the Oval, with Australia needing more runs and England needing the rest of their wickets on the final day tomorrow to level the series at 2–2. To make it even more emotional, Stuart Broad announced last night that this match will be his last, after more than 600 Test wickets and some of the most memorable spells in English cricket history. He got a standing ovation when he came out to bat today, and the whole crowd seemed to be cheering for him. After everything this series has thrown at us, from the Bairstow stumping to the comeback at Headingley, it feels right that it all comes down to one last day. I’ll be watching through my fingers tomorrow.
