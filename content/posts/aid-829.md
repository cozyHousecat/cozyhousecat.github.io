A few hours after Putin’s speech in Moscow, Biden spoke to a huge crowd in Warsaw, saying Ukraine “will never be a victory for Russia” and that NATO is more united than ever. Two speeches on the same day, from two leaders, telling completely different stories about the same war. Putin blamed the West for starting it, while Biden said it was a choice Putin made and could end whenever he wanted. Standing in Poland, which has taken in millions of Ukrainian refugees over the past year, Biden’s message about freedom felt especially powerful. With the anniversary on Friday, it really feels like the world is drawing breath before another long year.
