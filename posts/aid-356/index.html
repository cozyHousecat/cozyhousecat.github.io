Still not doing the politics

2022-09-23 2 min read (216 words)

Budget this morning. The pound’s had a rough afternoon.

I said on the fifth that I wasn’t going to do the politics on here and I’m holding to that, partly because everybody else has it extremely well covered today and partly because a blog that is three weeks old and mostly about weather and wasps is not where anyone should be collecting their economic analysis.

What I will say is that today was the first time in about six weeks that I have watched a number move and had genuinely no idea what it meant for me.

The cap I understood. I’d gone and measured things and done the arithmetic and could have told you what it cost this house, to the pound, on a Tuesday. Today I have read four separate explanations of what happened to gilt yields and I could not honestly repeat a single one of them back to you without checking.

Which is a strange feeling after a month of being unusually well informed about one very small thing.

Anyway. It’s properly autumn out there now. I had the heating on for twenty minutes this evening and then turned it off again, which I suspect is going to be the defining domestic ritual of this entire winter.

Season four’s good. Bins Wednesday.
