England are through to the Rugby World Cup semi-finals after beating Fiji 30–24 in Marseille this afternoon, though they made us all suffer, because Fiji fought back to level the scores at 24–24 with about ten minutes to go. Owen Farrell kicked a drop goal and then a penalty to seal it, and I'm not ashamed to say I spent the last few minutes peeking through my fingers from behind a cushion. Then, in the evening game, the hosts France were knocked out by a single point by South Africa, losing 29–28 in Paris, which must have been crushing for the home crowd after the brilliant start to the tournament they had. It means England will play the reigning champions South Africa in the semi-final next Saturday, and I think almost nobody expected them to get this far after the summer they had. Elsewhere in the world, Poland's voters seem to have voted out their governing party in a very high-turnout election, so it's been quite the Sunday for upsets! 🏉🌹
