Dance, Brazil

2022-12-05cozyHousecat

Brazil beat South Korea 4–1 tonight, scoring four goals in the first 36 minutes, with Neymar back from injury and every goal followed by a different dance, including the manager Tite joining in. Some people find all the dancing disrespectful, but I loved it, because football is supposed to be fun and they were clearly having the time of their lives. The nicest moment was at the end, when the players held up a banner wishing Pelé well, since he’s in hospital back in Brazil. South Korea deserve credit too for getting out of their group with that late winner against Portugal, even if tonight was too much for them. Brazil play Croatia next, and on this form they look like the team to beat.
