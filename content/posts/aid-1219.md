Yesterday the cross-party committee of MPs investigating Partygate concluded that Boris Johnson deliberately misled Parliament, and said it would have recommended a 90-day suspension if he hadn’t already quit as an MP. Johnson dismissed the report as a political witch hunt, while his critics say it’s damning proof he broke the trust of Parliament, and MPs are set to vote on it on Monday. Meanwhile, a delegation of African leaders, led by South Africa’s president, visited Kyiv today on a peace mission, and air raid sirens went off while they were in the city. They’re meeting Putin tomorrow, though it’s hard to see either side agreeing to much right now. A heavy week ends with a lot of unanswered questions, both here and in Ukraine.
