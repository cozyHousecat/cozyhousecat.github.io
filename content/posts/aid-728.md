Reports tonight say Germany has finally decided to send Leopard 2 tanks to Ukraine, and to let other countries like Poland send theirs too, after weeks of debate and pressure from allies. There are also reports that the US will send its own Abrams tanks, which seems to be what Germany wanted before it would move. Ukraine has been asking for modern tanks for months, and they could make a big difference to its chances of pushing Russian forces back in the spring. It’s been a long, frustrating wait, especially after the UK committed Challenger 2s ten days ago, but if the reports are right, this is a real turning point in Western support. After a heavy few weeks of news from Ukraine, tonight feels a bit more hopeful.
