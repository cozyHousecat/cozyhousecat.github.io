Manchester City beat Inter Milan 1–0 in Istanbul tonight to win the Champions League for the first time, with Rodri scoring the only goal in a tense, nervy final. That completes the treble, after winning the Premier League and beating Manchester United in the FA Cup final last week, which makes them the first English team to do it since United in 1999. Half of Greater Manchester will be celebrating into the early hours, while the other half will be quietly pretending football doesn’t exist until August. Not everyone is cheering, of course, since some rival fans point to the Premier League charges over City’s finances, which the club strongly denies, but tonight is about what happened on the pitch. Whatever you think of them, watching a team make history on a warm summer night is still something special.
