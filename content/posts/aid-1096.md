The local election results are in, and the Conservatives have lost more than 1,000 councillors across England, one of their worst results in years. Labour gained hundreds of seats and took control of councils such as Medway, Swindon and Plymouth, while the Lib Dems had a great night too, including winning Windsor and Maidenhead on the eve of the coronation, and the Greens made big gains. Sunak called the results disappointing but said he’s focused on delivering on his promises, while Labour says it shows it’s on course for government, though some experts say it still needs to do better before a general election. There were also reports of some people being turned away from polling stations because they didn’t have the right ID. It’s been a huge news week, and tomorrow there’s the coronation, so the country is about to switch from politics to pageantry almost overnight.
