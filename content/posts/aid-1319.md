The group stage of the Women’s World Cup has been full of shocks, and some of the biggest names in women’s football are already on their way home. Germany, twice world champions, went out in the group stage for the first time ever, Brazil were knocked out by a draw with Jamaica in what was probably Marta’s last World Cup, and Olympic champions Canada are gone too. Meanwhile, teams like Jamaica, Morocco and South Africa have made it to the knockouts, which shows just how much the women’s game is growing around the world. It’s brilliant for the sport, even if it makes predicting anything impossible. The Lionesses play Nigeria on Monday in the last 16, and after all these upsets, I’m not taking anything for granted.
