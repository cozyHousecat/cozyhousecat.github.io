President Zelensky announced tonight that he's replacing Defence Minister Oleksii Reznikov, saying the ministry needs "new approaches" after more than eighteen months of full-scale war. His pick is Rustem Umerov, a Crimean Tatar politician who has been running the state property fund and has been involved in prisoner swaps and the grain deal talks. The ministry has been under pressure over procurement scandals, including reports of inflated prices for things like food and winter jackets for soldiers, although Reznikov himself hasn't been accused of wrongdoing. Swapping defence ministers in the middle of a counteroffensive is a big move, and I can see it both as a sign that Ukraine is serious about rooting out corruption and as a risky moment of change at the top. Parliament still has to approve it, so we'll see how this week unfolds. 🇺🇦
