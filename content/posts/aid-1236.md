This evening the US Coast Guard confirmed that debris from the missing Titan submersible had been found near the Titanic wreck, and that it suffered a catastrophic implosion, killing all five people on board. They included the British businessman Hamish Harding and the Pakistani-British businessman Shahzada Dawood with his teenage son Suleman, and it’s heartbreaking to think of the families who spent days hoping. Many people have also pointed out how much attention this story received compared with last week’s shipwreck off Greece, where hundreds of migrants are thought to have died, and that’s an uncomfortable but fair question. Both were tragedies, and every life lost in them mattered. Tonight I’m thinking of everyone who lost someone at sea this month.
