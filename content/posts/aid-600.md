Post number 600, and tonight my timeline is full of screenshots of something called ChatGPT, a free chatbot OpenAI released today as a “research preview.” People are getting it to write poems, explain code, draft emails and answer questions in a way that sounds surprisingly like a real person, which is a big leap from the clunky chatbots I’m used to. It also gets things confidently wrong, and as someone who cares about who holds our data, I’m wary of typing anything personal into a box run by one company. Even so, it feels like one of those moments, like the first iPhone, where you can’t quite tell yet whether it’s a fun toy or the start of something huge. Six hundred posts in, I’m ending a month of lettuces, blue ticks and blank paper with a machine that can apparently write blog posts, so I’d better keep writing my own.
