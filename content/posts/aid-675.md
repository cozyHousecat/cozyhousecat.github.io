Sunak gave his first big speech of the year today and set out five promises he wants to be judged on: halve inflation, grow the economy, get debt falling, cut NHS waiting lists, and pass laws to stop small boats crossing the Channel. Supporters say it’s a sensible, measurable plan after the chaos of last autumn, while critics point out that inflation was already expected to fall and that there was nothing new on the nurses’ and ambulance strikes. I like that they’re specific enough that we’ll actually be able to check by the end of the year, which is more than most political speeches manage. Over in the US, the House voted three more times today and still couldn’t pick a Speaker, so that’s six failed rounds now. Between the two, I’m learning a lot more about how other people’s governments work than I ever expected to.
