Gorbachev

2022-08-30 1 min read (199 words)

Tuesday behaving like a Monday, which is the going rate for a long weekend and I’d pay it again. Bins went out a day late. I watched from the window to check everyone else had got the memo, and they had, which was a small relief.

Gorbachev died today. Ninety one.

I have no memory of him at all. He isn’t history I lived through, he’s history that arrived in documentaries and other people’s anecdotes and that particular way older relatives say “when the Wall came down” as though you’d been standing next to them at the time.

What I keep snagging on is the timing of it. He’s the man who took the Cold War apart, and he’s gone and died six months into the whole thing being put back together, in a war he lived long enough to watch start. I don’t know what you’re meant to do with that. It doesn’t resolve into anything.

Apparently the obituaries here and the obituaries there describe two entirely different men, which I gather has been the case for about thirty years.

Posties out tomorrow.

Odd thing, being a bit sad about somebody you only ever knew as a fact.
