A Billion-Euro Lesson

2023-05-22cozyHousecat

Meta, which owns Facebook, has been fined a record €1.2 billion by EU regulators for sending European users’ personal data to servers in the US, where it could be accessed by American intelligence agencies. It’s the biggest fine ever under the EU’s data protection rules, and Meta has been ordered to stop the transfers, though it says it will appeal and that the decision could affect thousands of other businesses. The case shows how much of our personal lives sits on servers we’ll never see, in countries with very different privacy laws. As someone who cares a lot about where my data lives and who can access it, this is exactly why I’ve been moving more of my stuff onto services I control. It’s not realistic for everyone, but every decision like this helps push the big tech companies to take privacy more seriously.
