Black Friday, Blue Week

2022-11-25cozyHousecat

The Royal College of Nursing announced today that nurses in England, Wales and Northern Ireland will strike on 15 and 20 December, the first time in the union’s 106-year history. When nurses feel they have no choice but to walk out, that tells you how bad things have got. It’s also Black Friday with Royal Mail on strike again, so there’s plenty of shopping but not many deliveries. The football didn’t cheer me up much either: Wales lost 2–0 to Iran after Wayne Hennessey got the first red card of the tournament and Iran scored twice deep into injury time, and England could only draw 0–0 with the USA, so we’ve still never beaten them at a World Cup. Qatar also became the first team knocked out, as hosts, which is quite something. I’m ending the week tired, a bit flat, and thinking about all the nurses who’ll be on a picket line two weeks before Christmas.
