Beijing Talks

2023-06-18cozyHousecat

US Secretary of State Antony Blinken arrived in Beijing for talks today, the first visit by a top US diplomat in five years, after his original trip in February was cancelled because of that Chinese balloon. Relations between the two countries have been very tense over Taiwan, technology and China’s relationship with Russia, so the hope is that just talking face to face might stop things getting worse. Neither side is expecting big breakthroughs, but keeping communication open between two nuclear-armed superpowers seems pretty important to me. It’s funny to think back to February, when a balloon floating across America felt like a huge international incident. Hopefully this week is a small step towards calmer relations.
