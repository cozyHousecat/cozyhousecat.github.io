Tragedy in Brussels

2023-10-16cozyHousecat

A really horrible end to the day: two Swedish football fans have been shot dead in Brussels ahead of tonight’s Euro 2024 qualifier between Belgium and Sweden, and another person has been injured. The match was abandoned at half-time once the news reached the stadium, and the fans inside were kept there for their own safety while police searched for the gunman, who is still at large tonight. Belgium’s prime minister has said it appears to be a terrorist attack, and the threat level in Brussels has been raised to its highest level, so the city must be incredibly tense right now. Football is supposed to be one of those things that brings people from different countries together, so the idea of supporters going to watch their team and never coming home is just heartbreaking. My thoughts are with the families of the two people who died, and with all the Swedish fans who are a long way from home tonight. 💙💛
