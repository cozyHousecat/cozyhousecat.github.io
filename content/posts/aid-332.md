Fortnite Vibin ends on sunday and I have to say this is the most fun I've had in a video game for a very long time, I don't want this season to end.

I haven't been playing Fortnite long but his season changed the look & feel of the game from something which was largely just PvP into a carefree, colorful, and playful vibe that made the whole island feel like one big celebration.

At its core, Vibin’ is fun because it ditches tension for summer recreation and relaxation. Instead of a grim survival mood, the island becomes a playground packed with festivals, beach energy, weirdly wonderful transformations, and lots of lighthearted chaos. Fortnite has always been good at changing the map, but this season made those changes feel especially exciting because every update added something new to discover.

The map was just stunning, my favourite area is the Reality Biome which I hope manages to survive into the next season. The giant colourful mushrooms, glowing roots, and the lobby music _Long Way Home_ creates such a wonderful atmosphere.

Well done Epic, so much fun. Please let the DMR survive to the next season!
