Now I Am Become Death

2023-08-08cozyHousecat

Tonight we finally went to see Oppenheimer, Christopher Nolan’s three-hour film about the physicist who led the project to build the atomic bomb, and I’m still a bit stunned. Cillian Murphy is incredible in it, showing a brilliant, complicated man who slowly realises what he’s helped unleash on the world, and the Trinity test scene is one of the most intense things I’ve ever experienced in a cinema, with that long silence before the sound hits. It’s a demanding film, full of dialogue and hearings, but it never felt three hours long. Watching it just two days after the anniversary of Hiroshima, in a year when nuclear threats have been in the news so often, made it feel very relevant and very sobering. We’ll need to watch Barbie soon to complete the Barbenheimer double bill and balance out the existential dread.
