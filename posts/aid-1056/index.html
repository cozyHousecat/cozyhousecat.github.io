Raab Resigns

2023-04-21cozyHousecat

Dominic Raab resigned this morning as Deputy Prime Minister and Justice Secretary, after an independent report into complaints from civil servants found he’d acted in an intimidating way and been persistently aggressive in some meetings. In his resignation letter he said he’d promised to resign if any bullying was found, but he strongly criticised the inquiry, warning that it set the bar for bullying too low and could stop ministers from pushing for change. Some civil servants and unions welcomed the outcome, saying nobody should be treated that way at work, while some of his allies agree with his worries about the process. Sunak accepted his resignation, though he’s been criticised for taking so long to act once the report landed. It’s the third senior minister to go under Sunak in six months, which isn’t a great record for a government that promised integrity.
