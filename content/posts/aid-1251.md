Tonight we all went out for a meal at Tampopo before Matilda and her dad headed off to see Iron Maiden at the MEN Arena (I know it’s the AO Arena now, but it’ll always be the MEN to me). Plates of noodles and katsu, lots of laughing, and a dad very excited about his band was a lovely way to start a Friday evening. This tour is all about their 1986 album Somewhere in Time and their newest one, Senjutsu, so from the sound of it they’re in for a proper night of classic metal. I came home for a quiet evening on my own, with a few rounds of Rocket League and the news on in the background, where France is facing a fourth night of unrest over Nahel’s death. I’m now waiting up to hear how it was, and expecting at least one person to come home slightly deaf.
