From Rescue to Recovery

2023-02-19cozyHousecat

Nearly two weeks after the earthquakes, Turkey has ended its search and rescue operations in most of the affected provinces, and more than 45,000 people are now known to have died across Turkey and Syria. For days, the search for survivors gave everyone something to hold onto, and the rescues after more than a week underground felt like small miracles. Now the focus is on the living: millions of people sleeping in tents, cars and makeshift shelters, many of whom have lost family, homes and jobs all at once. Rebuilding will take years, and there are still big questions to answer about why so many buildings collapsed. Tonight’s glitzy BAFTAs feel a world away, and I hope the attention doesn’t fade now that the rescue stage is over.
