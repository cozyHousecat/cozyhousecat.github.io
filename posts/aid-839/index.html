One Year

2023-02-24cozyHousecat

A year ago today, I woke up to pictures of tanks crossing the border and missiles hitting Kyiv, and like lots of people, I assumed it would all be over within days. Instead, Ukraine refused to fall: Zelensky stayed in Kyiv, ordinary people joined up to defend their towns, and the country has since taken back huge areas of land. Today he called it “the year of invincibility,” and buildings across the world, including here, were lit up in blue and yellow. The cost has been terrible, with tens of thousands killed, millions forced to leave their homes, and so many children growing up in shelters and on trains, some of whom are now in schools here thanks to families who opened their homes. I hope that by this time next year, we’re marking the anniversary of a just peace rather than another year of war.
