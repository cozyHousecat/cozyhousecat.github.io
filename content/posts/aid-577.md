England beat Iran 6–2 in their opening game, with Saka scoring twice, Bellingham looking like he’s been doing this for years, and Rashford and Grealish also scoring. The moment I’ll remember most, though, came before kick-off, when Iran’s players stood silent during their own national anthem, apparently in support of the protests back home, knowing they could face real consequences when they return. That puts England dropping the armband over a yellow card into perspective. Alex Scott wore a OneLove armband pitchside for the BBC anyway, which I loved. Tonight Gareth Bale scored a late penalty to earn Wales a 1–1 draw with the USA in their first World Cup match since 1958, and the Welsh fans’ faces were the best thing on TV all day.
