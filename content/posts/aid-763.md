The first week of February has been a strange mix: half a million people on strike, interest rates up again, Shell announcing record profits, and now a mysterious balloon causing an international incident. It’s hard to know what to make of a world where energy companies are breaking records while people are skipping meals to pay their bills. Still, it’s Friday, and the Six Nations rugby starts tomorrow, so at least the weekend has something to look forward to. I’m going to make a cup of tea, put my feet up and not check the news again until morning. Happy weekend, everyone.
