You Can Run

2023-05-27cozyHousecat

Twitter has pulled out of the EU’s voluntary code of practice on tackling disinformation, which most big tech platforms have signed up to. The EU’s response was pretty blunt, with a senior official warning Twitter that new laws coming into force later this year will make tackling disinformation a legal requirement anyway, and saying it can run but it can’t hide. Supporters of Musk see it as standing up for free speech, while critics worry it’ll make it even easier for false information to spread, especially around elections and the war in Ukraine. After the chaos with blue ticks and fake accounts this year, I’m not hugely surprised, but it’s another sign of how differently Twitter is being run now. It’s a good reminder to be careful about what we believe and share online, whichever platform we’re on.
